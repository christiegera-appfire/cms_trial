Risk reports

Hedge Risk reports deliver high-level information about your risks, broken down by risk levels. Use these reports to identify risks using a risk matrix, evaluate risk levels on a pie chart, break the risks down by status category, or determine risks raised over time.

Where to find risk reports

  1. Access the Hedge app under Apps > Risk management.

  2. Click the name of a risk register to open the risk table and see a list of risks.

  3. Click Report in the top right corner.

Use filters to narrow down results in reports.

Risk matrix

The Risk matrix can help you identify the risks associated with a project and how to address them. For example, by using impact-related metrics on X-axes and probability-related metrics on Y-axes, you can differentiate your risks into the three levels of high, moderate, and low.

Risk levels

Use the pie chart to evaluate the percentage of issues falling into each risk level.

Risk statuses

Use the Risk statuses column chart to break down your risks by status category.

Risk raised over time

Determine what risks were raised and when using the time chart.

If you want to quickly access risks related to each report category, hold the pointer over the necessary section and click to view issues in Jira.
