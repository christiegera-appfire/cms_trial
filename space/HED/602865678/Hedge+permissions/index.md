# Hedge permissions

## Overview

This page explains Hedge permissions.

## All users

All users in Hedge can:

- See all Risk Registers.
- Archive a Risk Register, no matter which projects are part of it.

## Project-based permissions

Specific users in Hedge can:

- Create new Risk Registers for the projects they have permissions.
- View the issues/risks for the projects that they have permission for inside a Risk Register.
- Add/remove projects that they have view permission for in the Risk Register Settings.
