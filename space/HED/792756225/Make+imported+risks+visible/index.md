# Make imported risks visible

If you’re unable to see your imported risks in the Risk Register, you may have filters or issue limitations blocking their visibility. To fix this, clear unnecessary filters and make sure the correct issue types are selected.

## Clear your filters

If you have a different project, issue type, or any other parameters applied in the filters that don’t match your risks, you will be unable to see your risks. To fix this:

1. In the Risk Register, click **Clear filters**.
2. Reapply filters, making sure to only apply filters that match the parameters of your risks.

![Screenshot of a Risk Register showing the Clear filters button.](/cms_trial/assets/518d136e-1d99-453d-b852-cf0a940d273e.png)

## Select the correct issue types in Risk Register settings

1. In the Risk Register, click **Settings** > **General settings** > **Issue Types.**
2. Add the issue types you want displayed on the register. Make sure the issue types you want to display are selected.

![Screenshot of a Risk Register showing the Settings and the Issue Types field.](/cms_trial/assets/7ba365f0-0b93-49cf-9055-65aa55aa745e.png)
