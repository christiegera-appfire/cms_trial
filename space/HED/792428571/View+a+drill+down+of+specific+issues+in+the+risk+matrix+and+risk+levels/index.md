# View a drill down of specific issues in the risk matrix and risk levels

In the *Risk Matrix* and *Risk Levels* sections, click the part of the report you’re interested in to drill down to the actual issues. For example, you can see all High-risk issues that are still open or in progress, so you're aware of potential risks and how to move the mitigation plans forward.
