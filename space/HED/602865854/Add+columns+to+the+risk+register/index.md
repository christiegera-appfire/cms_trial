# Add columns to the risk register

Enhance the Risk register table by choosing additional columns such as status, component, fix version, assignee, project, priority, epic link, parent link, and labels. With this feature, you can better understand each risk without having to access the risk details separately in Jira.

## Add columns to the risk register

1. In Jira, navigate to **Apps > Risk Management** to see the list of Risk Registers.
2. Choose a risk register that you want to add columns to.
3. Click the **Columns** drop-down in the top right corner of your risk register.
4. Search for the necessary Jira field you want to use for a column.
5. Click the checkbox next to the column to add it to the table.
