# Edit risk register names

## Overview

There are two ways to change the name of a Risk Register:

- Edit from the Risk Register.
- Edit from the Risk list.

## Edit from the Risk Register

1. Click the [ellipsis icon] button on the right of a Risk.
2. Click **Register Settings** in the dropdown menu.
3. Click the **General settings** tab.
4. Edit the name in the *Name* field.
5. Click **Save changes*****.***

## Edit from the Risk list

1. Click the Risk Register.
2. Click **Settings** at the top right of the Risk listings.
3. Click the **General Settings** tab.
4. Edit the name in the *Name* field.
5. Click **Save changes**.
