About Planning Poker

👋 Welcome to Planning Poker for Azure DevOps!

Top-rated Planning Poker app that facilitates agile team discussion aimed at reaching accurate and consensus-based estimations. Thanks to native Azure DevOps integration, teams can easily select game backlog, invite participants, view and edit issue details and comments during the poker session, and save their estimates for the selected fields.

Improved estimation accuracy

Planning Poker facilitates collaboration and discussion aimed at consensus-based estimations. It is a planning toolkit that supports interactive and async estimation.

Distributed but together

During the sessions, team members can voice their estimates unbiasedly and collaborate during group discussions to reach a consensus and achieve more accurate estimates.

Top notch estimation tools

The app also provides advanced features to help form a balance between the speed of the estimation process and expected accuracy, including multi-field estimation, custom estimation values, reference work items, and more.

  • Seamless integration

  • Real-time estimation

  • Async mode

  • Intuitive game setup

  • Private, unbiased voting

  • Custom settings

How does the game work?

The general idea of Planning Poker is that after a period of discussion, each participant chooses from their own deck the numbered card that represents their estimate of how much work is involved in the story under discussion.

All estimates are kept private until each participant has chosen a card. Then all estimates are revealed, and discussion can begin again.

This is all done inside the user’s Azure DevOps project.
