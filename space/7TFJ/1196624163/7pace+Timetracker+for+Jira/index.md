# 7pace Timetracker for Jira

## **Welcome to 7pace Timetracker for Jira!**

7pace Timetracker for Jira is a streamlined, intuitive time tracking app for recording, managing, and reporting time. Designed to support managers and individual contributors, it simplifies time-tracking tasks across the board. Thanks to seamless integration with Jira, project progress and time data are always interconnected and up-to-date.

Interested in learning more? [Schedule a demo with us!](https://calendly.com/vlad-puhach/7pace-for-jira?month=2024-11)
