## Add or delete columns

You can add or delete columns in the *Times Explorer* table and customize how your issues display.

Use the following steps to customize the *Times Explorer* table columns:

1. Click **Apps** > **Timetracker** > **Times Explorer**.
2. Click **Settings**.

   ![Click Settings to open the Customize columns dialog.](/cms_trial/assets/13fa5464-f5b3-4b00-9a78-a1f3b60b50d9.png)
3. The Custom columns modal opens.
4. There are two types of columns to select:

   1. **7pace columns** - Columns specific to Timetracker issues.
   2. **Jira columns** - Correspond to the baseline fields that are available in Jira projects.

      ![7pace and Jira columns can be added to the Times Explorer](/cms_trial/assets/dc98ea69-7926-41be-b6f1-e6be98c0b48b.png)
5. Scroll through the columns and select or deselect them, which automatically adds or deletes them from the *Times Explorer* table.
6. Or, you can search for columns by typing the name of the column in the Search columns field and clicking the column checkbox.

   ![Enter a checkmark to display a column in the Times Explorer](/cms_trial/assets/808a2988-0aca-4984-8ac7-fb7747c9e317.png)
