# Work preferences

![work-preferences.png](/cms_trial/assets/99050b11-6c48-4b04-867e-3f328184ac7a.png)

To improve worklog suggestions, you can define your work schedule by choosing specific weekdays and working hours.

The working schedule function is inactive when the [BigPicture integration](/cms_trial/space/7TFJ/2106556524/BigPicture+integration/) is active; in that case, working days and hours sync from BigPicture.
