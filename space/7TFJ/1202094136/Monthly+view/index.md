# Monthly view

7pace Timetracker has a *Monthly* view for tracking and editing time. To access this view, click **Apps** > **Timetracker** > **Monthly**.

## Watch the video

Review the following video for details about 7pace Timetracker’s *Monthly* view.

## Monthly view enhancements in January 2025

The following video reviews Monthly view enhancements released in January 2025.

Video transcript:

7pace Timetracker's Monthly view has been enhanced, making it easier to access important information and increase overall readability. Now, from Monthly view, Jira issues displayed on the calendar are color-coded by type: Red - for bugs, Green - for stories, Blue - for tasks, and Purple - for Epics. In addition, after selecting a date on the calendar, there are two views available in the right-hand pane: Stacked and Timeframe. The Stacked view displays a list of worklogs from the current day. Click Timeframe to view worklogs on a daily calendar. The Edit, Delete, and Duplicate features are available in both Stacked and Timeframe view. In previous versions, time entries were displayed inline on the calendar. In summary, it is now easier to identify the Jira issue type associated with specific worklogs in Monthly view. Also, the Stacked and Timeframe display options are available when selecting a date in Monthly view.

## Monthly view

### Access

7pace Timetracker has a *Monthly* View for tracking and editing time. To access this view, click **Apps** > **Timetracker**. Then, in the left-hand pane, select **Monthly**.

![In the left-hand pane, click the Monthly link](/cms_trial/assets/940ab6b1-38a7-4d30-acda-612bbf3d7654.png)

### Displayed information

Here, your time displays for each day of the month, for example, 8 hours on Tuesday, November 5.

In the header of the table you can see Total - a sum up number of all the hours recorded in the month.

![A view of the Monthly page in 7pace Timetracker for Jira](/cms_trial/assets/a75da0b7-8b6f-4a25-ac74-74d2798fc421.png)

If you enable the [BigPicture integration](/cms_trial/space/7TFJ/2106556524/BigPicture+integration/), capacity appears in the header:

![monthly-view-capacity.png](/cms_trial/assets/a54f4ad7-151c-405f-8300-861d3f93c0e4.png)

You can also check weekly capacity by hovering over a specific week’s tile:

![weekly-capacity-in-monthly-view.png](/cms_trial/assets/4962a027-386a-48fa-90ea-c1c838e2e772.png)

### Edit time in Monthly view

Use the following steps to edit time:

1. Select a day.
2. The existing time entries are displayed. To edit an entry, hold the pointer over it and click the Edit button.

   ![The Edit button displays when you hold the pointer over a time entry](/cms_trial/assets/dd0515d6-3fa8-4ed6-9b2c-f176e9dc0525.png)
3. The *Edit Time* window displays where you can change the assigned ticket, the date, the duration, or the comment.

   ![The Edit Time window](/cms_trial/assets/3dd49a32-cf68-4d91-9427-2d0d20f1f30a.png)
4. To display the work log's **From** and **To** times, activate the **Timeframe** field.

   ![Turn on the Timeframe feature to edit the From and To fields](/cms_trial/assets/e6c6a252-5dfe-4cbb-8171-a983c57e41fc.png)
5. Click **Save**.

### Delete a work log in Monthly view

Use the following steps to delete a work log:

1. Hold the pointer over the appropriate row and click **Delete**.

   ![Click the Delete button after holding the mouse pointer over the appropriate row.](/cms_trial/assets/a8e8d13f-8a92-4f6c-8247-01c1bd12ce9d.png)
2. The *Delete worklog* message displays.
3. Click **Delete** to confirm that the work log should be removed.

   ![Click the Delete button to remove the work log.](/cms_trial/assets/496ea434-d6e7-4cfa-8673-46e829365a83.png)

### Add time in Monthly view - Current date

Use the following steps to add time for the current date in *Monthly* view:

1. Hold the pointer over the appropriate day, and click the **Add Time** button.

   ![Click the Add Time button](/cms_trial/assets/8d091be8-be36-4f8e-bba5-c77a6073bf41.png)
2. The *Add Time* window displays.
3. Select an issue, confirm the date, and then specify either **From** and **To** times or select a duration.
4. Add a comment and then click **Save**.

   ![Use the Add Time window to select an issue, a data, and duration.](/cms_trial/assets/6c4b22cf-0afe-4ff6-81ea-e006c9332d7e.png)

### Add time in Monthly view - Non-current date

Use the following steps to add time for a non-current day:

1. Hold the pointer over the day and click the **+ Add Time** button.

   ![The Add Time button](/cms_trial/assets/a125f5a4-f736-45ee-a1de-fd8869a399fb.png)
2. The *Add Time* window displays, where you can complete the appropriate fields.

   ![monthly-view-add-time.png](/cms_trial/assets/93d1a9a9-4ebf-416a-8857-8fcbbfc38691.png)

   In the left panel you can check smart suggestions for Jira issues to use, based on you previous time logs. For more information, check <https://appfire.atlassian.net/wiki/x/VYE7rg> .  
   To add time that isn't associated with a ticket, for example, a one-on-one meeting with your manager, enter a duration and a comment.

1. Click **Save**.

### Change months in Monthly view

While the Monthly view defaults to the current month, you can scroll months using the arrow keys in the upper right-hand corner.

![Scroll months using the arrows in the upper right-hand corner.](/cms_trial/assets/e5df1a67-547f-46a5-94d6-7b3bd1cbb892.png)
