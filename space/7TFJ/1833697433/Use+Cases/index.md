Your time and your team’s time are the most valuable resources. **7pace Timetracker for Jira** provides extensive tools for tracking time spent - all without wasting precious time trying to capture actionable data!

Imagine your team and their varied methods of tracking their time - calendar blocks for those who plan ahead, immediate entries for those who try to stay in the moment, end-of-week estimations for the forgetful, or even paper capture for the traditionalists. 7pace enables all of these to enter time quickly and efficiently while providing project leaders detailed data for enhanced planning.
