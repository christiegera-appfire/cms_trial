## Enhancements

## Calendar integration

We’re excited to launch the ability to [integrate a Google calendar in 7pace Timetracker](/cms_trial/space/7TFJ/2187624468/Connect+a+calendar+in+Weekly+view/) for Jira!

Google Calendar integration syncs your calendar events with 7pace Timetracker, allowing you to view events and create worklogs directly from *Weekly* view. This feature saves you time and effort by automatically importing calendar events, making time tracking and scheduling more seamless and accurate. This seamless integration with Google Calendar enables effortless time tracking for users, reducing manual entry and errors.

Watch the video below to see it in action, or [read the full documentation](/cms_trial/space/7TFJ/2187624468/Connect+a+calendar+in+Weekly+view/) to get started.

With the 7pace Timetracker and BigPicture integration, you can leverage time tracking information by viewing track time in BigPicture through custom 7pace Timetracker fields and gain insights into team member capacity. To begin, from BigPicture, click Settings > Integrations. Select the 7pace Timetracker tab, and use the slider to enable 7pace Timetracker integration. Save the change. Next, we'll display two custom time tracking fields in BigPicture, Activity type and Billable, allowing us to learn more about how team members spend their time. Open a BigPicture project in the Gantt chart. Here, epics and work items display along with original time estimates and actual time spent. Let's add custom time tracking information from 7pace to the BigPicture Gantt chart. First, we'll add the custom field for Activity type R&D, then add Billable hours. Now we see additional information from 7pace Timetracker on the Gantt chart, illustrating how the team tracked billable research and development time in the project. 7pace Timetracker strives to provide users with a seamless, integrated tool for tracking time. There's no context switching with 7pace Timetracker. Everything you need to track time exists in Jira. To track time, click the option in 7pace Timetracker for + Add time. Select the date, enter the duration, a comment if necessary, and complete any custom fields, Billable and Activity type in this example. Save the worklog. From 7pace Timetracker's Times Explorer view, click the BigPicture and 7pace integration button, and filter for a time period to view capacity notes for each team member. Or, group the Times Explorer view by a custom field, such as Activity type, to see where team members are spending their time. To summarize, the BigPicture and 7pace Timetracker integration leverages time tracking data to assist your team in keeping projects on track.
