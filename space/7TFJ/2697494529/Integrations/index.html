The integrations feature includes built-in connections between 7pace Timetracker and other Jira apps. This section will cover each integration as it is added.

BigPicture

The integration described in this article is an Early Access feature, and the 7pace development team would love to hear your feedback. Use our Calendly link to book a meeting!

BigPicture is available in the Atlassian Marketplace and takes your Project and Product Portfolio Management to the next level. It includes a powerful timeline (Gantt) module and can be used to plan and manage all your tasks in a single workspace. BigPicture can be customized to meet your organization’s needs and track your undertakings at any granularity level. Learn more about BigPicture.

In BigPicture, you can define resource capacity based on workload plans and non-working days resulting from holiday plans and individual absences. Now, you can view BigPicture capacity in 7pace Timetracker and quickly understand if team members are below, at, or over capacity.

Review the following video on how to configure the 7pace Timetracker for Jira and BigPicture integration.

Integration overview

One of BigPicture’s most valuable features is tracking individual capacity. Now, you can integrate BigPicture’s capacity features with 7pace Timetracker for Jira and quickly monitor whether team members are below, at, or above capacity, all within 7pace Timetracker’s Times Explorer.

The capacity badges displayed in the screenshot above (Below Capacity) are only visible to users with BigPicture’s Resource Administrator and App Admin roles.

Activate the integration

When BigPicture is installed in your Jira instance, a new message displays in 7pace Timetracker, indicating that BigPicture is ready to integrate.

You can activate the BigPicture integration in the BigPicture setting. For more information go to https://appfire.atlassian.net/wiki/x/DIDsd .

Note that all BigPicture data is processed in the EU.

You can check if BigPicture integration is turned on in Settings > Integrations:

To manage the integration, select the arrow icon next to Disable integration. This will take you to the BigPicture integration settings.

Integration functionality

Now, in Times Explorer, the icons at the top of the page indicate that 7pace Timetracker displays individual capacity from BigPicture.

And you can quickly view cumulative time tracking data along with an individual’s capacity:

The capacity badges displayed in the screenshot above (On Track, Above Capacity, Below Capacity) are only visible to users with BigPicture’s Resource Administrator role.

Additionally, there is a new view available in the Times Explorer for Person capacity.

Notes about the new Person capacity view:

  1. It’s only available when an administrator activates the BigPicture integration.

  2. Once an administrator activates the BigPicture integration, users cannot update or remove the view. However, the view can be modified and saved as a new layout.
