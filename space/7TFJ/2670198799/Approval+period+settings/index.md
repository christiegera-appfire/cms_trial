# Approval period settings

![TryItButton (2).png](/cms_trial/assets/b74e0222-5077-42da-8d08-f476f768f2b0.png)

The Approval Periods settings include setup of your approval schedule as well as settings to enable or disable your approval schedule and time approvals. Additionally, you can reset all approval periods, deleting all existing locked approval periods.

**Warning**: Resetting approval periods cannot be undone!

For more information, see [Approval periods](https://appfire.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/7TFM/pages/2602074199).
