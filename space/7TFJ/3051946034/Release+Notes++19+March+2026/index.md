# Release Notes  19 March 2026

**Release date**: March 19, 2026

Our team is thrilled to announce the latest release of 7pace Timetracker for Jira.

---

## New features

### Integrations

- Approval Capacity Insights - information about BigPicture capacity in the Approvals view.
- Capacity from BigPicture is now displayed in every view (when the BigPicture integration is turned on).

### Times Explorer

- When the Approvals setting is turned on, two additional columns are available to choose from: Approval Status and Approver.

### Timesheet

- Hierarchy view for work items added.

### Settings

- New Custom Rules setting: [Custom Rules](/cms_trial/space/7TFJ/3064791114/Custom+Rules/) .

## Enhancements

### Times Explorer

- The Group by Epic option improved.
