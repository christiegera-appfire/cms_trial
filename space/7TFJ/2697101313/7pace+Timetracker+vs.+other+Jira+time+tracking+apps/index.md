# 7pace Timetracker vs. other Jira time tracking apps

**7pace Timetracker for Jira** is a comprehensive and seamlessly integrated time tracking app that empowers teams to effortlessly record, manage, and report their time directly within Jira. Designed with an intuitive interface and built for both teams and managers, it supports tracking data like billable and non-billable hours or CapEx/OpEx on issues, calendars, or timesheets—down to the minute.

The app tightly integrates with leading PPM app **BigPicture**, ensuring high-quality time data that drives better project planning, resource allocation, and billing. Team leads can streamline time management, generate insightful reports, and make data-driven decisions with ease. With support for an **unlimited number of worklogs** and full backing from **Appfire**, customers also benefit from a single EULA, unified support, robust security, and enterprise-grade compliance.
