Video transcript:

The Times Explorer feature in Timetracker allows you to quickly search for all of your worklog items. To navigate to the Times Explorer in your Jira project, click Apps and then under YOUR APPS, click Timetracker. On the next screen, in the left-side menu, click Times Explorer. To search for items in the Times Explorer, type a word (or words) in the Issue Title, or words in the in the Project name, the day or month that the issue was logged, the start or end time, the person who created the worklog, or a person referenced in the worklog comment, or a person referenced in the worklog comment, text in the worklog comments, or anything else that is searchable in the worklog fields, including logged time. You can also focus your search on a specific date range using the date drop-down. Or, you can select a specific date range by clicking the calendar view selection and choosing a start and end date. The Times Explorer makes searching for your worklog items simple and straightforward.

Search in Times Explorer

Use the following steps to search for worklog items in the Times Explorer:

  1. Click Apps > Timetracker > Times Explorer.

  2. In the Search box, enter terms from the following columns:

    1. Issue Title

    2. Project

    3. Date Logged

    4. Person

    5. Start

    6. End

    7. Logged Time

  3. You can also search for text in the worklog comments

Note that you can also focus your search on a specific date range using the date dropdown.

Or, you can select a specific date range by clicking the calendar and choosing a start and end date.
