# Release Notes 3 September 2026

**Release date**: September 3, 2026

Our team is thrilled to announce the latest release of 7pace Timetracker for Jira.

---

## Fixes

Fixed a problem with the Export function not working in Firefox.

## Improvements

### Capacity and period lock validation for worklog proposals

7pace Timetracker now checks configured time limitations during worklog proposals, suggesting a shorter duration when the requested hours exceed remaining capacity, and blocking submissions against locked accounting periods with a clear explanation.

### Duplicate and conflict detection for worklog proposals

7pace Timetracker now automatically validates proposed worklogs against existing entries before confirmation, blocking exact duplicates and severe overlaps (>75%), and warning users of partial conflicts with a reference to the conflicting entry.

### Data residency

Minor improvements to the data residency mechanism.
