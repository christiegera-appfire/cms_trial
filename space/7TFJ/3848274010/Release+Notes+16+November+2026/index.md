## Model Context Protocol (MCP) Integration

![7pace-mcp.png](/cms_trial/assets/fd94bf85-843d-4275-9d0a-c861b4ed38ca.png)

We are excited to introduce the **Model Context Protocol (MCP) integration** for 7pace Timetracker for Jira. This integration connects your favorite AI coding assistants - such as **Cursor** and **Claude Desktop** - directly to 7pace Timetracker, allowing them to intelligently propose and record time entries based on your actual development activity.

---

### Key Capabilities

#### Context-Aware Time Suggestions

Your AI assistant analyzes active Git branches, commit history, prompt activity, and coding flow to detect what you worked on and determine worklog durations (accounting for breaks and idle gaps).

#### Automatic Target Resolution

If you don't specify a Jira issue key in your prompt (for example, *"Log time for what I worked on today"*), the assistant infers the correct issue from your active branch or commits.

#### Direct Natural-Language Logging

Prompt your assistant naturally (for example, *"Log time to DEMO-6"*) without switching context away from your IDE or chat client.

#### Strict Safety and Approval Guardrails

#### **Mandatory Confirmation**

Worklogs are generated as proposals first. The assistant will never submit a worklog without your explicit review and confirmation.

#### Conflict and Rule Checks

Built-in safeguards check for overlapping entries, enforce workspace daily limits, respect locked accounting periods, and require mandatory custom fields or billing attributes.

For more information, check the [MCP](/cms_trial/space/7TFJ/3500081256/MCP/) documentation.
