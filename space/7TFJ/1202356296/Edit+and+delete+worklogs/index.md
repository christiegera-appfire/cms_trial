## Edit and delete time in 7pace Timetracker for Jira

Tracked time can be quickly edited or deleted from the *Time tracking* summary. Use the following steps to edit time tracked by a specific user.

1. Expand the user’s time box, and then hover over the *Logged Time* row.
2. Here, options for **Edit** and **Delete** display.

   ![Edit and delete logged time in 7pace Timetracker for Jira](/cms_trial/assets/1e3f3073-0fde-4da0-b962-13a8bf5ae988.png)
3. Click the **Edit** button.
4. From the *Edit Time* window, change any of the displayed fields. For example, update the **Duration**.

   ![The Edit Time window in 7pace Timetracker for Jira. Change the date, duration, or add a comment.](/cms_trial/assets/e4f0f4d0-4d1d-4374-998a-ff16ffbb64a4.png)
5. Click **Save**.
6. To delete track time, simply hover over the *Logged Time* row and click the **Delete** button.
