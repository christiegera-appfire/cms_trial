# Get to know 7pace Timetracker for Jira

7pace Timetracker for Jira helps teams record, manage, and report time directly where work happens in Jira. Whether you need simple daily time tracking, clearer team timesheets, or better reporting for budgets, billing, approvals, and delivery oversight, 7pace gives contributors and managers a shared, reliable view of time data connected to live Jira work.

## Time tracking in Jira

7pace makes it easy to log time on Jira work items without leaving your workflow. Teams can add worklogs directly in Jira, use timesheet and calendar views to manage entries, and track work in a way that feels natural for both occasional users and heavy time reporters.

## Timesheets, calendars, and daily adoption

Different teams prefer different ways of working, so 7pace supports flexible time entry experiences and view of logged time. You can use timesheets, calendar-based views, and Time Explorer, helping you stay consistent and accurate whether you plan time in advance or capture it as work happens.

## Approvals and operational control

When organizations need more structure, 7pace supports approval periods and manager review workflows to improve timekeeping quality and accountability. This helps teams standardize submissions, review logged time before it is finalized, and maintain stronger operational control across departments and reporting cycles.

## Reporting, insights, and visibility

7pace turns worklog data into useful insights for project reporting and decision-making. Teams and managers can analyze time across issues, projects, and periods to understand effort distribution, monitor progress, support status reporting, and identify bottlenecks or inefficiencies earlier.
