# Export reports in Times Explorer

![TryItButton (2).png](/cms_trial/assets/b234c878-cb0d-4b87-8835-7268a04b286c.png)

Export the *Times Explorer* to perform data analysis, calculations, backups, and offline access. You can also export a report of issues for a specific timeframe.

## Watch the video

Review the following video to learn how to export reports in the *Times Explorer*.

Video transcript:

You can export spreadsheet report files of your issues in the Times Explorer for data analysis, calculations, backups, and offline access. To export your reports in the Times Explorer, click the check boxes for specific issues you want to export. Then a menu opens at the bottom of the page that shows how many worklogs are selected and the total time of the selected worklogs. Click the Export button to export these work logs. Or you click the Select All checkbox in the Times Explorer table to select all the issues in the table, and then click the Export button to export the worklogs. The report file downloads to your device, PC, or Mac. When you open the report file, you will see the issue details that match the issues exported from the Times Explorer table. To export grouped issues to a report, set the groups you want it to export in the Times Explorer table, and then click the checkboxes next to each item or the Select All checkbox in the group categories to export all the group category items. Click the Export button to export the group work logs. When you open the report file, you will see all the grouped worklogs you selected in the Times Explorer table. You can also export a report of the issues for a specific timeframe. To select a timeframe, click the date selection drop-down menu and select a date or date range. Then, click the Select All checkbox in the Times Explorer table and click the Export button to download the file.

## Export individual worklogs

Use the following steps to export worklogs from the *Times Explorer*:

1. Click the checkbox next to individual issues.

   ![Enter a checkmark next to each worklog you want to export.](/cms_trial/assets/8d0a5140-c66f-4c97-b3c3-293ccc67b5fa.png)
2. At the bottom of the page, a worklog summary is displayed. Click Export.

   ![A summary displays at the bottom of the page. Click Export.](/cms_trial/assets/09b8e5cd-079e-49a7-bee4-1d6ed4ec6f68.png)
3. The file downloads to your device.
4. Open the file.
5. The selected worklogs are displayed in a spreadsheet.

## Export all worklogs

Use the following steps to export all worklogs:

1. Click the select all checkbox in the column title row.

   ![Click the Select All button.](/cms_trial/assets/1df2f302-eefc-4316-a26b-0cb7fa4f7e98.png)
2. At the bottom of the page, a worklog summary is displayed. Click Export.
3. The file downloads to your device.
4. Open the file.
5. The selected worklogs are displayed in a spreadsheet.

## Export grouped worklogs

Use the following steps to export grouped worklogs:

1. Select groups to export.

   ![Select which groups you want to export.](/cms_trial/assets/5730d806-67d7-4bd2-bf4e-641b043df37d.png)
2. At the bottom of the page, a worklog summary is displayed. Click Export.
3. The file downloads to your device.
4. Open the file.
5. The selected worklogs are displayed in a spreadsheet.

## Export worklogs for a timeframe

Use the following steps to export worklogs for a timeframe:

1. Use the date selection dropdown to select a start and end date.

   ![Select a data range for the report.](/cms_trial/assets/8079ad78-c032-4d97-89d6-d54ff16986e7.png)
2. Click the select all checkbox in the column title row.
3. At the bottom of the page, a worklog summary is displayed. Click Export.
4. The file downloads to your device.
5. Open the file.
6. The selected worklogs are displayed in a spreadsheet.
