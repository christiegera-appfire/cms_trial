## Enhancements

## BigPicture integration

The integration described in this article is an Early Access feature, and the 7pace development team would love to hear your feedback. [Use our Calendly link](https://calendly.com/d/cnnp-929-n53/call-with-7pace-for-jira-product-team?month=2025-04) to book a meeting!

[BigPicture is available in the Atlassian Marketplace](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/apps/1212259/bigpicture-project-management-ppm?hosting=cloud&tab=overview) and takes your Project and Product Portfolio Management to the next level. It includes a powerful timeline (Gantt) module and can be used to plan and manage all your tasks in a single workspace. BigPicture can be customized to meet your organization’s needs and track your undertakings at any granularity level. [Learn more about BigPicture.](https://appfire.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/DLP)

In BigPicture, you can define resource capacity based on workload plans and non-working days resulting from holiday plans and individual absences. Now, you can view BigPicture capacity in 7pace Timetracker and quickly understand if team members are below, at, or over capacity.

One of BigPicture’s most valuable features is tracking individual capacity. You can integrate BigPicture’s capacity features with 7pace Timetracker for Jira and quickly monitor whether team members are below, at, or above capacity, all within 7pace Timetracker’s *Times Explorer*.
