Release notes 24 January 2025

1 min read

Release date: January 24, 2025

Our team is thrilled to announce the latest release of Time to SLA for Jira Cloud.

Bug fixes

The following bugs are fixed in this release:

  • Resolved an issue where recalculating SLAs failed for issues with more than 100 comments when the SLA included a condition based on comments.

Questions and feedback

  • Explore exciting features, pricing updates, reviews, and more on the Marketplace.

  • Stuck with something? Raise a ticket with our support team.

  • Do you love using our app? Let us know what you think here.

Credits

A heartfelt thank you to our valued customers! Your incredible support and feedback inspire us to improve our apps and products continually. We appreciate your trust in Time to SLA!
