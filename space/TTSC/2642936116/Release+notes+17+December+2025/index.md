# Release notes 17 December 2025

**Release date**: December 17, 2025

Our team is thrilled to announce the latest release of Time to SLA for Jira Cloud.

---

## New features

### **Time to SLA is now on Forge**

We are excited to announce that Time to SLA for Jira Cloud has fully migrated to Atlassian’s Forge platform!

This update introduces a few important changes, including updated SLA panel behavior and a new Dynamic Calendar field. If you use automations, scripts, or integrations with Time to SLA, **you must update your configuration to prevent failures**.

Please follow the steps in our [documentation](/cms_trial/space/TTSC/2513797296/Changes+after+the+Forge+migration/) to ensure everything continues working as expected.

If you need help, our [support team](https://appfire.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portals) is here for you.
