# Release notes 6 July 2025

**Release date**: July 6, 2025

Our team is thrilled to announce the latest release of Time to SLA for Jira Cloud.

---

## Bug fixes

The following bugs are fixed in this release:

- Goals assigned with a 7x24 calendar were previously not migrated correctly. This issue has been resolved.
- The Pre-Extension Target Date tooltip was not displaying properly and was difficult to read. This has now been fixed for better clarity.
