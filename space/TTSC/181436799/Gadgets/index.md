# Gadgets

By using Time to SLA gadgets, you can elevate your Jira dashboard experience and gain deeper insights into SLA performance and team productivity.

## Best practices

To ensure optimal performance, it's important to manage the number of gadgets on your dashboard:

- Add no more than **8–10 gadgets** per dashboard.
- Consider creating multiple dashboards to avoid performance issues.

## Available gadgets

### Periodic Met vs. Exceeded SLA Chart gadget

The [Periodic Met vs. Exceeded SLA Chart gadget](https://appfire.atlassian.net/wiki/pages/createpage.action?spaceKey=ttsc&title=The%20Periodic%20Met%20vs.%20Exceeded%20SLA%20Gadget&linkCreation=true&fromPageId=181436799) displays weekly or monthly SLA numbers by status as a bar chart.

![Time to SLA Gadgets page with dashboard gadget list](/cms_trial/assets/c22e2a76-8a33-40e3-9b68-d1383cfbc44b.png)

### SLA/Assignee Performance gadget

The [SLA/Assignee Performance gadget](/cms_trial/space/TTSC/182780060/SLA%2FAssignee+Performance+gadget/) displays the number of SLAs that are met, breached, or in the critical zone based on the assignee or specific SLA.

![Time to SLA Gadgets configuration panel for SLA dashboard gadget](/cms_trial/assets/c3be0fa9-e665-4e46-911d-f1be01d2d540.png)

### SLA report gadgets

Display saved Duration, Status, Summary, and Detail reports directly on Jira dashboards. Use existing report configurations to monitor SLA performance and explore report data without opening Time to SLA.

For setup instructions and important information about dashboard permissions, see [**Display SLA reports on Jira dashboards**](/cms_trial/space/TTSC/3869049192/Display+SLA+reports+on+Jira+dashboards/).
