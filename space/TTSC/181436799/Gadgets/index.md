# Gadgets

By using Time to SLA gadgets, you can elevate your Jira dashboard experience and gain deeper insights into SLA performance and team productivity.

## Best practices

To ensure optimal performance, it's important to manage the number of gadgets on your dashboard:

- Add no more than **8–10 gadgets** per dashboard.
- Consider creating multiple dashboards to avoid performance issues.

## Available gadgets
