# Atlassian Government Cloud

JSU for Jira Cloud is available in Atlassian Government Cloud (AGC).

AGC is an Atlassian Cloud environment for US government agencies and their industry partners. Currently in the Early Access Program, AGC is available for Jira, Jira Service Management, and Confluence.

AGC currently has **FedRAMP Moderate** authorization.

To learn more about AGC or to sign up for the EAP, refer to the Atlassian documentation:

- <https://www.atlassian.com/blog/announcements/expanding-pathways-to-cloud>
- <https://www.atlassian.com/government/cloud>
- <https://support.atlassian.com/organization-administration/docs/feature-availability-for-atlassian-government-cloud/>
