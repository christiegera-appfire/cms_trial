# JSU Automation Suite for Jira Workflows - Cloud

We’ve moved our app to Atlassian Forge, Atlassian’s most secure and modern cloud platform. Forge keeps your data within Atlassian’s trusted infrastructure and meets the highest security and compliance standards (Level 3 Forge and Level 4 RoA).

This upgrade ensures you’re using an **enterprise-grade, scalable, and future-ready** solution built on Atlassian’s strongest foundation.

## Easy, no-code workflow automation for Jira with a drag-and-drop rule builder and unlimited automation rule executions

- Quickly configure workflows, cut repetitive tasks, and save your team time
- Fine-tune your processes
- No-code solution for faster onboarding
- Unlimited automation rule runs, regardless of your Jira plan
