Release date: August 21, 2025

Our team is pleased to announce the latest release of JSU Automation Suite for Jira Workflows.

Enhancements

Improved messaging

  • Update any issue field post function: We now display an info panel if you define a field value using a {{…}} expression. Curly brackets are used in Jira wiki markup to render monospace text.
    Learn more in Jira’s wiki markup documentation.

Questions and feedback

  • Explore exciting features, pricing updates, reviews, and more on the Marketplace.

  • Stuck with something? Raise a ticket with our support team.

  • Do you love using our app? Let us know what you think.

Credits

Thank you to our valued customers. You are the driving force behind why we create software, and we appreciate your trust in JSU!
