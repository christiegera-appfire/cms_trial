## Duplicate conditions and validators

If you want to add multiple conditions or validators of the same type, you can now use the duplicate feature in our rule builders for quicker rule setup. The duplicate is added under the original, and then you can update any of the settings. To create a duplicate, click the **Duplicate** icon in the configuration panel.
