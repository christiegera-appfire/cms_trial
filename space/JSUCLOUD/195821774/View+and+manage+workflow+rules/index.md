# View and manage workflow rules

Once you have created your rules, you can use JSU Automation Suite for Jira Workflows to keep track of them and visualize how and where they are being used across your team’s projects.

- [My Workflows](/cms_trial/space/JSUCLOUD/12519535/My+Workflows/): a single-page overview of your saved JSU workflow rules
- [Execution Log](/cms_trial/space/JSUCLOUD/138149902/Execution+Log/): a report that displays details for your JSU rule executions, including results, updated issues, error descriptions, and troubleshooting tips
