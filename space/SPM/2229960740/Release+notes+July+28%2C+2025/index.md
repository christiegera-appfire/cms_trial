## New homepage

### The new homepage is available

rolling out

The new homepage helps you easily navigate to your work. The Dashboard is divided into three sections:

- Quick links - first, select a module you want to see, then select a box.
- Recent - links based on your recent activity.
- My favorites - the list of boxes you have added to your favorites.

In the **Overview module** of the main/home box, click **Try the new homepage**.

## Enhancements

## OKR module

### Link boxes to OKRs

Instead of linking multiple Jira work items to a Key Result individually, you can now organize them into a single box and link the entire box to the KR. As you add or remove tasks from the box, the KR’s progress will update automatically (for auto-KRs only) without the need to manually manage links to individual tasks.

## Built-in fields

### A built-in “Sprint” field has been added

![image-20250728-132640.png](/cms_trial/assets/6994d3ac-a6b2-40a7-b981-6ce6ad6f09c5.png)

It displays the name of the Jira sprint in which the task is. If the task is not in any sprint, the field displays an empty value.

The field is editable. Selecting a sprint will assign the task to the selected sprint in Jira.

**Note**: Closed sprints are not available on the list.

![image-20250728-132756.png](/cms_trial/assets/37931535-5376-46f3-8e03-d105a696db96.png)
