App feature tour

7 min read

App overview

BigPicture is a powerful project and portfolio management tool that integrates seamlessly with Jira solutions to help teams manage projects, resources, and priorities efficiently, whether you're working on a single project or overseeing an entire portfolio. The multi-level security roles ensure no unauthorized person will access the app or your initiative.

With BigPicture, you can visualize your projects through Gantt charts, roadmaps, and customizable task structures, enabling clear planning and tracking. It supports agile, traditional, hybrid, and custom project management methods, allowing teams to adapt to their preferred workflows. Resource management features help balance workloads, while risk and issue tracking ensures potential problems are addressed early.

The main driving force behind the app’s flexibility and unprecedented control are so-called boxes that allow organizing initiatives, deliverables, risks, teams, and objectives, much like sorting photos into collections. Each box comes with its individual security roles and settings that allow you to adjust your box to perfectly fit your initiative and the teams involved.

The app also houses rich customizable reports, providing key insights into project status, performance, and resource use. BigPicture's ability to centralize project data and enhance collaboration ensures that teams stay aligned and projects progress smoothly, all while maintaining alignment with organizational goals.

BigPicture modules

BigPicture offers a suite of modules designed to streamline project and portfolio management. Each module is tailored to specific needs, such as Gantt charts for planning, roadmaps for strategic alignment, resource management for optimal workload distribution, and agile boards for flexible project execution.

You can import and export data from almost every module in different formats, such as PDF and/or CSV.

Overview

The Overview module, or command center, provides a holistic and comprehensive overview of the individual projects, programs, portfolios, and initiatives managed in your organization.

In this module, you can quickly access various boxes, modules, and their configurations. It also provides time-tracking details and summarized data for the visible boxes.

Notable features:

  • Quick access to the box hierarchy

  • Convenient access to box lifecycle management

  • Customizable filters and column views

  • Three distinctive view modes: Hierarchy, Timeline, and Kanban board

Gantt

The Gantt module is a powerful tool for visualizing and managing project timelines. It lets you create detailed Gantt charts and roadmaps, plan tasks, set dependencies, and track progress. This provides a clear overview of project schedules and helps ensure timely project delivery.

It offers flexible features like drag-and-drop scheduling, resource capacity overview, critical path tracking, and the possibility to visualize iterations/sprints directly on the Gantt chart.

Notable features:

  • Customizable task hierarchy

  • Customizable filters and column views

  • Various data aggregations on column views

  • Gantt timeline with markers, milestones, and critical path

  • Project and cross-project dependencies

  • Infobar with comprehensive tasks and box details

Scope

The Scope module helps you define and manage the boundaries of your projects, initiatives, and tasks. It provides a clear overview of the work involved, helping you break down larger objectives into manageable pieces. This ensures that your project stays focused and aligned with strategic goals.

Notable features:

  • Customizable task hierarchy

  • Customizable filters and column views

  • Various data aggregations on column views

  • Detail view for quick access to Jira work item details

Board

The Board module is a powerful tool for visualizing feature delivery dates and cross-team dependencies. It helps teams plan their tasks and coordinate work across teams.

It's especially useful during planning sessions, like Program Increment (PI) Planning, Sprint, or iteration planning, as it shows backlog and team capacities. Additionally, the Board module can enhance the way you track timebox execution.

Notable features:

  • Customizable board filters and filtering by teams

  • Project and cross-project dependencies

  • Timeline with markers

  • Backlog panel

  • Customizable card views

  • Timebox reports

Objectives

The Objectives module is a high-level board based on agile Program Increments that focuses on high-level planning, including forecasts and commitments, rather than particular tasks. You can configure it for specific durations, set objectives for the upcoming PI, and then use it to review and change the status of the objectives.

Notable features:

  • Convenient access to timebox lifecycle management

  • Objectives reports

  • Objectives heatmap mode

  • Timeline with markers

  • Team picker

Resources

The Resources module is designed to help teams efficiently manage and allocate resources across projects, programs, and portfolios. It provides a clear overview of team members, their skills, capacities, and availability to help you ensure that the right people are assigned to the right tasks.

This module allows for streamlined resource planning, tracking, and optimization, enabling project managers to avoid bottlenecks and ensure smooth project execution. With its intuitive interface, the Resources module empowers teams to make data-driven decisions for better resource utilization and project success.

Notable features:

  • Full visibility into resource allocation, workload, availability, capacity, and skills

  • Two-level grouping by individuals, projects, teams, and skills

  • Different effort modes (Original Estimate, Remaining Estimate, and story points)

  • Different effort expression units (hours, man-days, percent, story points)

  • Yearly timeline view for long-term planning

  • Customizable filters and rich visibility options

Teams

In the Teams module, you create teams and manage team memberships to streamline effort-based planning. These teams can be used across several other modules for quick and easy work assignments.

Notable features:

  • Creation of teams based on Jira teams

  • Creation of teams based on Jira groups

  • An insight into which initiatives teams are assigned to

  • Convenient team membership management

Risk Management

The Risk Management module makes it easy to register, assess, and prioritize risks, while also allowing you to track progress and link risks to daily tasks. With this module, you can assess any type of Jira work item as a risk. It’s a simple, user-friendly module with pre-made industry-standard templates for quick setup and use.

Notable features:

  • Unlimited risk registers

  • Customizable risk matrix templates

  • Customizable risk score formulas

  • Risk reports

  • Customizable risk column views

Risks

The Risks module is where you can assess the risk probability and consequence and visualize the assessed risks on the heat map or list them in the risk table.

Notable features:

  • Customizable risk heatmap

  • Customizable risk table (register)

  • Display of work items as risks and risks as work items

  • Customizable risk register column view

Reports

The Reports module helps you visualize and communicate project, program, or portfolio progress in an organized, detailed way through various reports. The reports you generate in this module pull data only from those boxes you have access to.

Notable features:

  • Customizable real-time and interactive reports

  • Various data representation formats (charts, graphs)

OKR

The OKR module helps teams set, track, and align their Objectives and Key Results (OKRs). It streamlines the process of defining goals, tracking progress, and ensuring alignment across teams, so everyone stays focused on what matters most. With the OKR module, teams can easily measure success and drive performance while keeping their projects and goals tightly integrated with the initiative.

Notable features:

  • OKR and linked issues visualization

  • Automatic and manual KR progress

  • Multi-level security roles, OKR restrictions, and advanced permission settings

  • Dedicated Jira dashboard gadgets

  • Global OKRs that can be linked to multiple boxes

Priorities

The Priorities module helps you prioritize tasks and initiatives in strategic management to allocate resources efficiently and meet project goals on time. This module houses several prioritization templates based on the popular prioritization frameworks, which you can customize or create a new one from scratch.

Notable features:

  • Customizable list view

  • Robust filters and view adjustment options

  • Priority poker

  • Popular priority templates with customizable score formulas and metrics

Financials

The Financials module is designed to help manage the financial aspects of your projects, programs, and portfolios, allowing you to track budgets, costs, and other financial metrics. This module is particularly useful for project managers and stakeholders who need to ensure that their projects are staying within budget and meeting financial goals.

Notable features:

  • Cost calculation per box (project, program, portfolio)

  • Interactive visual charts in various formats (charts, graphs)

  • Detailed cost distribution by task and initiative

  • Box and team hourly rates
