# Manage OKR updates

**About this page**

This page describes features that are supported **only** in [**BigPicture Advanced**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/3451617346/BigPicture+Standard+vs+Advanced/). These features are not available in BigPicture Standard.

Regular OKR updates keep teams aligned, help track progress, flag challenges early, and keep focus on Key Objectives, boosting accountability and performance.

However, your recent status and/or progress update may be outdated or incorrect. In that case, you can edit or delete it to keep your OKR updates current and accurate.

## Delete/Edit OKR update

The **Activity** section on the [*OKR details*](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918536390/OKR+details+page/) page and [OKR Details side panel](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918865686/OKR+Details+side+panel/) displays a chronological list of updates and comments made to the currently viewed OKR.

To delete or edit an update:

1. In the **Activity** section, click **More actions** (**…**) under the update you want to delete or modify.
2. Select:

   1. **Edit**.
   2. **Delete**.

![Contextual menu for the OKR update.](/cms_trial/assets/39aa5a26-b627-4eeb-a66a-2f25c618196c.png)

#### Edit

1. The OKR update modal displays. Update OKR status, progress, and/or description.
2. Click **Save**.

#### Delete

1. A confirmation modal displays.
2. Click **Delete** to confirm deletion of the OKR activity.

## Add OKR update

You can add a new [OKR update](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918637972/Update+OKR/) (status/progress change) directly in the **Activity** section.

1. Click **Add update** on the section header.
2. The OKR update modal displays. Update your OKR.
3. Click **Submit** to finish.

![add-new-okr-update.png](/cms_trial/assets/496b7d4e-d870-46a2-b99d-4a7445d2a0f6.png)

## Filter OKR updates

1. Click **Filter updates** on the section header.
2. Select filter type:

   1. **All updates** to see automatic (made by app and via API) and manual updates.
   2. **Manual updates** to see only manual updates.

## Comment OKR update

## Pin OKR update

Click the **Pin** icon to pin any activity entry so that it stays visible at the top of the list.
