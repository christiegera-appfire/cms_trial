# Create dependencies

## Create dependencies

You can create a dependency in the following ways:

1. Click the dot next to the taskbar and then drag it onto the target task, or drop to the dependency dot of the target task. When you hover over a dependency dot or the taskbar of the target task, both the dot and half of the taskbar pulsate.
2. Click the dot that appears next to the taskbar when you hover over the task.
3. Create dependencies directly on a Jira issue page.
4. Right-click on a task and choose **Create dependency**.
5. Select a task and click **More actions** (**…**) (make sure that the Editor Slider is enabled under the View menu). Next, choose **Create dependency**.

### Method #1

1. Click the dot next to a taskbar.
2. You can drag it directly onto the target task or onto its dependency dot.

![The video of creating a dependency by dragging and dropping.](/cms_trial/assets/0a1ea509-f264-49cb-9108-3d564006df9e.mp4)

### Method #2

1. Click the dot next to a taskbar.
2. The **Source task** field is automatically filled in.
3. Find a target task.
4. Choose a [dependency type](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918702538/Dependency+Types/) (you can hover over a camera icon to see an explanation of each dependency type).
5. Define a [lag time](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918406747/Lag+time/).
6. If needed, enable the [ASAP mode](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918764948/ASAP+mode/).
7. When ready, click **Save**.

![The video of creating a dependency by clicking the dot.](/cms_trial/assets/fa696e90-d69a-4699-ba2c-94474e3191e7.mp4)

### Method #3

Create a dependency directly in Jira.

## ASAP mode hotkey

As you create a dependency by manually dragging and positioning the arrow, when you hover over the target task, you see a message letting you know you can toggle **ASAP on/off** by **holding the Alt key**.

![The video of creating a dependency and holding the ALT key to enable or disable the ASAP mode.](/cms_trial/assets/78b0e0c4-eff5-4f37-ae9b-5565f902fc0d.mov)
