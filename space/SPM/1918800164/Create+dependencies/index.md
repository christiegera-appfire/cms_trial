# Create dependencies

## Create dependencies

You can create a dependency in the following ways:

1. Click on the dot next to the taskbar and drag&drop it on the target task, or drop to the dependency dot of the target task. When you hover over a dependency dot or a taskbar of the target task, both dot and half of a taskbar pulsate.
2. Click the dot that appears next to the taskbar when you hover over the task with the mouse.
3. Create dependencies directly on a Jira issue page.
4. Right-click on a task and choose **"Create dependency"**.
5. Mark a task and click on three dots (make sure that the "Editor Slider" is enabled under the "View" menu). Next, choose **"Create dependency"**.

### **Example 1**

1. Click on the **dot** next to a taskbar.
2. You can drag and drop it on the target task or drop it on its dependency dot.

### **Example 2**

1. Click on the **dot** next to a taskbar.
2. The **source task** field is automatically filled in.
3. Find a target task.
4. Choose a [dependency type](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918702538/Dependency+Types/) (hover over a camera icon to see an explanation of each dependency type).
5. Define a [lag time](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918406747/Lag+time/).
6. If needed, enable the [ASAP mode](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918764948/ASAP+mode/).
7. When ready, click **Save**.

## ASAP mode hotkey

As you create a dependency by manually dragging and positioning the arrow, as you hover the mouse over the target task, you see a message letting you know that you can change the **ASAP on/off** by **holding the ALT key**.

The video presents creating a dependency and holding the ALT key to enable/disable the ASAP mode.
