# General (App configuration)

Under the **General** tab, you can configure the following:

- Field mapping and additional task settings
- Dependencies
- Live synchronization (the tab is moved under the [Performance (App configuration)](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918764560/Performance+(App+configuration)/) page)

![Screenshot of the App Configuration page.](/cms_trial/assets/af5df651-3d26-4e11-84f0-20acf1c87a77.png)

## Access and security

Only Jira administrators can access this page.

1. Click the **wrench** icon at the top right and select**General** from the drop-down list.

   ![Screenshot of the wrench icon with General tab selected.](/cms_trial/assets/0ea670a7-80e4-46d6-bcd8-262d02d2ba57.png)
