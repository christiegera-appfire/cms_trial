# Add weight (contribution) to OKR

**About this page**

This page describes features that are supported **only** in [**BigPicture Advanced**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/3451617346/BigPicture+Standard+vs+Advanced/). These features are not available in BigPicture Standard.

The weight of a child OKR is a numeric value that defines its relative importance or impact when calculating the parent OKR’s overall progress. It allows teams to prioritize high-impact goals over smaller supporting tasks. Instead of treating every child item equally, weighting lets you scale its contribution.

By default, each OKR has a weight of 1 (25%), meaning they are equally important. You can adjust the individual weights to reflect the effort, strategic value, or complexity.

You can assign weights only to sub-themes, sub-objectives, and Key Results that contribute to a parent Strategic theme or parent Objective. Top-level Strategic themes and Objectives do not contribute to any other goal; they are the primary goal themselves.

## Add a weight to an OKR

1. On the OKR creation screen, click the weight value next to **Contribution** to edit it.

![OKR creation screen. A contribution field is highlighted.](/cms_trial/assets/b3bda821-49ef-4195-8358-9d1e4b11bbe5.png)

1. The **Contribution to parent Objective** modal displays. Type in the new value or use the up/down arrows to increase or decrease the weight.
2. The app will automatically calculate the contribution.

- If you are creating a Strategic theme or Objective without an assigned higher-level parent, you will not be able to add weight to it, as top-level parent OKRs do not require weights. Once you assign a higher-level parent to it, the weight field becomes available.
- When creating the first KR for an Objective, its contribution will be 100%, regardless of the weight. However, as you add more KRs, the contribution of each KR will decrease.
- It is possible to assign 0 to the weight. A weight of 0 means the child OKR's progress does not contribute to the progress of its parent Strategic theme or Objective.

## Edit the OKR weights

You can edit OKR weights on the following pages:

### Tree view page

After you create a new OKR, you automatically land on the [*Tree view*](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918801711/Tree+view/) page.

1. On the right-hand side pane, click the **pen icon** next to **Weight**.
2. On the weights edit screen, edit the weights.
3. Click **Save** to finish the process.

![Edit okrs on the tree view page.](/cms_trial/assets/c5b68465-2e76-4d97-8651-772d854325f6.png)

### OKR details page

1. On the [*OKR details*](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918536390/OKR+details+page/) page, navigate to the **Info** section.
2. Click the **pen icon** next to **Weight**.

![Edit okrs on the okr details page.](/cms_trial/assets/5a5663dc-5df5-4c39-9267-a0efccd69d68.png)

1. Click **Save** to finish the process.
