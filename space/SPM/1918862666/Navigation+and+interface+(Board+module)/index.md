# Navigation and interface (Board module)

## Navigation and interface - Board module (old navigation)

Click to expand the guide

## Board elements

The Board module includes four elements:

- Module header
- Timeline with markers
- Board with tasks and dependencies
- [Infobar](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918633681/Infobar+(Board)/):

  - Backlog
  - Dependencies
  - Warnings

![An overview of the Board module.](/cms_trial/assets/a7131313-7312-4783-a2e3-b3f94491786b.png)

## Adjust the view

Your task cards can be presented in different ways to help you during the planning and monitoring of your work.

### Show objectives

Integrate the Objectives with the Board module and display team objectives and committed tasks in one view using the Board to see how each team contributes to achieving the objectives.

![contentId-1918862666](/cms_trial/assets/908d6f34-2de5-4594-af72-33471ef1caed.png)

### Layout

There are three ways to display tasks:

- Compact
- Regular
- Wide

Switch to compact mode if you want to fit more cards on the screen. Read more on the Layout page.

![Screenshot of the View menu in the Board module.](/cms_trial/assets/480f9785-53cf-4365-9ede-49bb2dd23e08.png)

### [Task warnings](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918636230/Task+warnings+(Board+module)/)

To see warnings, ensure the **Task warnings** option is active, as indicated by a checkmark.

Warning indications will be visible directly on task cards and in the panel on the right under **Warnings** (in the form of a list).

![View options in the board module.](/cms_trial/assets/1233344d-176c-4d84-a51a-45a64d0f7d7b.png)

### [Totals](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918666880/Aggregations/)

Totals will help you plan and monitor the execution of your timeboxes (like PIs and Sprints). You can also use past data to analyze your teams' performance in depth.

![Upper menu. The Totals option is under the View (eye icon) button.](/cms_trial/assets/49a1c56e-6cad-4780-ac67-93248f531820.png)

## Sort cards

Sort task cards by any field added to them in the task card layout. Your [card configuration](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918832263/Card+view+creator/) can have a different layout in each box.

![Sort cards option in the board module.](/cms_trial/assets/bd9666d7-2814-4471-8e2a-7eb40d4d2f30.png)

## [Search and filter tasks](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918503449/Filters+and+search/)

The board module can be searched and filtered using the search bar and pre-defined [quick filters](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918635901/Quick+filters/). You can define more quick filters on the [box configuration](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918666176/Box+configuration/) > Tasks > [Quick filters](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918830532/Manage+quick+filters/) page.

![Board module's filters.](/cms_trial/assets/06c157d6-24c8-44ee-822a-9c0304f7dda5.png)

The filter button also affects the backlog in the Infobar. In addition, the Backlog tab in the Infobar has a separate show/hide lower-level tasks button that lets you show either:

- all tasks
- show only tasks not planned on a lower level

![Board module's infobar's backlog filter.](/cms_trial/assets/70aad983-1631-42dc-80e7-6848014674b4.png)

## Adjust the view

Your task cards can be presented in different ways to help you during the planning and monitoring of your work.

### Show objectives

Integrate the Objectives with the Board module and display team objectives and committed tasks in one view using the Board to see how each team contributes to achieving the objectives.

Click **View** > **Objectives**.

![Screenshot of Objectives enabled in the Board module.](/cms_trial/assets/4554a30f-1574-42d5-823a-fd17c45eb2b9.png)

### Layout

There are three ways to display tasks:

- Compact
- Regular
- Wide

Switch to compact mode if you want to fit more cards on the screen. Click **View** > **Layout**.

![Screenshot of the compact layout in the Board module.](/cms_trial/assets/778f042f-d2bd-4ee1-8b6c-48bd12dbc8c9.png)

### [Task warnings](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918636230/Task+warnings+(Board+module)/)

To see warnings, click **View** > **Task warnings**.

Warning indications will be visible directly on task cards and in **Infobar** > **Warnings**.

![Screenshot of task warnings enabled in the Board module.](/cms_trial/assets/665d8422-e298-4b80-853e-aaa4b3d828a8.png)

### [Aggregation](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918666880/Aggregations/)

Aggregation will help you plan and monitor the execution of your timeboxes (like PIs and Sprints). You can also use past data to analyze your teams' performance in depth.

![Screenshot of the Aggregation option in the Board module.](/cms_trial/assets/1c7a0821-a015-49fc-95a6-2534bf75433c.png)

## Sort cards

Click **View** > **Sort** to sort task cards by any field added to them in the task card layout. Your [card configuration](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918832263/Card+view+creator/) can have a different layout in each box.

![Screenshot of sorting options available in the Board module.](/cms_trial/assets/aa6ac75a-45b6-4485-98cd-26e7c3f1ba06.png)

## [Search and filter tasks](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918503449/Filters+and+search/)

The Board module can be searched and filtered using the search bar and pre-defined [quick filters](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918635901/Quick+filters/). You can define more quick filters on the [box configuration](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918666176/Box+configuration/) > Tasks > [Quick filters](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918830532/Manage+quick+filters/) page.

![Screenshot of the search and filter options in the Board module.](/cms_trial/assets/830e8815-cd39-45d6-ac6a-4415c76c9a3c.png)

The filter button also affects the Backlog in the Infobar. In addition, the Backlog tab in the Infobar has a separate show/hide lower-level tasks button that lets you show either:

- All tasks
- Show only tasks not planned on a lower level

![Screenshot of the Backlog tab in the Board module with the Click to show all tasks button highlighted.](/cms_trial/assets/cfd6bbb0-fa8c-4447-bf91-95f0b04a5fde.png)
