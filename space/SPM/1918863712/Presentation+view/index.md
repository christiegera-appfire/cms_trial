# Presentation view

The presentation view (called 'Lightbox') is a perfect tool to present charts to your audience.

To open a chart in full-screen mode, hold the pointer over one of the charts and click the magnifying lens at the top-right corner.

![Lighbox view highlighted in Reports module](/cms_trial/assets/1d70ba41-3406-4fc2-b195-1706bc582598.png)

### Switching between charts

When the Lightbox mode is active, you can use the arrows to switch between different rows or charts and also switch to the next or previous chart:

![Chart highlighted in the Reports module](/cms_trial/assets/80830e81-456c-4669-b31b-417a361773d7.png)

### Chart detailed information

Hold the pointer over the bars to show the task count in each category:
