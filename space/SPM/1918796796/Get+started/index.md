# Get started

## BigPicture PPM and Strategic Portfolio Management

BigPicture brings Project Portfolio Management (PPM) and Strategic Portfolio Management (SPM) together in Jira, helping organizations align goals, portfolios, resources, finances, and delivery in one place.

Use this documentation to learn about BigPicture features, configure the app, and discover best practices for every stage of your portfolio management journey.

## BigPicture for Jira Cloud is available in two plans: Standard and Advanced

Each plan offers a different set of features and capabilities, so you can choose the option that best fits your organization's needs, team size, and level of maturity.

- The **Standard** plan includes the core Project Portfolio Management (PPM) features you need for day-to-day work, such as portfolio planning, timeline scheduling, resource and capacity management, dependency tracking, risk management, and cross-team coordination.
- The **Advanced** plan includes everything in the Standard plan, plus additional features for Strategic Portfolio Management (SPM). It helps you connect strategy with execution through strategic objective management, initiative prioritization, financial management, and executive portfolio visibility.

## Connect strategy, planning, and execution in Jira

BigPicture Advanced is a powerful solution used as a strategic platform to bridge the gap between long-term goals and day-to-day execution. At the enterprise level, aligning strategy with execution becomes increasingly difficult as coordination across teams, departments, and portfolios grows more complex. BigPicture Enterprise addresses these issues with a comprehensive strategic portfolio planning layer, introducing new modules for prioritization, objectives (OKR) tracking, financial oversight, and risk management. With deep Jira integration, faster time-to-value, and support for SAFe®, LeSS, Scrum, hybrid, and custom frameworks, it enables organizations to drive alignment and execution with greater clarity and control.

BigPicture Advanced is faster to implement, competitively priced, and highly flexible, enabling tailored approaches to enterprise agility.

Backed by Appfire, it offers a scalable, deeply embedded solution with unified support, security, and compliance under a single [EULA](https://appfire.com/eula).

**Please note:** BigPicture Enterprise requires an active BigPicture license (licensed separately).
