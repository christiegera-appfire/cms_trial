# Risk registers list

## Risk registers list (old navigation)

Click to expand the guide

## About the risk registers list

Once you create your first risk register, you’ll see a list where all risk registers within a given box are created.

You can perform the following activities:

- Create new risk registers
- Archive and unarchive existing risk registers
- Delete risk registers
- Filter by name and status
- Go to the settings page of a particular risk register
- Go to the risks table

![List of Risk Registers.](/cms_trial/assets/7786fc1a-6d01-4bc5-afd1-85829c8165ef.png)

## Risk registers list elements

You can have a quick overview of the **Name**, **Owner**, and **Status** of each risk register in the list. Under the **More actions (…)** menu, you can archive, unarchive, delete, or change risk register settings.

![Risk register list elements (name, owner, and status).](/cms_trial/assets/93cef356-8b8e-4a68-9fc9-30eeed440c8d.png)

## Filter by name

To filter by a risk register name, start typing in the field to see the results.

![Risk register list elements (name, owner, and status).](/cms_trial/assets/33df994f-c3f0-41a2-9239-9149effe5be2.png)![Risk register filter by name field.](/cms_trial/assets/20f0d4dd-8f13-40b6-b8c6-87dab7acc432.png)

## Filter by status

You can also filter risk registers by the **Active** or **Archived** status.

![Risk register list filtered by Active or Archived status.](/cms_trial/assets/bcf4f6e9-2571-4873-9f33-77ac2577e319.png)

## Create risk registers

See the [Create new risk register](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918406430/Create+new+risk+register/) page to learn more about creating a new risk register.

## Archive / Unarchive risk registers

To archive a risk register, click the **… More actions** menu > **Archive**.

Once you archive a risk register, the status changes to **Archived**.

![Risk register page showing the Archive option in the More actions menu.](/cms_trial/assets/8073c385-6543-4a40-80d2-d3f34c29bc9b.png)

To unarchive a risk register, click **More actions (…)** > **Unarchive**.

Once you unarchive a risk register, the status changes to **Active**.

![Risk register page showing the Unarchive option in the More actions menu.](/cms_trial/assets/c1297988-ddeb-4bcc-8fbb-774cda1d5ccd.png)

## Delete risk registers

When a risk register is deleted, all assigned risk metrics and scores are lost. This action is irreversible.

To delete a risk register:

1. Click **More actions (…)** > **Delete**.

   ![Risk register page showing the Delete option in the More actions menu.](/cms_trial/assets/1777a546-a236-4522-b26a-0aeac8a30aed.png)
2. To confirm, click **Yes delete**.

   ![Delete this risk register message.](/cms_trial/assets/b216f140-ffc8-47aa-bbb9-66161ee030ab.png)

## Risk register settings

To access the risk register settings, click **More actions (…)** > **Register settings**.

See the [Risk register settings](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918669173/Risk+register+settings/) page for more information.
