# Skills

You can create and assign skills to resources—both individuals and teams—based on the roles required for your project. Skills can be defined for various professions, such as developers, designers, construction managers, plumbers, and more.

By associating skills with your resources, you can effectively match people and teams to tasks that require specific expertise. [The](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918405181/Find+the+perfect+match/) [**Find the perfect match**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918405181/Find+the+perfect+match/) [feature](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918405181/Find+the+perfect+match/) helps streamline task assignments by suggesting the most suitable assignees based on their skills.

You can manage and review assigned skills in [the](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918535629/Resources+module/) [**Resources**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918535629/Resources+module/) [module](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918535629/Resources+module/).

Find out more about skill settings on [the](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918636059/Skill+management/) [**Skill Management**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918636059/Skill+management/) [page](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918636059/Skill+management/).

You can:

- [Assign skills to individuals](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918862960/Assign+skills+to+individuals/)
- [Assign skills to tasks](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918832104/Assign+skills+to+tasks/)
