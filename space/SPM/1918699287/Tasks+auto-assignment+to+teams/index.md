# Tasks auto-assignment to teams

You can assign tasks to teams [manually](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918506877/Manual+assignment/) and automatically.

## Automatic assignment

With the **Tasks assigned to an individual are auto-assigned to their team** option enabled, a task can be automatically assigned to a team when specific conditions are met.

Read more about the task auto-assignment on the following page: [Automatic assignment](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918408224/Automatic+assignment/).
