Move OKR

1 min read

About this page

This page describes features that are supported only in BigPicture Advanced. These features are not available in BigPicture Standard.

The OKR Overview page visualizes all the OKRs associated with a box in a tree-like hierarchical structure. You can move up and down the individual OKRs.

To move an OKR, drag it and drop it in its new location.

Limitations

You cannot:

  • move a parent Objective if there are no other Objectives in the structure (Key Results cannot exist without an Objective).

  • move an OKR if any of the columns in the column layout are sorted (sorting affects the hierarchy order).

  • move the top OKR up and the bottom OKR down.

  • nest a parent Objective under another parent Objective (top-level Objectives cannot become sub-objectives to other top-level Objectives).

  • move an Objective without its sub-objective (a lower-level Objective cannot become a top-level Objective).

  • swap an Objective with its sub-objective (a lower-level Objective cannot become a top-level Objective).

  • swap a Key Result with its Objective unless it will fall under a sub-objective (parent Objectives are always the top-level items).
