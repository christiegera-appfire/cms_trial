# Move OKR

**About this page**

This page describes features that are supported **only** in [**BigPicture Advanced**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/3451617346/BigPicture+Standard+vs+Advanced/). These features are not available in BigPicture Standard.

You can move individual OKRs up and down on the [*OKR Overview*](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918834317/OKR+Overview/) page. To move an OKR, drag it and drop it in its new location.

![A partial screenshot of the OKR hierarchy.](/cms_trial/assets/47a64ad7-6615-4632-9ded-08d9a6e28106.png)

## Limitations

You cannot:

- Move a parent OKR if there are no other Strategic themes/Objectives in the structure (Key Results cannot exist without a parent).
- Move an OKR if any of the columns in the [column layout](https://appfire.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/SPM/pages/1918835243) are sorted (sorting affects the hierarchy order).
- Move the top OKR up and the bottom OKR down.
- Nest a parent OKR under another parent OKR (top-level Strategic themes/Objectives cannot become sub-objectives to other top-level Strategic themes/Objectives).
- Move a Strategic theme without its sub-themes (a lower-level Strategic theme cannot become a top-level Strategic theme).
- Move an Objective without its sub-objective (a lower-level Objective cannot become a top-level Objective).
- Swap a Strategic theme/Objective with its sub-item (a lower-level Strategic theme/Objective cannot become a top-level Strategic theme/Objective).
- Swap a Key Result with its parent OKR unless it will fall under a sub-theme or sub-objective.
