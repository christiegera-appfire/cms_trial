# Manage portfolio and initiative costs

**About this page**

This page describes features that are supported **only** in [**BigPicture Advanced**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/3451617346/BigPicture+Standard+vs+Advanced/). These features are not available in BigPicture Standard.

With your budget and cost-tracking foundation in place, this section details the practical workflows for actively managing your project and portfolio finances. It moves beyond initial setup to focus on the day-to-day and ongoing tasks of financial oversight. Here, you'll learn how to monitor your spending in real time, forecast future costs, and analyze performance against your budget. These processes are essential for maintaining financial control and ensuring your projects stay on track.
