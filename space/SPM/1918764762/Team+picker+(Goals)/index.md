# Team picker (Goals)

## Team picker - Goals module (old navigation)

Click to expand the guide

The **Team picker** lets you focus on selected teams only. Selected teams are represented as swimlanes.

![image-20260508-102849.png](/cms_trial/assets/09d63cc3-2277-44a8-984f-9e472c1540b4.png)

## Select all / Deselect all teams

Select or deselect all teams to focus only on the high-level or general goals. If you uncheck all the teams or click **Deselect all**, only the general objective is displayed.

![teams-select-deselect.png](/cms_trial/assets/6a130800-3592-4f4f-b107-e88bfd6f1821.png)

## Team search

To quickly find the specific team, use the search box and start typing in the team name.
