## About the reports in the Goals module

Using the Goals module, you can generate reports at two levels

- Upper level – for example, Program Increment or a Project phase
- Lower level – for example, Iteration or a sub-phase

The reports summarize the goals in their statuses and visualize them as pie charts or bar charts under their respective timboxes.

![Screenshot of the Reports view in the Goals module.](/cms_trial/assets/8f5dc7c4-2a5f-403c-9a7b-4c9123d27d58.png)

To generate separate reports for each timebox:

1. [Add a goal](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918864284/Add+goals/) to a timebox.
2. Go to the **Reports** view.

## Interact with report charts

Mouse over the pie chart or the statuses next to a chart to highlight the selected status on the pie. If the report is visualized as a bar chart, mouse over the bars to see the number of goals under a specific status (represented as an individual bar).
