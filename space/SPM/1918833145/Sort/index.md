# Sort

## Sort risk cards (Risks module)

Risk cards can be sorted in ascending or descending order within each matrix.

![Screenshot of sorting data in the Risks module.](/cms_trial/assets/356640da-1a6f-4e8d-9cc1-345e6cc3d18e.png)

### Sort the risk register

To sort the risk register, click the column header. You can sort in ascending or descending order.
