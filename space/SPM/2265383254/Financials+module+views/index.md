# Financials module views

**About this page**

This page describes features that are supported **only** in [**BigPicture Advanced**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/3451617346/BigPicture+Standard+vs+Advanced/). These features are not available in BigPicture Standard.

The Financials module provides powerful views designed to give you a clear, visual representation of your project and portfolio finances directly on your dashboard. These views help you monitor key financial metrics, track performance against your budget, and gain valuable insights into your cost data.

**Currently available view:**

- **Actual vs. Planned:** This view provides a side-by-side comparison of your planned costs against the actual costs incurred. It helps you quickly identify where you are on track and where you might be over- or under-budget.
- **Cost Variance:** This view displays the difference between your actual and planned costs. It lets you see the magnitude of any cost overruns or savings, helping you understand the financial health of your projects at a glance.
