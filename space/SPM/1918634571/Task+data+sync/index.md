# Task data sync

In this section, you can find information related to the data sync between BigPicture and external platforms (like Jira):

## Built-in fields

Built-in fields—one of the two field types in BigPicture—can pull data from various external platforms and map it to their corresponding fields.

[**Read more**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918832240/Built-in+fields/)

---

## Mapping options for built-in fields

Built-in fields can be:

- Configurable (example: End Date)
- Non-configurable (example: Summary)
- Native to the app (there is no corresponding field in the external tool)

They can also vary depending on the external platform.

[**Read more**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1926234390/Mapping+options+for+built-in+fields/)

---

## Field mapping

Configuring field mapping ensures that data from Jira fields in your issues matches the fields in BigPicture.

[**Read more**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918700586/Field+mapping/)
