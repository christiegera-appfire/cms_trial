# OKR Notifications

**About this page**

This page describes features that are supported **only** in [**BigPicture Advanced**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/3451617346/BigPicture+Standard+vs+Advanced/). These features are not available in BigPicture Standard.

Updating your OKRs regularly is key to tracking progress and reaching your goals, but it can be easy to forget. That's where email reminders help.

## Permissions

Only [permitted users](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918765815/OKR+module+permissions/)[OKR module permissions](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918765815/OKR+module+permissions/)can enable/disable and customize **OKR reminder emails**:

- Jira Admin
- In-module Admin
- Users in roles with the granted **Manage notifications** permission.

To customize options in the **Comments & discussions** section, individual users do not require any permissions.

## Set up email notifications

![Screenshot of the Notification settings in the OKR module.](/cms_trial/assets/43d75c93-f51d-4c62-b4a2-06587e2c0321.png)

### OKR email reminder

Note that the time zone for the email reminders is set to **UTC** (Coordinated Universal Time).

1. On the **Settings** > **Notifications** page, switch on the **OKR update reminder** option.
2. Select recipients, email reminder frequency, day, and time.
3. Optional: Send yourself a test email to see what the OKR email reminder looks like.

![An example of the OKR email reminder.](/cms_trial/assets/a2b7d12b-4cb9-4b8d-83d0-236983e36599.png)

The email contains the following information about the OKRs:

- OKR Key and full name
- OKR progress
- Date of the last update

### Comments & discussions (per-user)

Every user can customize email updates on comments, even if they do not have the required global permissions to make changes on the **Settings** pages.

![Email notification settings as seen by the non-permitted user to make global changes.](/cms_trial/assets/a0e5157c-9479-41fb-8e3e-5f656fedc787.png)

1. On the **Settings** > **Notifications** page, switch on the **Comments & discussion** option.
2. Check the options on the list to customize your notifications.
