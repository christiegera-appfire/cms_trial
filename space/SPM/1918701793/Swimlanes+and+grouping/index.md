# Swimlanes and grouping

## Swimlanes and grouping (old navigation)

## Available swimlanes

![image-20240909-074940.png](/cms_trial/assets/1b8e7f56-0c25-4418-a9a7-4181587c6009.png)

Organize data into the following swimlanes:

| **Swimlane** | **Result** |
| --- | --- |
| **Individuals** | People assigned to tasks (assignee) |
| **Teams** | BigPicture teams (go to the teams module to review the teams). |
| **Projects** new | Sub-boxes (for a project box that would be lower-level boxes, such as Program Increments, Iterations, Stages, Sprints etc.; for a portfolio box those would be project boxes in a portfolio) |
| **Skills** New | BigPicture skills assigned to tasks |

## Grouping

The new grouping mechanism allows for a **two-level grouping** of tasks. Grouping provides you with better insight into your portfolio. Now, you can easily track things like individual allocation and workload across projects and teams.

![contentId-1918701793](/cms_trial/assets/dfef5781-419d-416e-8e1c-064d2ee1a027.png)

Adjust the view to ensure you get the most out of your data.

Thanks to grouping, you can get **high-level insights into your projects on a portfolio level** (see a few examples):

- Based on assigned tasks, what is the workload assigned to each person in a project.

![image-20240312-092142.png](/cms_trial/assets/642f240d-a397-435d-a8f5-c64b62550c5c.png)

- What percentage of individuals' capacity is allocated to a project.

![image-20240320-054200.png](/cms_trial/assets/0c446003-33c6-4a2f-889e-278b751671df.png)

- Workload associated with timeboxes on a project level (insight into the person’s aggregate workload in each timebox)

![image-20240311-150428.png](/cms_trial/assets/0b0dcd12-f5cc-4979-b8c0-9adb312833b4.png)

## Manage grouping

### Activate grouping

Adjust the view to ensure you get the most out of your data.

First, select the main swimlane. Then, select how to group the data further into additional swimlanes.

![Group resourece module swimlanes.mp4](/cms_trial/assets/4ed260f5-88b3-4e06-9ec4-5e1200e2d942.mp4)

**Note:** Grouping is unavailable when the box you are in doesn’t have sub-boxes.

### Grouping combinations

The new mechanism currently allows for the following grouping combinations.

1. **Individuals** by:

   1. **projects** (sub-boxes)
   2. **teams**
2. **Teams** by:

   1. **projects** (sub-boxes)
   2. **individuals**
3. **projects** (sub-boxes) by:

   1. **individuals**
   2. **teams**

### Clear grouping

Click the orange dot to clear the grouping.

![image-20240819-103711.png](/cms_trial/assets/2b504675-3095-4e25-9534-ee0556e93559.png)
