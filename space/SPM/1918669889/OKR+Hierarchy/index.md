# OKR Hierarchy

**About this page**

This page describes features that are supported **only** in [**BigPicture Advanced**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/3451617346/BigPicture+Standard+vs+Advanced/). These features are not available in BigPicture Standard.

The *Hierarchy* page visualizes a family tree for your company's goals. It shows how big, ambitious goals (like the company vision) are broken down into smaller, actionable steps for different teams. The hierarchical view helps everyone understand how their work fits into the bigger picture.

## Current view

Your current view depends on the active view. Open a dropdown to switch to the default or another custom view. Views are shared across the *Overview*, *Dependencies*, *Hierarchy*, and *Progress Dashboard* pages.

You can expand the tree to view everything planned for that period. Use the buttons on the screen or your mouse/trackpad to zoom in and out.

![Hierarchy page.](/cms_trial/assets/26c6b009-9885-4f03-99b7-a2926377f05d.png)

To see the contributing items of the top OKRs, click **Show** on the OKR card. The number of expandable elements will be shown. To collapse the structure, click **Hide**.

![OKR cards.](/cms_trial/assets/b8869fd1-fe7f-4e54-ba7b-2c6db343bfe4.png)

## Filter view

You can [filter visible records](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918637654/OKR+Filters%2C+search%2C+and+saved+views/) by **Owner**, **Team**, **Label**, **OKR Type**, **Progress**, **Collaborators**, and/or **Status**.

![OKR filters expanded.](/cms_trial/assets/9daa01d3-860b-442c-af4f-f7794ff30c3d.png)

## **Export/Share the hierarchical view**

Currently, the easiest way to export the view is by taking screenshots of the selected section. You can also [share the current view](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918408436/Share+view+(OKR+module)/) via the shareable link.
