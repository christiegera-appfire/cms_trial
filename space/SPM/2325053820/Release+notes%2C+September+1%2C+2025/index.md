## New

## Strategic Areas

rolling out

Strategic Areas (SA) are a key component of strategic portfolio management. They act as a centralized hub that connects strategy and delivery. Strategic Areas provide unified single-views that display rich data related to the company’s strategic goals, where you can:

- See your short and long-term goals at a glance.
- Track how the initiatives that support those goals are progressing.
- Track how investments align with those goals.

Strategic Areas provide a concise, high-level summary of the most critical data a leadership team in your organization might need. They aggregate relevant information and data across the app and present it on one dashboard.

See an overview video below. Learn more on the [Strategic Areas](/cms_trial/space/SPM/2325217590/Strategic+Areas/) page.

## Enhancements

## Gantt module’s timeline

### Expand/Collapse sub-tasks on the Gantt timeline

You can expand and collapse sub-tasks on the task timeline.

![Screenshot of the Expand and collapse levels option in the task timeline in the Gantt module.](/cms_trial/assets/955bd1ee-5514-4be2-9c3a-a41924c26070.png)

### Right-click tasks without dates on the Gantt timeline

Right-click on the Gantt timeline to open the task details window for a task without dates.

- Click **Edit** to open the task details window (available for basic tasks and Jira work items)
- Click **Open Jira window** to open the Jira window for a given work item.

See the video below.

![2025-09-01_13-35-40.mp4](/cms_trial/assets/58e30fd6-cf4e-4ae1-8dd8-7bb85a04c4ab.mp4)

## Built-in Priority field

An editable built-in **Priority** field has been added. You can now set priorities on basic tasks, giving you more control and clarity when managing work.

![priority-built-in-field.png](/cms_trial/assets/14fd13a9-aceb-4ec4-82bb-31125059b79a.png)

## OKRs visibility in the Gantt module

The **Objectives** and **Key Results** columns were redesigned. For work items linked to multiple objectives or key results, click the counter to see the full list.

![image-20250901-115317.png](/cms_trial/assets/baa70bcc-cb10-4aa3-a1f0-929cccca5f29.png)
