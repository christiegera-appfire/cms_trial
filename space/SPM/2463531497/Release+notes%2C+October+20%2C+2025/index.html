Release notes, October 20, 2025

1 min read

Release date: October 20, 2025

Our team is thrilled to announce the latest release of BigPicture Enterprise Cloud.

New

Clone tasks

Cloning tasks now supports cloning Jira epics. To learn more about supported fields, see the https://appfire.atlassian.net/wiki/pages/createpage.action?spaceKey=spm&title=Clone%20tasks&linkCreation=true&fromPageId=2463531497 page.

Manage milestones (Financials module)

Navigate directly to the Budget milestones page from the chart in the Financials module. To learn more, see the Actual vs Planned page.

Enhancements

Resources panel in Gantt

The redesigned Resources panel in the Gantt module provides enhanced customization and allows you to switch between the individual’s and team’s workload views. To learn more, see the Resources panel in Gantt module page.

View the team’s workload

You can now view the team’s workload, which can be displayed in hours, man-days, percent, or Story Points.

More aggregation options

New aggregation options available in the Resources panel:

  • Quarterly

  • Yearly

  • By timeboxes

Capacity calculation

Improved individual capacity calculation when grouping by Individuals then Projects in the Resources module. To learn more about calculating individual capacity in different grouping combinations, see the Capacity calculation page.

Questions and feedback

  • Stuck with something? Raise a ticket with our support team.

  • Do you love using our app? Let us know what you think here.

Credits

A heartfelt thank you to our valued customers! Your incredible support and feedback inspire us to continually improve our apps and products. You are the driving force behind why we create software. We appreciate your trust in BigPicture Enterprise Cloud!
