# End of support for BigPicture Power-Up for Trello

**Support for BigPicture Power-Up for Trello will officially end on September 1, 2025. After this date, BigPicture Power-Up will no longer be available or maintained.**

If you’re using Trello together with Jira, you can continue managing your Trello Boards (including Cards, Lists, Check Items, and Checklists) inside BigPicture boxes. Learn more on the [Connect Trello](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918536760/Connect+Trello/) page.
