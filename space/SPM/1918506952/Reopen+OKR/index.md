# Reopen OKR

**About this page**

This page describes features that are supported **only** in [**BigPicture Advanced**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/3451617346/BigPicture+Standard+vs+Advanced/). These features are not available in BigPicture Standard.

Reopening closed OKRs means revisiting and reactivating an OKR previously marked as **Closed**. There are various reasons why a team or individual might choose to reopen an OKR, such as:

- If an Objective was closed but some key results were not fully achieved**,** or more time is needed to finish the work, reopening the OKR gives the opportunity to continue making progress.
- Sometimes, business needs or goals shift, and a previously closed OKR can need to be reopened to reflect new priorities or requirements.
- After closing an OKR, if you realize certain key results were not measured properly or there were discrepancies in the evaluation, reopening the OKR allows reassessment and correction.
- If new information, insights, or feedback emerge after the OKR has been closed, reopening the OKR could help apply those learnings more effectively to achieving the Objective.
- Reopening closed OKRs can also be part of a continuous improvement process, where an Objective is revisited to ensure that its results are sustained and any further improvements can be tracked.

## Reopen OKR

You can reopen Objectives, sub-objectives, and Key Results only on their respective details pages.

1. On the [*OKR details*](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918536390/OKR+details+page/) page, open the dropdown under **More actions** (**…**).
2. Select **Reopen**.

   ![reopen-okr.png](/cms_trial/assets/c8092dcb-3bf5-4bd2-8567-d44d9db535be.png)

1. The OKR is now open again.
