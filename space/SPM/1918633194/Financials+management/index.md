# Financials management

**About this page**

This page describes features that are supported **only** in [**BigPicture Advanced**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/3451617346/BigPicture+Standard+vs+Advanced/). These features are not available in BigPicture Standard.

## About the Financials module

In most organizations, managing a portfolio of projects is not just about tracking tasks and deadlines. It is also about monitoring the financials. Instead of using another tool for finance tracking, you can now use a dedicated BigPicture function: The Financials module.

The Financials module lets you track real-time actual costs (cost of past tasks) and estimated costs (cost of future tasks). The costs are calculated based on hourly rates assigned to a box or a team and estimated time and effort for tasks. You can also verify if those costs align with the planned budget.

The cost calculations can be done for a single initiative or a portfolio of initiatives, giving you real-time insight into the financial side of the portfolio.

The **key features** of the Financials module include:

- Real-time tracking of actual costs against planned costs
- Automated cost calculation based on time logged in Jira and configurable hourly rates
- Hierarchical cost roll-up from tasks to projects, programs, and portfolios
- Budget baselining to track planned spending and cost variance
- Forecasting of future costs based on remaining time estimates
