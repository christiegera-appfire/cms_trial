# Week numbers (Gantt chart)

The timeline displays days, months, quarters, and years, but you can also enable weeks if you prefer to plan using weeks.

![Wek numbers on the Gantt timeline.](/cms_trial/assets/ff1018cd-a9a9-4df1-b8dd-2570a6fd1def.png)

## Display week numbers

To show the weeks, click the **Show on timeline** (triple dots button) and select Week numbers from the dropdown menu.

- Week numbers are not shown at every zoom level. If you cannot see the week (W) numbers on your timeline, zoom in until they appear.
- Visit the **Region** >[**Week numbering**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918833426/Week+numbering/) tab to customize the week numbering settings to match the week numbering system used in your organization (requires Jira/App Admin security role).
