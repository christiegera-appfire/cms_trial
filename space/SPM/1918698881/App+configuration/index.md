## Access App configuration

Only a Jira administrator can access this page.

There are four ways to access App configuration:

### Method 1 - "." (dot) context

If BigPicture is a Jira add-on, you can use the Jira "." shortcut to access the BigPicture configuration.

1. Click the "." (dot) key shortcut.
2. Start typing “Go to manage apps” and choose the related option from the suggestion list:

   ![dot-shortcut.png](/cms_trial/assets/8183fb13-93bc-4b94-940b-283640a5df8a.png)
3. Click on **BigPicture configuration**:

   ![bp-configuration.png](/cms_trial/assets/8ce280d4-a69f-4fcf-b880-910d1e3eb123.png)

### Method 2 - App settings (wrench icon)

1. Open **BigPicture**.
2. Click the **App settings** located at the top right of every box.
3. Select one of the tabs under **App configuration**.

   ![app-configuration-wrench.png](/cms_trial/assets/c5fd49ce-1588-40ad-ac4f-e7f073adfc7e.png)

### Method 3 - Left panel

1. In the left panel select **Apps** > **BigPicture** > the **More Actions** icon. Choose **App settings** from the menu:

   ![bp-app-settings.png](/cms_trial/assets/fe7ae7e8-338b-46c4-b8bb-8c767a6d2745.png)

### Method 4 - Jira settings

1. Click the **Settings** icon (the cog icon in the top-right corner).
2. Select **Marketplace apps** from the list.

   ![bp-marketplace-apps.png](/cms_trial/assets/6aa4e705-4223-4b6a-8937-84c0c1a8e6fa.png)
3. Navigate to **BigPicture configuration**.

   ![bp-configuration.png](/cms_trial/assets/8ce280d4-a69f-4fcf-b880-910d1e3eb123.png)
