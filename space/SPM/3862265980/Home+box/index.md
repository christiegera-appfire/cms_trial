## Top bar

### View settings

![choose-view.png](/cms_trial/assets/c6c9e209-f686-4733-ba23-52c72bddcd84.png)

Here you can choose a view of the root box in the Financials module, edit it, or define a new one.

A view is defined by choosing its name, period, and boxes to be displayed.

You can choose one of the following periods:

- Current quarter
- Next quarter
- Previous quarter
- Current year

You can choose boxes to be displayed from a list (only boxes directly nested under the root box are displayed) or select/deselect all of them.

![create-view.png](/cms_trial/assets/c1f6ffe7-7379-439b-8f83-b7df5ebe399a.png)

Once the view is created, you can choose it from the dropdown list.

### Calculations

Here you can check calculations for each of the box. Select a specific box name to go to its calculation settings.

![calculations-details.png](/cms_trial/assets/e4133b08-0f2a-431c-9467-4bf1f2f1f3a3.png)

### View

![view-hide-boxes.png](/cms_trial/assets/114665bc-a9a3-470f-b5a9-525e16932ac7.png)

Here you can decide whether to display in the Box costs and budgets boxes that do not have financials defined.

## Cost summary

![cost-summary.png](/cms_trial/assets/7b1bc11b-fd5a-4535-8eaf-4b9dcc380d2a.png)

### Budget

The amount of money for all projects planned for the current period.

Click **Edit** ▢next to the amount to edit it.

Hold the pointer over **Info** ▢ next to the Budget to check the date of the last edit and the editor.

### **Total Cost**

The total cost of all tasks, both already performed and planned.

### Actual cost

The cost of the effort in tasks that is already recorded at this moment.

### **Remaining cost**

The cost of planned, future effort in tasks.

### Progress bar

Under the actual and remaining costs, you can see a percentage value and a bar representing the percentage of the total cost already spent.

### Completed tasks

Finished tasks compared to the total number of tasks.

### Teams involved

A number of Teams involved in the tasks. Select **Configure** ▢ to edit team hourly rates.
