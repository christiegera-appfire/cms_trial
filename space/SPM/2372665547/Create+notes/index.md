# Create notes

## About notes

You can create notes for Jira work items, BigPicture boxes, and basic tasks. Since basic tasks don’t have the Description field, notes can serve as their primary description. Notes are useful for documenting details, capturing decisions, and sharing updates. Because they are public, they also act as a communication channel, ensuring everyone is up-to-date.

Notes support plain text only and are limited to 1,000 characters.

Notes exist **only** within BigPicture and are not synchronized with Jira.

## Display notes

You can enable and disable the visibility of notes:

1. Click **My settings**.
2. Select or deselect the **Display notes** checkbox.

![Screenshot of the Display notes option available in the My settings menu.](/cms_trial/assets/c1443298-d98d-440e-924e-60e5bc0f5960.png)

## Show/hide notes on the taskbar

You can show and hide notes on the taskbar on the Gantt module timeline:

1. Click **Taskbar** from the top menu.
2. In the **Show** section, select **Notes**.
3. Note icons are now visible on the taskbars.

This option applies only to taskbars. Hiding notes on taskbars does not affect note icons next to the Summary field.

![Screenshot of the Notes option available under the Taskbar drop-down menu in the Gantt module.](/cms_trial/assets/7eb0587d-1d08-4321-b923-f2bd2d763713.png)

## Action on notes

You can create, edit, and delete notes in multiple places. The process is the same everywhere. See the table above to check where you can create, edit, and delete notes.

If you accidentally close a note, its draft is preserved until the page is refreshed. Clicking **X** clears the draft.

### Create notes

To create a note in the Gantt module:

1. Find a task you want to create a note for.
2. Hold the pointer over the taskbar on the timeline or next to the Summary field in the task tree and click **Create a new note**.

   ![Screenshot of the note icon next to the taskbar in the Gantt module.](/cms_trial/assets/bb8cdbb6-613a-41c0-bb49-cd04b9c4b678.png)
3. Provide your note in the field.
4. When ready, click **Save**.

   ![Screenshot of a note added to a task in the Gantt module.](/cms_trial/assets/1248ec4e-05d1-4cdb-af46-033213845618.png)
5. To close the window, click **X** or click outside the window.

### Edit notes

To edit a note in the Gantt module:

1. Find a task you want to edit a note for.
2. Click the note icon next to the taskbar on the timeline or next to the Summary field in the task tree.
3. Update a note as needed.
4. When ready, click **Save**.
5. To close the window, click **X** or click outside the window.

### Delete notes

To delete a note in the Gantt module:

1. Find a task you want to delete a note for.
2. Click the note icon next to the taskbar on the timeline or next to the Summary field in the task tree.
3. Click **Delete**.
4. To confirm, click **Delete**.

   ![Screenshot of deleting a note added to a task in the Gantt module.](/cms_trial/assets/37152883-661e-4349-b208-c2470060b46d.png)

## Constraints

- Notes can’t be created for milestones.
- If you can’t see the note icon, make sure you have enabled the **Display notes** option from **My settings**.
