# Trello tasks

## Trello tasks (old navigation)

## About Trello tasks

Once you connect BigPicture with Trello, you can add Trello boards (including cards, lists, checklist items, and checklists) to the scope of your boxes and have all your work in one place.

BigPicture displays Trello cards in the form of tasks, which can be easily allocated on the Gantt chart accompanied by the task structure panel.

## Connect Trello

You can connect a Trello instance using the **+Add new integration** button in:

- **Box configuration** > **Tasks** > **Scope definition**
- **App configuration** > **Integrations** > **Connections**

See the [Connect Trello](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918536760/Connect+Trello/) page to learn more about connecting Trello.
