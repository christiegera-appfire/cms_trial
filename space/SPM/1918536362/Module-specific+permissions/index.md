# Module-specific permissions

In addition to security settings configured on the app and box levels, you can additionaly assign users and security permissions in individual modules.

## OKR module

In the OKR module, you can set basic and advanced permissions on the **Settings** > **Permissions** page. These permissions control who can view, create, edit, or delete OKRs. You can also decide who can access and manage the OKR module settings.

[**Read more**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918765815/OKR+module+permissions/)

---

## Priorities module

Permissions in the Priorities module are configured by Jira Admins on the **Jira settings** > **System** > **Security** > **Global permissions** page. The permissions control access and management of priorities, priority matrix, saved views, and priority poker.

[**Read more**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918505859/Priorities+module+permissions/)

---

## Financials module

The Financial module comes with two unique roles (App Financial Viewer and App Financial Admin) that are assigned on the app **Administration** > **Security** page.
