# Prioritization templates

**About this page**

This page describes features that are supported **only** in [**BigPicture Advanced**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/3451617346/BigPicture+Standard+vs+Advanced/). These features are not available in BigPicture Standard.

## Prioritization templates (old navigation)

Click to expand the guide

The **Priorities module** offers eight predefined prioritization templates. You can also [create your own template](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918506690/Create+new+prioritization+templates/). You can switch templates whenever needed, and all data entered in the previous template is saved.

The template you choose will be consistently applied across all tasks, boxes, and goals.

![Screenshot of the prioritization templates in the Priorities module.](/cms_trial/assets/fdbaaf96-3c99-4336-a70c-bcbd1af3d4b6.png)

## App-level and box-level templates

In the Priorities module, you can create two types of prioritization templates:

- **App-level templates** are global templates available in all boxes. Only App Admins and App Priorities Admins can create and edit them.
- **Box-level templates** are local templates available only in the box where they were created. Box Admins can create and edit box-level templates.

To learn more about the permissions, see the [Priorities module permissions](https://appfire.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/SPM/pages/1918505859) page.

![Screenshot of the App-level and Box-level templates in the Priorities module.](/cms_trial/assets/3ee786b2-21ae-4efc-af9f-c4a6cb857d89.png)

When creating a template, App Admins and App Priorities Admins can choose whether it should be an app-level or box-level template.

![Screenshot of the Save as new option in the Priorities module. ](/cms_trial/assets/be8c3b74-f74f-4b47-9836-4071a02e32da.png)

## Predefined prioritization templates

You can use one of the predefined prioritization templates to get you started quicker:

- ICE
- RICE
- Value vs Effort
- WSJF (Weighted Shortest Job First)
- Quick Wins
- Important vs Urgent (Eisenhower Matrix)
- Balanced Scorecard
- Strategic Alignment Model

The table presents a short overview of each prioritization template.

| **Template** | **Description** | **Screenshot** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ICE | It is best suited for growth experiments, marketing projects, and all product backlogs that can’t be quantified because of a lack of data.  Score formula:  `Impact * Confidence * Ease` | Screenshot of the ICE prioritization template. |
| RICE | It is commonly used in product backlogs when you have data about the number of people you want to reach.  Score formula:  `(Reach * Impact * Confidence) / Effort` | Screenshot of the RICE prioritization template. |
| WSJF (Weighted Shortest Job First) | It is commonly used in scaled Agile frameworks to quantify the financial impact of delaying a task or solution. It’s called the cost of delay.  Score formula:  `(Business value + Time criticality + Risk reduction) / Estimated size` | Screenshot of the WSJF prioritization template. |
| Value vs Effort | The template considers the value a priority delivers and the effort required to complete it. This method is recommended for the priority matrix.  Score formula:  `Value / Effort * 10` | Screenshot of the Value vs Effort prioritization template. |
| Quick Wins | It goes beyond the traditional approach of the high-impact, low-effort quadrant of the matrix. It adds a health score that looks at the feasibility of delivering an item, the team size needed to complete the job, and the type of win it is.  Score formula:  `Health assessment * (2 * (Value / Effort) + Team size + Type)` | Screenshot of the Quick Wins prioritization template. |
| Important vs Urgent (Eisenhower Matrix) | With this template, you can prioritize tasks by categorizing them based on their importance and urgency.  Score formula:  `Importance * Urgency` | Screenshot of the Eisenhower Matrix prioritization template. |
| Balanced Scorecard | The Balanced Scorecard (BSC) is a complex prioritization template that uses a framework to prioritize tasks based on their alignment with the organization's strategic goals.  Score formula:  `( (Expected Net Profit / Cost of Investment) * 100) * (40 / 100) + Customer Impact * (30 / 100) + Risk Reduction * (10 / 100) + Innovation * (20 / 100)` | Screenshot of the Balanced Scorecard prioritization template. |
| Strategic Alignment Model | The Strategic Alignment Model is a complex prioritization template that connects an organization's overall strategy with its operational execution.  Score formula:  `Strategic Alignment * (40 / 100) + Potential ROI * (30 / 100) + ( (6 - Risk Score) * (15 / 100) ) + ( (6 - Resource Required) * (15 / 100) )` | Screenshot of the Strategic Alignment Model prioritization template. |
