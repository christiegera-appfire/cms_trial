# Keyboard shortcuts (Teams module)

## Display available keyboard shortcuts

Click the **Get help** ( **i** )button in the upper-right corner menu (or press **Shift + /**) to display the keyboard shortcuts dialog box.

![Menu under the Get help button.](/cms_trial/assets/b93743d8-36a9-4410-8d04-4bed978cab78.png)

## Teams module shortcuts

The shortcuts listed in the box will only show the shortcuts applicable to the module you are viewing. The app will adjust the displayed shortcuts to your device system (Mac and Windows). Also, depending on your Windows hardware, you may need to use the **Fn** button in place of **Ctrl**.

The Teams module’s keyboard shortcuts are divided into the following categories:

![A list of keyboard shortcuts in the Teams module.](/cms_trial/assets/f62669c1-f0d5-4306-b0cd-9654293c907a.png)

### Teams

| Allocate a team | **Shift** + **T** |
| --- | --- |

### View

| Show or hide task details | **]** |
| --- | --- |

### Others

| Show/Hide menu | **Z** |
| --- | --- |
| Undo | **Ctrl** or **Cmd** + **Z** |
| Open the System-info dialog box | **Shift** + **I** |
| Play the BigPicture game | **Shift** + **Ctrl** or **Cmd** + **G** |
| Re-synchronize data | **Alt** or **Option** + **R** |
