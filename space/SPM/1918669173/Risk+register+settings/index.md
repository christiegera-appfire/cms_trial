# Risk register settings

## Risk register settings (old navigation)

Click to expand the guide

## About risk register settings

For each risk register, you can configure the following settings:

- Risk register frameworks, where you can add new risk calculations or edit existing ones.
- General settings, where you can edit the risk register attributes.

![Risk Register frameworks Settings, General settings page.](/cms_trial/assets/49fba6bb-8d83-46f3-a46c-128c4aeb4a41.png)

## Access

### From the risk registers list

1. Click the **… More actions** menu > **Register settings**.

   ![Risk Register screen showing the Register settings button.](/cms_trial/assets/8ea0af72-c208-4a22-ba96-ad751caffd43.png)

### From the risks table

1. On the selected risk register screen, click the **Settings** button in the top-right corner.

   ![Risk register screen showing the Settings button.](/cms_trial/assets/11cdc2e3-9e1c-461c-a679-3537d47433f3.png)
