# Critical path (Infobar)

## Critical path (Inforbar) (old navigation)

The [**Critical path**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918405060/Critical+path/) section displays a list of tasks on your project’s critical path. Once you establish dependencies, click **View** > **Critical Path** to create a critical path on the Gantt chart.

The Gantt timeline will highlight all tasks on the critical path and show their details in the [Infobar](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918799264/Infobar+(Gantt)/).
