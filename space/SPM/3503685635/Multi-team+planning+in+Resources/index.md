# Multi-team planning in Resources

## Multi-team planning in the Resources module

With the multi-team planning in Resources, you can analyze team capacity, detect unused capacity, and balance workloads.

See the video to learn more.

Select the **Multi-team planning** swimlane from the **View** drop-down.

![Screenshot of the Multi-team planning feature in the Resources module.](/cms_trial/assets/2b6ce214-d62b-42e8-9933-d013a74a6aa6.png)

### Update team allocations

Once you identify workload imbalances, you can adjust the plan.

1. Drag and drop a task to another team.

   ![Dragging and dropping a task to another team in the multi-team planning in Resources.](/cms_trial/assets/c4dc124b-612d-45da-b474-aeebde1bd0b2.png)
2. Update teams and estimates.

   ![Screenshot of the multi-team planning window.](/cms_trial/assets/db8eb22c-3461-4370-9bf3-2fe7fae6aee7.png)
3. To confirm, click **Save**. The task is now visible in both swimlanes.

   ![Screenshot of a task that is assigned to two teams in the Resources module.](/cms_trial/assets/7fac720d-99be-4221-a1e8-632836e195f4.png)

### Move tasks from backlog

You can use the multi-team planning feature when dragging and dropping tasks from the **Backlog** panel.

1. Open **Backlog** and find a task.
2. Drag and drop the task to one team swimlane.

   ![Dragging and dropping a task from the Backlog to the team swimlane in Resources.](/cms_trial/assets/784be467-8bda-4d6b-9048-26da0936ff2d.png)
3. Select teams and provide time estimates.

   ![Screenshot of completing the Plan task window.](/cms_trial/assets/15e27fec-011b-4e65-aae5-f32d49bfd38b.png)
4. To confirm, click **Save**.

### Multi-team planning vs. original estimates

- In the Resources module, you can use either the multi-team planning feature or the **Original estimate** field.
- If a task doesn't have a multi-team planning estimate, BigPicture automatically uses the value from the **Original estimate** field.
- As soon as you add a multi-team planning estimate, it takes priority. The original estimate stays on the task, but it's no longer used in workload calculations.
