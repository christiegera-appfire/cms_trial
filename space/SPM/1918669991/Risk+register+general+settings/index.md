## About general settings

On the **General settings** page, you can customize each risk register by editing its name and owner and selecting which issue types are included.

1. From the **Risk management** module, click the risk register name.
2. Click the **Settings** button.
3. Switch to the **General settings** tab.

   ![Screenshot of the General Settings tab in the Risk Management module.](/cms_trial/assets/254f83e1-c4d6-465a-9015-4e17e5daee04.png)

## Risk register attributes

The table presents a list of risk register attributes you can edit from the **General settings** tab.

| **Risk register attribute** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Change the risk register name. |
| **Owner** | Change the risk register owner. |
| **Issue types** | Update selected issue types. They will be visible in the risk register.  If an issue type is highlighted in red, it’s unavailable in selected projects. Screenshot of the Issue Types field in General settings of the Risk Management module. |
| **Action progress (Mitigation links)** | You can track your progress against the risk and mitigation plan. Issues linked with the selected link type are considered in the Action progress column in the risk register.  Example:  The mitigation links are set as `is related to` and `is mitigated by`. Screenshot of mitigation links set in General Settings in the Risk Management module. The **Action progress** column displays the progress of issues that are linked using the `is related to` and `is mitigated by` relation types. Screenshot of the Action progress column in the Risk Management module. |

## About general settings

On the **General settings** page, you can customize each risk register by editing its name and owner and selecting which issue types are included.

1. From the **Risk Management** module, click the risk register name.
2. Select **Configuration** from the dropdown menu.

   ![Screenshot of selecting the Configuration button from the drop-down menu in the Risk Management module.](/cms_trial/assets/d8134625-3f42-45f2-8307-7ded1366f2db.png)
3. Switch to the **General settings** tab.

   ![Screenshot of the General settings tab in the Risk Management module.](/cms_trial/assets/83570f70-2900-4b9a-babe-c204a94b8415.png)

Issues linked with the selected link type are considered in the Action progress column in the risk register.  Example:  The mitigation link is set as `is mitigated by`.
