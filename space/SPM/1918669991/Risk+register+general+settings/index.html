About general settings

On the General settings page, you can customize each risk register by editing its name and owner and selecting which issue types are included.

  1. From the Risk management module, click the risk register name.

  2. Click the Settings button.

  3. Switch to the General settings tab.

Risk register attributes

The table presents a list of risk register attributes you can edit from the General settings tab.

Risk register attribute

Description

Name

Change the risk register name.

Owner

Change the risk register owner.

Issue types

Update selected issue types. They will be visible in the risk register.

If an issue type is highlighted in red, it’s unavailable in selected projects.

Action progress (Mitigation links)

You can track your progress against the risk and mitigation plan. Issues linked with the selected link type are considered in the Action progress column in the risk register.

Example:

The mitigation links are set as is related to and is mitigated by.

The Action progress column displays the progress of issues that are linked using the is related to and is mitigated by relation types.
