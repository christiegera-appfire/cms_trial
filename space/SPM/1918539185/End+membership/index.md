# End membership

Deleting a team member from a team will result in them being completely removed.

**WORKAROUND:** Add a new membership period with 0% availability to end a membership period without losing data connected to their past assignment to a team.

![Screenshot of closing membership for a team member in the Teams module.](/cms_trial/assets/e610c3e7-c816-4520-9b97-f845e1c9e6e0.png)

The end date of the previous membership is automatically filled in, and the new membership is marked as Unavailable.

![Screenshot of closing membership for a team member in the Teams module.](/cms_trial/assets/59500e35-2203-4c38-8e6e-60f5ca2b9a02.png)
