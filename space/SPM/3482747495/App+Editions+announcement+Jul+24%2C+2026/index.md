## BigPicture for Jira Cloud is available in two plans: Standard and Advanced.

Each plan offers a different set of features and capabilities, so you can choose the option that best fits your organization's needs, team size, and level of maturity.

- The **Standard** plan includes the core Project Portfolio Management (PPM) features you need for day-to-day work, such as portfolio planning, timeline scheduling, resource and capacity management, dependency tracking, risk management, and cross-team coordination.
- The **Advanced** plan includes everything in the Standard plan, plus additional features for Strategic Portfolio Management (SPM). It helps you connect strategy with execution through strategic objective management, initiative prioritization, financial management, and executive portfolio visibility.

If you're an **existing BigPicture customer**, you automatically remain on the Standard plan with all your current features.

**Note**: If you have BigPicture Enterprise, read about the [migration path to the Advanced version.](/cms_trial/space/SPM/3483959305/BigPicture+Enterprise+migration+to+BigPicture+Advanced/)

As your needs evolve, you can easily upgrade between plans to access additional value.

Learn the pricing and use cases differences on the [Marketplace page](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/apps/1212259/bigpicture-portfolio-resource-management-for-jira?hosting=cloud&tab=pricing).

### Frequently asked questions

**Can I trial the editions?**  
Yes.

- If you're a new customer, you can start a free 30-day trial of any plan and switch plans at any time.
- If you're already using the Standard plan, you can try the Advanced plan free for 30 days.

**Can I upgrade or downgrade between editions?**  
Yes, you can upgrade from Standard to Advanced at any time through [Marketplace](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/apps/1212259/bigpicture-portfolio-resource-management-for-jira?hosting=cloud&tab=pricing) or your Atlassian admin hub in Jira. Downgrading is also possible, but please review feature dependencies to ensure a smooth transition. See [Install and manage app access](https://support.atlassian.com/organization-administration/docs/installing-and-managing-app-access/).

**Note**: Customers on an **annual billing cycle** will have to contact Customer Advocate to upgrade.

**Were any features removed from the Standard plan in favor of the Advanced plan?**  
No. In fact, **more** features are now available in the Standard plan, and we will continue to invest in both plans.

## Data Center to Cloud migration

There are **no changes** to the [migration process](https://support.appfire.com/space/DLP/298320391/Migration+(data+center+-%3E+cloud)?utm_source=htmlredirect&utm_medium=confluence).

### Risks module

BigPicture Standard includes the new [Risk Management](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918798201/Risk+management+module+(new+module)/) module. **Data from the** [**legacy Risks module**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918699830/Risks+module+(old+module)/) **cannot be migrated to the new module.** The legacy Risks module is not available in new Cloud installations.

### Finanancials module

The Financials module is **not affected**. Its data is migrated regardless of the app edition and becomes available when you enable the Advanced plan.

## Impact on BigPicture Enterprise customers

At present, BigPicture Enterprise and the BigPicture Advanced plan offer full feature parity.

BigPicture Enterprise is no longer available for purchase and is **planned to be sunset in 2027**. The exact date will be confirmed and communicated to all customers once finalized.

**We strongly recommend enabling the Advanced plan in BigPicture and uninstalling the BigPicture Enterprise app at your earliest convenience**. Your data is safe and will be preserved. This ensures continued access to the latest updates, improvements, and capabilities.

For more information on migration, go to the [following page](/cms_trial/space/SPM/3483959305/BigPicture+Enterprise+migration+to+BigPicture+Advanced/).
