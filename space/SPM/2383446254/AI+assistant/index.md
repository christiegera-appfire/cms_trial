# AI assistant

The AI assistant function is now rolling out and is available only to some customers.

The AI chatbot is designed to help you quickly find answers regarding BigPicture without leaving the app. It is based on our product documentation and provides context suggestions so you can easily explore relevant topics.

## Access

Click the AI assistant icon in the window's upper-right corner to open the AI assistant.

![ai-assistent.png](/cms_trial/assets/432448b4-287e-4f04-badf-01d9f63e6468.png)

Use the preloaded suggestions or type in your question. Then, you can also ask follow-up questions.

## What the chatbot can do

- Suggest relevant documentation pages based on your questions.
- Help you understand product features in more detail.
- Offer guidance on exploring new functionality or adjacent use cases.

## What the chatbot cannot do

- It does **not** pull information from your individual Jira environment.
- It does **not** read or access your company’s BigPicture instance.
- It does **not** read the data displayed within BigPicture.
- It cannot resolve very custom implementations or data-specific issues. For those, please contact our Support Team or use the in-app chat.
