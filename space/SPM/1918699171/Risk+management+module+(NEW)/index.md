# Risk management module (NEW)

## Risk management module (old navigation)

Click to expand the guide

## About the Risk management module

With the new Risk management module, you can register, assess, and prioritize risks without leaving BigPicture. You can also monitor progress and link risks to your company's day-to-day work. When creating a risk register, you can choose from four industry-standard risk management templates. All templates are fully customizable.

The module allows you to create as many risk registers and calculations per box as you need.

The following video presents an overview of the Risk Management module.

### Multiple risk registers

Multiple risk registers can help you with:

- Categorizing risks by type, category, or nature.
- Managing risk assessments by different teams.

![Screen showing a list of Risk Registers.](/cms_trial/assets/d5631572-1443-43b7-8387-aeae2c2eb7e9.png)

### Multiple calculations

Multiple calculations within a single risk register can help you with:

- Comparing initial risk assessments with residual risks.
- Evaluating the same risks using different frameworks or scenarios.
- Analyzing risks from various perspectives, for example, business and development.

![Risk register list showing multiple calculation columns.](/cms_trial/assets/4cd5019f-9f4a-4dac-ad2a-683671774905.png)

## Constraints

1. The Risk management module isn’t available in boxes with the sub-scope type. When you open the module in such a box, you will see the following screen.

![Message displayed when the Risk management module isn't available.](/cms_trial/assets/2f5d18e9-cecc-4e04-a811-2d409764529e.png)

1. Risk registers can only be created in own-scope boxes.
2. At the none-scope level, you can view risk registers from own-scope child boxes but cannot create new ones.
