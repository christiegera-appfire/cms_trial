# Progress Dashboard

**About this page**

This page describes features that are supported **only** in [**BigPicture Advanced**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/3451617346/BigPicture+Standard+vs+Advanced/). These features are not available in BigPicture Standard.

The *Progress Dashboard* page provides a clear, visual overview of your OKRs, helping you track progress, celebrate successes, and pinpoint areas for improvement. It’s ideal for meetings, presentations, and management.

This page focuses on your team’s performance, showing overall progress and recent wins and highlighting any OKRs that are off track or at risk.

![An overview of the Progress Dashboard page in the OKR module.](/cms_trial/assets/8f0f2a0f-8b20-46a0-8770-a06bb9c85ea6.png)

## Filters

You can [filter the visible OKRs](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918637654/OKR+Filters%2C+search%2C+and+saved+views/) on the Progress Dashboard by the following [OKR attributes](/cms_trial/space/SPM/2324726185/OKR+attributes/):

- period
- teams
- labels
- OKR types

Once you apply a filter, you can **save** that filtered view and reuse it whenever you need it.

![okr-dashboard-filters.png](/cms_trial/assets/02d6d607-8f6f-466b-9284-faefb3a7db6c.png)

## OKR statistics

### Team progress

The two **Team progress** charts show the total number of OKRs for the selected teams in the chosen period. They give an overview of how Objectives and Key Results are distributed across different statuses.

The progress shown is based on each Key Result, weighted according to its assigned contribution. Strategic themes’ and Objectives’ progress does not duplicate the overall progress, ensuring accuracy. Higher-weighted Key Results are given more importance in the calculation.

![Team progress charts in the OKR module.](/cms_trial/assets/9e487a35-a8c0-4922-944d-1f057db61768.png)

### Successes

The **Successes** section highlights the number of OKRs that are on track or were [updated](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918637972/Update+OKR/) from at-risk or off-track to on track. These accomplishments are the milestones to celebrate with your team.

![The successes section on the progress dashboard page in the OKR module.](/cms_trial/assets/ce741958-217e-4687-8f26-2ce60ad70e4f.png)

### OKRs off track and OKRs at risk

These two sections highlight OKRs that are off track and at risk. They display a detailed list of OKRs assigned to the chosen team, making it easier to identify risks early and implement mitigation plans.

Click the **Show details** link to see those OKRs on the [*OKR Overview*](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918834317/OKR+Overview/) page.

![A list of OKRs that are off track and at risk in the OKR module.](/cms_trial/assets/08b120e9-55fb-4b5b-b2da-f66ac72e6ea8.png)

### Statuses over time

This section provides a separate historical view of Strategic themes and Objectives and of the Key Results, showing their statuses over the past ten weeks. It helps track OKR progress, identify trends, and understand performance changes over time.

![Statuses over time charts in the OKR module.](/cms_trial/assets/aaa0edf3-e071-4eb1-922b-4ac8116d4b57.png)
