# Help and support

## Support

If you have any specific feedback to share as you test, please submit it via the [support portal](https://appfire.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/11). Our product team will reach out for any additional information.

## Feedback sessions

Channel Managers and our Product team will conduct two feedback sessions (one for the sandbox and one for the real environment) to collect feedback and gather additional insights.

## Urgent issues

For any urgent issues, you can always reach out to your channel manager directly using Slack or email.
