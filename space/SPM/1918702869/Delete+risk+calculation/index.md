# Delete risk calculation

## Delete risk calculation (old navigation)

Click to expand the guide

To learn more about risk calculation attributes, see the [Risk register frameworks](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918865091/Risk+register+frameworks/) page.

To delete an existing risk calculation:

When you delete a template, all metrics assigned to your issues and risks using this template will be deleted. This change is irreversible.

1. From the **Risk management** module, click the risk register name.

1. Click the **Settings** button.
2. Select a template you want to delete.
3. Click **Delete framework**.
4. To confirm, click **Yes delete**.

See an interactive demo on how to delete a risk calculation.

## Delete risk calculation (new navigation)

Click to expand the guide

To learn more about risk calculation attributes, see the [Risk register frameworks](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918865091/Risk+register+frameworks/) page.

To delete an existing risk calculation:

When you delete a template, all metrics assigned to your issues and risks using this template will be deleted. This change is irreversible.

1. From the **Risk Management** module, click the risk register name.
2. Select **Configuration** from the dropdown menu.

   ![Screenshot of selecting the Configuration button from the drop-down menu in the Risk Management module.](/cms_trial/assets/173a5e07-a0be-4ddc-933f-37cc1efa5eeb.png)
3. Select a template you want to delete.
4. Click **Delete framework**.
5. To confirm, click **Yes, delete**.
6. Click **Save**.

See an interactive demo on how to delete a risk calculation.
