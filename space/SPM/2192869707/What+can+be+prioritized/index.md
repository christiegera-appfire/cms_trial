# What can be prioritized

**About this page**

This page describes features that are supported **only** in [**BigPicture Advanced**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/3451617346/BigPicture+Standard+vs+Advanced/). These features are not available in BigPicture Standard.

## What can be prioritized (old navigation)

Click to expand the guide

In the Priorities module, you can prioritize these key elements:

- **Tasks** (Jira work items)
- **BigPicture boxes**
- **Goals** (OKRs)

You can easily switch between the tabs and manage priorities among tasks, boxes, and goals in one place.

The [template](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918766042/Prioritization+templates/) you choose will be consistently applied across all tasks, boxes, and goals.

![Tabs in the Priorities module in BigPicture.](/cms_trial/assets/789683c3-7e44-4e3f-9291-9d913a493eea.png)

## Prioritize tasks

[BigPicture basic tasks](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918405930/Basic+tasks+%2F+BigPicture+tasks/) aren’t displayed in the Priorities module, and you can’t prioritize them.

Under the **Tasks** tab, you can prioritize individual Jira work items added to the box you’re in.

![The Tasks tab in the Priorities module in BigPicture.](/cms_trial/assets/764cb82c-0f43-4327-9434-07c84d5b8883.png)

To see Jira work items from all boxes, open the Main box in the Priorities module.

![The Tasks tab in the Priorities module in BigPicture.](/cms_trial/assets/8381b9f5-bae1-49cd-b87b-6c5b03f15cda.png)

## Prioritize BigPicture boxes

Under the **Boxes** tab, you can prioritize all child boxes of the box you’re in.

![The Boxes tab in the Priorities module in BigPicture.](/cms_trial/assets/e5c6415c-abd5-4c0a-9833-7ba9f3c12de0.png)

To prioritize all BigPicture boxes, open the Main box in the Priorities module.

![The Boxes tab in the Priorities module in BigPicture.](/cms_trial/assets/874ea748-2092-4e89-86ee-a537e8fbad74.png)

## Prioritize goals (OKRs)

Under the **Goals** tab, you can prioritize Objectives and Key Results from the [OKR module](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918535397/OKR+module/) linked to the box you’re in.

If you don’t see OKRs, go to the OKR module and make sure they are linked to the box.

![A close up of the goals tab in the priorities module put side by side with the okr module.](/cms_trial/assets/d7309a68-e47e-4e2b-823e-e4820929f2e4.png)

To prioritize Objectives and Key Results from all boxes, open the Main box in the Priorities module.

![The Goals tab in the Priorities module in BigPicture.](/cms_trial/assets/6657a531-37ad-4665-a386-7df674caa1c1.png)

## Filters

The filtering options differ depending on the tab (Tasks, Boxes, Goals). See the [Filter and search priorities](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918539227/Filter+and+search+priorities/) page to learn more.

## Limitations

- If a prioritization template is based on Jira fields, it will not be applied to boxes and goals. You’ll see the following message.

  ![A warning in the Boxes tab saying tha the selected template does not support prioritizing boxes and OKRs. The linked Jira field is not compatible. To enable prioritization, switch to a different template.](/cms_trial/assets/e73ec422-9e58-4841-8761-f8b07ec3b30d.png)
- The [priority poker game](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918407173/Priority+poker/) is not available for boxes and goals.
- The Priorities module can display up to 1,000 boxes and goals (OKRs). To view and prioritize more, apply filters to narrow down the results.
