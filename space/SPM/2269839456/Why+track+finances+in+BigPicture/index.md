# Why track finances in BigPicture

**About this page**

This page describes features that are supported **only** in [**BigPicture Advanced**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/3451617346/BigPicture+Standard+vs+Advanced/). These features are not available in BigPicture Standard.

The BigPicture Financials module by Appfire offers a powerful, integrated solution for managing project and portfolio costs directly within BigPicture. Financials moves beyond the limitations of external tools and manual processes, providing a comprehensive overview of your financial landscape, enabling smarter, data-driven decisions.

**Benefits of using the BigPicture Financials module include:**

- **No more spreadsheets and separate financials tracking:** Eliminate the need for cumbersome external spreadsheets and disparate financial systems. The Financials module centralizes all your cost data within BigPicture, creating a single source of truth for your project finances.
- **Real-time insights:** Gain immediate visibility into your financial performance. The module tracks actual versus planned costs in real-time, allowing you to identify potential overruns, monitor trends, and make timely adjustments to keep your projects on budget.
- **Shorter implementation time:** BigPicture Financials offers a significantly shorter implementation time than other methods of tracking costs in Jira. Its native integration with Jira means less setup and configuration, allowing you to start monitoring costs quickly and efficiently.
- **Understanding the "Big Picture" of project costs:** The Financials module provides a holistic view of your project and portfolio finances. You can configure rates and effort modes and drill down into specific initiatives to understand the cost breakdown at various levels, ensuring you always completely understand your financial "Big Picture."
