## Can I upgrade my BigPicture license to BigPicture Enterprise?

BigPicture Enterprise requires an active BigPicture product on your instance. You can additionally buy a [BigPicture Enterprise Cloud](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/apps/1215158/bigpicture-enterprise-strategic-management-for-jira) license from the Atlassian Marketplace. These are independent licenses that need to be paid for separately.
