# Backlog panel

## Backlog panel (old navigation)

## Unscheduled tasks new view

The Backlog panel holds unscheduled tasks, which are:

- Tasks without start and end dates defined
- Tasks with one date (start or end date defined)
- Tasks that are not assigned to any individual, team, or skill

Having those tasks available in the Backlog panel lets you easily assign them by moving a task from the backlog to swimlanes.

## Resources backlog

### Backlog

To see unscheduled tasks, open the sidebar on the right.

## Available columns

The following columns are available in the Backlog panel:

- Key
- Summary
- Start Date
- End Date
- Assignee
- Team
- Skills
- Original Estimate

![An overview of the Backlog panel i nthe new resources module.](/cms_trial/assets/a2151a54-c8bc-42c1-8710-247d377e4b96.png)

## Search box

You can find tasks quickly with the [search box](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918407393/Search+box/) functionality. The search box operates in two modes:

- Text search
- JQL search

![Search box inside the Backlog panel.](/cms_trial/assets/edd6fd90-62f1-44ff-99cb-6184cc6fcb9a.png)

You can also filter the tasks by the following filters:

- No dates
- No assignee
- No team

You can also choose more than one filter at a time.

Example:

Checking the **No team** and **No assignee** checkboxes shows:

- Tasks without a team and with an assignee
- Tasks without an assignee and with a team
- Tasks without an assignee and without a team

![image-20241210-104855.png](/cms_trial/assets/842d90f1-f331-43c5-8e81-3e2a673ef9b8.png)

## Inline edit

You can quickly make changes (for example, add dates, change an assignee, or modify a summary) with [inline editing](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918637324/Inline+edit/).

![backlog-inline.png](/cms_trial/assets/4cdfe780-a0b1-40a2-9995-259215770d93.png)

## Schedule tasks

### Move tasks from the Backlog panel

You can schedule tasks by moving them from the Backlog panel. For more information, see the [Move tasks](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918864819/Move+tasks/) page.

### Edit missing dates inline

Double-click and complete the missing dates.

![backlog-add-dates.png](/cms_trial/assets/d006cb10-a15e-4940-99b7-03a3cd6c5e3c.png)

### Reports new view

More information about reports: [Contextual reports](/cms_trial/space/SPM/2481848664/Contextual+reports/).

The Reports tab displays contextual reports you can show or hide by selecting or deselecting a report in the dropdown menu.

![Reports tab in the backlog panel.](/cms_trial/assets/2796e562-3f06-454a-a106-efa13aafdeb9.png)
