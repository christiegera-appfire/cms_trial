# Close OKR

**About this page**

This page describes features that are supported **only** in [**BigPicture Advanced**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/3451617346/BigPicture+Standard+vs+Advanced/). These features are not available in BigPicture Standard.

Closing OKRs is an important step in their lifecycle. It is where you assess whether the goals you've set for a particular period (typically a quarter or a year) have been achieved.

This process involves reviewing results, determining whether you met, exceeded, or fell short of your Key Results, and reflecting on performance. It is a crucial part of goal-setting and continuous improvement in any organization.

- By reviewing the OKRs at the end of the period, businesses can determine if everyone was working on the right things. Closing OKRs ensures efforts focus on what truly matters for the organization's success.
- Closing OKRs allows the organization to hold individuals and teams accountable for their contributions. This accountability drives better performance and ensures that people understand how their work impacts the larger business objectives.
- Whether a Key Result was achieved (or not) gives tangible insights into how the business is progressing toward its long-term goals. It also helps identify areas of underperformance and strengths that can be leveraged.
- After closing OKRs, businesses often gather valuable data that can inform strategic decisions, such as whether the results indicate a shift in market conditions.

## Close an OKR

Closed OKRs cannot be updated and no longer contribute to the progress of their parent OKRs.

When you close a parent OKR, you can close all its child OKRs as well. For that reason, when closing your OKRs, consider starting with the top one.

The closed OKR summary is struck through, but the OKR is not removed from the hierarchy.

![A closed Key Result under an Objective.](/cms_trial/assets/cfb1aff1-a8e9-41ae-a204-d76571de910a.png)

You can close an OKR on the following:

- [*OKR Details*](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918536390/OKR+details+page/) page
- [OKR Details side panel](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918865686/OKR+Details+side+panel/)

### OKR Details page

1. Click the **Update progress** button located in the upper-right corner.
2. The **OKR update** modal displays. Click the **Close & grade** button.
3. The **Close the OKR** modal displays. Fill in the details.
4. Click the **Close strategic theme**/**Close objective/ Close key result** buttonto finish the process.

### OKR Details side panel

1. Click the **Post an update** button.
2. The **OKR update** modal displays. Click the **Close & grade** button.
3. The **Close the OKR** modal displays. Fill in the details.
4. Click the **Close strategic theme**/**Close objective/ Close key result** buttonto finish the process.
