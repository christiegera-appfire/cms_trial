# Comment OKR update

**About this page**

This page describes features that are supported **only** in [**BigPicture Advanced**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/3451617346/BigPicture+Standard+vs+Advanced/). These features are not available in BigPicture Standard.

Posting comments on OKRs lets team members to share feedback, ask questions, or provide updates, fostering collaboration and keeping everyone informed about progress and challenges.

The OKR module lets you post, edit, and delete your own comments made to individual OKR updates, as well as reply to comments left by other team members.

## Post a comment

To add comments to OKR updates, you need [editing permissions](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918765815/OKR+module+permissions/).

In the advanced permission setup, admins can additionally allow or disallow individual user roles to comment.

![The activity section ](/cms_trial/assets/8e83674f-f6b6-4413-8703-531b4d5d0c45.png)

The **Activity** section on the [*OKR details*](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918536390/OKR+details+page/) page and [OKR Details side panel](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918865686/OKR+Details+side+panel/) displays a chronological list of updates and comments made to the currently viewed OKR.

To add a new comment:

1. In the **Activity** section, click the **arrow** icon under the update you want to comment on.
2. A **New comment** text box displays. Enter your comment. If you want to mention a specific person in your comment, use the **@** (at) sign followed by their name.
3. Click **Save**.
4. A new comment appears marked with the date and time it was made. You can collapse and expand comments under each activity.

## Delete, edit, and reply to a comment

- You can edit and delete only your own comments.
- You can reply to your own comments and the comments left by other team members.

After you add a comment or navigate to one left by another team member, you will see additional options to take further action.

You can delete, edit, and reply to comments on the OKR Details side panel and the *OKR Details* page.

To delete, edit, or reply to a comment:

1. In the **Activity** section, under the update you want to delete or modify, expand comments and select:

   1. **Reply** to reply to your own or another’s comment.
   2. **Edit** to edit your own comment/reply.
   3. **Delete** to delete your own comment/reply.

## Email notifications on comments

When a comment is posted, all relevant users will get an email notification. Each user can choose which notifications they want to receive. To adjust your preferences, go to [**Settings**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918832829/OKR+Notifications/) [>](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918832829/OKR+Notifications/) [**Notifications**](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918832829/OKR+Notifications/).
