# Release notes May 20, 2025

**Release date**: May 20, 2025

Our team is thrilled to announce the latest release of BigPicture Enterprise Cloud.

## Enhancements

## Timebox schedule

### Use existing sprints to generate a timebox schedule

When you set up a new timebox schedule, you can choose to turn on automatic timebox creation.

**Note**: This cannot be enabled for an existing schedule.

![image-20250519-115421.png](/cms_trial/assets/0de3b99f-2efa-414b-a315-7e0ae93a5288.png)

Next, connect a timebox schedule to a Jira board to ensure correct synchronization. Timeboxes will be created automatically, and their content will be synchronized with Jira sprints.

![image-20250519-115545.png](/cms_trial/assets/b852b43b-ca19-4dae-a4ba-429719f956e3.png)

When a sprint-based timebox schedule is assigned to a box, **the board is automatically added to the scope of the box** to ensure all tasks from your board are included.

![image-20250519-120741.png](/cms_trial/assets/cde6372a-7e2b-4ce9-9e65-0dd5c6e789d5.png)

## Financials module

### Compare the budget to the total cost

The total cost includes the cost of both completed and future tasks, helping you see how the project is doing compared to the assigned budget.

## Resources module

### Scroll down without losing the timebox context

When you scroll down, the timebox information stays pinned to the top.

![image-20250519-122831.png](/cms_trial/assets/be9a1b27-ee52-4328-a866-b16e833b446c.png)

**Note**: Display settings must be adjusted to show timeboxes.

![image-20250519-122941.png](/cms_trial/assets/63b06c78-274b-4141-98f4-e6d15907aa29.png)

### Use the ‘No Iteration’ swimlane to identify tasks that aren’t assigned to timeboxes

When you adjust the view of the Resources module to display ‘projects’ swimlanes (swimlanes based on timeboxes, at the top, you will find the **No Iteration** swimlane. It contains tasks that aren’t assigned to any timeboxes.

**Note**: Unavailable for portfolio boxes.

![image-20250519-145623.png](/cms_trial/assets/028595fc-c2a3-4a22-a1fd-b8486c80ec9b.png)

The **No Iteration** swimlane is also available when you group swimlanes by project.

![image-20250519-123610.png](/cms_trial/assets/90fc5daf-8d3e-447f-9955-3b4d70099add.png)

### Information about a user not being in any team is visible under the resource name

Information is visible when a resource hasn’t been added to any team in a box, and the selected effort mode unit is **%.**

![image-20250519-124121.png](/cms_trial/assets/92388627-215e-48bd-a780-4cffdd885d18.png)
