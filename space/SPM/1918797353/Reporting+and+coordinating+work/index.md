# Reporting and coordinating work

## About reporting and coordinating work

BigPicture provides a powerful suite of tools for reporting and coordinating work, helping teams stay aligned and informed. With customizable reports, real-time data visualization, and flexible aggregation options, you can track progress, monitor key metrics, and gain valuable insights into project performance.

Features like [Share view](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918504353/Share+view/) and [Gadgets](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918830223/Gadgets/) further enhance collaboration, making it easy to share updates and streamline team coordination.

### OKR module

Use the <https://appfire.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/SPM/pages/1918801225> that provides a clear overview of your team's OKRs, highlighting achievements, overall performance, and areas that need attention. With the <https://appfire.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/SPM/pages/1918864924>, you can view key OKR data on your Jira dashboard, along with other project metrics and stats.

Additionally, the [Share view (OKR module)](/cms_trial/space/SPM/1918408436/Share+view+(OKR+module)/) feature allows you to share your current OKR module view, including filters, sorting, and column setup, ensuring seamless collaboration across teams.
