# August 12, 2022

## Export your issues to Excel and CSV

The [Rich Filter Results](/cms_trial/space/RFCDOC/783941759/The+Rich+Filter+Results+Gadget/) gadget now allows you to export the issues it displays to an Excel or CSV file. The export uses the currently selected [view](/cms_trial/space/RFCDOC/783941729/Configure+views/), so you'll get the same columns in the exported table. You can export up to 1,000 issues at one time.

The export is directly available by clicking on the corresponding icon in the gadget footer.
