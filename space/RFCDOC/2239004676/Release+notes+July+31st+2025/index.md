# Release notes July 31st 2025

**Release date**: July 31, 2025

Our team is thrilled to announce the latest release of Rich Filters for Jira Dashboards.

---

## New feature

## Define presets in the controller

Every dashboard user can now define their own presets in the controller. Any combination of quick filters can be saved as a preset. Presets appear as toggle buttons at the top of the controller and allow you to apply the corresponding filters with a single click.

To access presets, click the presets icon (▢) in the controller footer.

To learn more, see [Create presets in Rich Filter Controller](/cms_trial/space/RFCDOC/2241069104/Create+presets+in+Rich+Filter+Controller/).

## Enhancements

## Navigate between filter states in the controller

You can now move back and forth between previous filter states in the dashboard—just like navigating through your browser history. Simply click the arrow icons (▢) at the bottom-right of the controller to step through your filter history.

![click to go back.png](/cms_trial/assets/e80400f2-8606-460f-bf86-5cac7b16d5b1.png)![Click to go forward](/cms_trial/assets/653c1205-09a8-41f8-a507-745687dedb50.png)

## Freeze columns in the Rich Filter Results table

You can now freeze selected columns in a view so they remain visible during horizontal scrolling in the Rich Filter Results gadget. For example, you might freeze the *Issue Type*, *Key* and *Summary* columns to keep key information visible as you scroll through the rest of the data.

![Frozen columns.png](/cms_trial/assets/1448378c-60d6-4fec-af59-761ad7e860b8.png)
