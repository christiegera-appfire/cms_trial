# December 7, 2022

## Quick tables in Flexi Charts

In the previous release, we added quick tables in two rich filter chart gadgets: Time Series Chart and Created vs Resolved Chart. This release completes the coverage of this feature by adding *quick tables* in the remaining chart gadget: [Rich Filter Flexi Charts](/cms_trial/space/RFCDOC/783941863/The+Rich+Filter+Flexi+Charts+Gadget/). Reminder: the *quick table* allows you to easily display a table with all the values used in the chart. Click on the corresponding icon at the bottom-right of the gadget to toggle the quick table on and off.
