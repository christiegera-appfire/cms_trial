# July 27, 2022

## Edit your issues directly from the dashboard

The [Rich Filter Results](/cms_trial/space/RFCDOC/783941759/The+Rich+Filter+Results+Gadget/) gadgets now give direct access to an issue dialog – a screen where you can view and edit your issues without leaving the dashboard. When you hover over the lines of the results table, an icon appears at the right of the line. Simply click on this icon to open the dialog for that issue. Any changes you make in the dialog are automatically saved, and when you close the dialog, the dashboard is automatically updated with the changes.
