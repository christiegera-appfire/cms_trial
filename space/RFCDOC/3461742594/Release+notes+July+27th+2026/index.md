**Rich Filters for Jira Dashboards is now fully on Forge**

The app now runs completely on Atlassian Forge, Atlassian's Cloud platform. This means better security and an app that stays up to date with Atlassian's latest standards.

From your side, nothing changes about how you use our apps inside Jira. The experience is the same.
