# July 20, 2021

## Aggregation by Smart Filters in Statistics and Flexi Charts Gadgets

The [Rich Filter Statistics](/cms_trial/space/RFCDOC/783941781/The+Rich+Filter+Statistics+Gadget/), [Two Dimensional Statistics](/cms_trial/space/RFCDOC/783941803/The+Rich+Filter+Two+Dimensional+Statistics+Gadget/) and [Flexi Charts](/cms_trial/space/RFCDOC/783941863/The+Rich+Filter+Flexi+Charts+Gadget/) gadgets can now aggregate issue data by [*smart filters*](/cms_trial/space/RFCDOC/783941713/Configure+smart+filters/) in addition to issue fields.

The *smart filters* defined in the selected *rich filter* are now available in the options for the Statistic Type field in the configuration form of the gadgets. When used in this manner, *smart filters* behave like *smart fields* and are displayed in the table view of the gadgets with the colors defined in their configuration.
