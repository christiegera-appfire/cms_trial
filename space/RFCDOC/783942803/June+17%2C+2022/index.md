# June 17, 2022

In this release:

## Support for additional fields in Rich Filter Results views

The following fields can now be added as columns in [views](/cms_trial/space/RFCDOC/783941729/Configure+views/) to be displayed in [Rich Filter Results](/cms_trial/space/RFCDOC/783941759/The+Rich+Filter+Results+Gadget/) gadgets:

- the issue field *Parent*,
- the fields *Team* and *Parent Link* from [Advanced Roadmaps](https://support.atlassian.com/jira-software-cloud/docs/get-started-with-advanced-roadmaps/).

## Support for statistics and dynamic filtering on the Field Team

The field *team* from [Advanced Roadmaps](https://support.atlassian.com/jira-software-cloud/docs/get-started-with-advanced-roadmaps/) can now be:

- added as a [dynamic filter](/cms_trial/space/RFCDOC/783941707/Configure+dynamic+filters/) in the configuration of your rich filters, allowing you to filter the issues on your dashboard;
- used as *Statistic type* in the statistics and flexi charts gadgets: [Rich Filter Statistics](/cms_trial/space/RFCDOC/783941781/The+Rich+Filter+Statistics+Gadget/), [Rich Filter Two Dimensional Statistics](/cms_trial/space/RFCDOC/783941803/The+Rich+Filter+Two+Dimensional+Statistics+Gadget/) and [Rich Filter Flexi Charts](/cms_trial/space/RFCDOC/783941863/The+Rich+Filter+Flexi+Charts+Gadget/).
