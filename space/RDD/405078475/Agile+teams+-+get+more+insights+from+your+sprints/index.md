# Agile teams - get more insights from your sprints

Agile teams can get the most powerful insights from their sprints with highly customizable and shareable dashboards. No technical skills are needed; anyone on the team can create them.

![Dashboard Hub Agile teams get more insights from your sprints dashboard preview](/cms_trial/assets/f89508ff-c74c-4975-9803-21b5c116bc37.png)

## Benefits for Scrum teams

- Stay aligned on sprint goals, spot blockers, and measure team velocity at a glance with a pre-built Agile dashboard template.
- Keep track of the latest releases, resolved issues, and sprint health.
- Track epic progress, the amount of work remaining, and work completed to identify opportunities to provide better results.

## How to start

1. Select the **Agile pre-built dashboard template** and configure the gadgets (all compatible gadgets will be configured automatically to save time).
2. Adapt it to your concrete visualization needs by adding your preferred gadgets from our collection and 10+ scrum-specific metrics.
3. Need even more tailored displays? Add the **JQL custom charts gadget** to create customized data representations with JQL or filters, or add the **Formula Cards gadget** to build specific data views calculating custom fields.

   - For example: Story Points per Developer in the current sprint represented in a Tile chart.
4. If you want even more customization, you can move and resize gadgets, change the colors of the slideshow, and more. Also, make sure to review the dashboard permissions.
5. Share it with your team using a **Public Link**.

Dashboard Hub dashboards will serve as a single source of truth for your teams. Everyone will check, track, and align on the same data, making informed decisions.
