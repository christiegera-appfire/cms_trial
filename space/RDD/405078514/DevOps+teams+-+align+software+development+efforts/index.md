# DevOps teams - align software development efforts

Create centralized dashboards that align software development and operations to elevate your data game. Stay on top of everything that is going on with advanced metrics that centralize data from all your relevant products into one place.

![Dashboard Hub DevOps teams align software development efforts template preview](/cms_trial/assets/8926d281-f042-4ca9-9c52-902c74512658.png)

## Benefits for DevOps teams

- Boost communication and collaboration between development, IT, and operations teams.
- Ensure continuous integration and delivery, integration, automation, and deployment.
- Monitor all the processes involved in the DevOps chain. Track the last deployments, critical incidents, and who is on-call duty when an alert or a service is down.

## How to start

1. Select the **DevOps prebuilt dashboard template** and configure the gadgets (all compatible gadgets will be configured automatically to save time).
2. Adapt it to your concrete visualization needs by adding your preferred gadgets from our collection of Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket, Assets, Opsgenie, and Statuspage metrics.
3. Needing even more tailored displays? Add the **JQL custom charts gadget** to create customized data representations with JQL or filters, or add the **Formula Cards gadget** to build specific data views calculating custom fields.

   - Example: Create reporting charts about Assets Objects from Atlassian Assets and display them in a table represented in custom columns.
4. If you want even more customization, you can move and resize gadgets, change the colors of the slideshow, and more. Also, make sure to review the dashboard permissions.
5. Share it with your team using a **Public Link**.

Dashboard Hub dashboards will serve as a single source of truth for your teams. Everyone will check, track, and align on the same data, making informed decisions.
