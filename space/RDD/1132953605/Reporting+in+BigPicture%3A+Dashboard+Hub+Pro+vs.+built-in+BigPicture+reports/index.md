# Reporting in BigPicture: Dashboard Hub Pro vs. built-in BigPicture reports

## Overview

[BigPicture](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/apps/1212259/bigpicture-project-management-ppm?hosting=cloud&tab=overview)'s built-in reports offer robust capabilities for project management, providing valuable insights into your project's progress, resource allocation, and timelines. These reports are highly effective for tracking key metrics and visualizing project data within BigPicture.

However, the reporting capabilities can be significantly extended with an integration with [**Dashboard Hub Pro**](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/apps/1223898/dashboard-hub-pro-charts-reports-time-in-status-tables?tab=overview&hosting=cloud).

Below, we outline the key differences between using native BigPicture reports and extending your reporting capabilities with **Dashboard Hub Pro.** This comparison will help you understand how each solution can be utilized to maximize your project management effectiveness.
