# Connect data from other Appfire products

This section provides information on how to connect data from other Appfire products in Dashboard Hub including BigPicture, Comala Document Management, and Time to SLA. These integrations use our dedicated gadgets. If you want to connect data from other products with a REST API, you can use the Dashboard Hub Custom Reports gadget. See the [Custom Reports](/cms_trial/space/RDD/1529219266/Custom+Reports+-+REST+API+integration/) section to learn more.

## BigPicture

BigPicture is Appfire’s leading Portfolio, Product, and Project Management application for Jira. It gives managers unprecedented clarity of information, helps them neatly visualize roadmaps of even the most complex initiatives, smartly distribute workload and allocate resources, efficiently coordinates teams' work, quickly reports progress, and ensures perfect strategy-to-execution alignment at all levels.

The system supports agile (SAFe® included), classic, and hybrid management methodologies and allows for their free combination at the portfolio level.

If you use BigPicture, you can use the [BigPicture Custom Charts gadget](/cms_trial/space/RDD/629873831/BigPicture+Custom+Charts/) to create BigPicture charts and dashboards, streamlining access and visualization of crucial information. The BigPicture Custom Charts gadget is tailored for creating charts based on BigPicture boxes. See [BigPicture integration](/cms_trial/space/RDD/628033554/BigPicture+integration/) for more information.

To get started with a free trial of BigPicture, visit the BigPicture [Marketplace listing](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/apps/1212259/bigpicture-project-management-ppm?hosting=cloud&tab=overview).

## Comala Document Management

Comala Document Management is a powerful lifecycle management tool for Confluence documentation. It helps you build robust data governance, a fundamental best practice for small and large organizations. Use Comala Document Management to manage, review, and approve your documents in Confluence and automate your document lifecycle.

Comala Document Management’s reporting tools can be connected with Dashboard Hub for wider visualization using our [Document Workflow Custom Charts](/cms_trial/space/RDD/874905671/Document+Workflow+Custom+Charts/) gadget.

See [Comala Document Management](/cms_trial/space/RDD/1358136375/Comala+Document+Management/) for more information.

This integration works with Comala Document Management for Confluence Data Center. If you are using the cloud app and want to connect with Dashboard Hub, you can use our [Custom Reports](/cms_trial/space/RDD/1529219266/Custom+Reports+-+REST+API+integration/).

To get started with a free trial of Comala Document Management Data Center, visit our [Marketplace listing](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/apps/142/comala-document-management?hosting=datacenter).

## Time to SLA

Time to SLA is Appfire's best-selling service-level agreement (SLA) tracking app for Jira. It lets you define, track, and report on SLAs and operational-level agreements (OLAs) in minutes, with real-time countdowns, automated notifications before deadlines are missed, and powerful JQL-based reporting to monitor performance across projects.

If you use Time to SLA, you can use Dashboard Hub’s Time to SLA gadgets to visualize SLA performance directly on your Jira dashboards, streamlining access to critical service metrics. See [Time to SLA integration](/cms_trial/space/RDD/2281373700/Time+to+SLA+integration/) for more information.

To get started with a free trial of Time to SLA, visit the [Time to SLA Marketplace listing](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/apps/1211843/time-to-sla?hosting=cloud&tab=overview&utm_source=appfire.com&utm_medium=website&utm_campaign=grw_mustapps&utm_content=product_pages).
