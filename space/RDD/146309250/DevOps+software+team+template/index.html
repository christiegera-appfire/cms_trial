DevOps software team template

2 min read

Overview

DevOps is about communication and collaboration between development, IT, and operations teams. At the heart of the DevOps movement are practices like continuous integration and delivery, integration, automation, and deployment. Each of these practices produces outputs that require constant monitoring.

This dashboard groups gadgets to help monitor all the processes involved in the DevOps chain. By combining all the involved tools into a single source of truth, teams can deliver value more quickly and efficiently.

Transmit to the involved teams information about the last deployments, critical incidents, or who is on-call duty when an alert or a service is down. Combine these gadgets with others from the Scrum software team template or the IT Service Management team template.

Gadgets

This dashboard template comes with a set of gadgets that you can organize according to your needs. Learn more about adding and configuring gadgets in Add and configure gadgets.

STATE OF THE DEV UNION

Display the number of developers involved in your repositories, the number of repositories and the PRs merged

PIPELINES HISTORY

Display the average time to resolution for all requests in a period of time

DEPLOYMENTS

Display the current status of the deployment environments of the selected repositories

HISTORICAL UPTIME

Display in a calendar view the days your components had no incidents

ON-CALL SCHEDULE

Display the final schedule of the on-call schedule

LAST FAILED PIPELINE

Display the last failed pipeline of any of the selected repositories

OPERATIONS TEAM

Display the members of a specific response team together with their roles

LAST INCIDENTS

Display the last three incidents with their priority and associated information

OPEN ALERTS

Display the last three open alerts, together with their priority
