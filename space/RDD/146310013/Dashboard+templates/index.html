Dashboard templates

1 min read

Our dashboard templates feature gadgets tailored to the specific needs of various teams. To learn more about the individual gadgets in each template, see the Dashboard gadgets section.

Start sharing information quickly using one of our ready-to-go dashboard templates.

Scrum Software Team

SCRUM SOFTWARE TEAM

Help your scrum team to progress during your sprints and keep all the relevant information clearly visible

IT Service Management Team

IT SERVICE MANAGEMENT TEAM

Facilitate the spotting of business changes to deliver an amazing employee and customer experience

DevOps Software Team

DEVOPS SOFTWARE TEAM

Help monitoring all the processes involved in the DevOps chain

BigPicture PPM Insights

BIGPICTURE PPM INSIGHTS

Visualize data from multiple BigPicture boxes and tasks alongside Jira data for a comprehensive, cross-project view of project management

SALES CRM FOR MONDAY

Get useful insights into your Sales CRM for monday.com: Activities, leaderboards, sales representatives and business goals at a glance

AGILE DEV FOR MONDAY.COM

Keep all the relevant information available to your Agile dev team: Sprint goals, completed and remaining work, velocity, etc.

PROJECT TRACKING INSIGHTS

Track your projects portfolio and make informed decisions based on visual insights
