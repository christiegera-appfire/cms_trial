# Show data from different teams in a single dashboard

With the full-fledged metrics that Dashboard Hub provides, you can display data across your different teams in a single dashboard to obtain a greater view of what’s happening and take appropriate action.

Learn more about [how to enhance your data visualization](/cms_trial/space/RDD/146309637/How+to+enhance+your+data+visualization/).
