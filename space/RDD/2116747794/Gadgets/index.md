We have over 90 gadgets to help you build reports for a wide range of sources including Jira and Jira Service Management, Assets, BitBucket, BigPicture, Time to SLA, and more. We also let you design your own with Jira Custom Charts and Jira Custom Segments, or create reports for anything using REST API with our Custom Reports.  
To help you find the right gadget fast, have a look at our short gadget finder video, or, explore, the complete [catalogue](/cms_trial/space/RDD/146309915/Dashboard+gadgets+-+KPIs+and+metrics/).
