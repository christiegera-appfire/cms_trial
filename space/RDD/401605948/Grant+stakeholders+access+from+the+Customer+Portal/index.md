# Grant stakeholders access from the Customer Portal

Provide transparency to stakeholders by giving access to dashboards from the Customer Portal (Jira Service Management). With access to these dashboards, stakeholders can now check their relevant data whenever they need it – no more asking for reports!

Learn more about [managing user access to dashboards from the Customer Portal](/cms_trial/space/RDD/146309699/Manage+access+to+the+Jira+Service+Management+Customer+Portal/).
