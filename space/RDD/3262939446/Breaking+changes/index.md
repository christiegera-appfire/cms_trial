# Breaking changes

This page outlines breaking changes to the Dashboard Hub family of apps.

---

## Retirement of External Page gadget

**Announcement date**: November 4, 2026

**Affects version**: 5.8.0

The External Page gadget will be retired in version 5.8.0 and won't display data on your dashboards. To keep your views clean, remove the gadget from any affected dashboards.

---

## Upcoming breaking change

**Announcement date**: June 3, 2026

## Permissions scope change for subscriptions

**Date of change**: June 3, 2026

You do not need to do anything before this date.

We will soon be implementing a permissions scope change to Dashboard Hub to enable a new dashboard subscriptions feature. **Dashboard Subscriptions** lets you schedule dashboard snapshots to be delivered automatically to your stakeholders' inboxes. This feature will be released in the next major release, version 5.3.0.

To support this capability, the app requires an update to its permissions to access users' email addresses, which are used solely for sending subscription notifications. Email data is processed transiently and never stored, ensuring a seamless experience without compromising data privacy.

This change will require a manual update to Dashboard Hub Pro and Dashboard Hub for Confluence.
