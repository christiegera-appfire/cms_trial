# XKCD webcomic

## Overview

Your team will often view dashboards, metrics, charts, and indicators. This gadget provides a smile in the form of the xkcd webcomic, offering a small break in your day.

> [**Wikipedia**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xkcd): The serial webcomic ***xkcd*** was created in 2005 by American author Randall Munroe. Sometimes styled ***XKCD***, the comic's tagline describes it as ‘a webcomic of romance, sarcasm, math, and language.’

## Display the xkcd comic in your dashboard

This is not a normal gadget and must be implemented using [Custom Reports](/cms_trial/space/RDD/1529219266/Custom+Reports+-+REST+API+integration/).

### Add the Custom Reports gadget

1. Navigate to your **Dashboard Hub** app.
2. If you have not created a dashboard,

   1. Click the dashboard dropdown in the left corner or in the **Action** menu ([Blue elipse icon]) in the right corner, and select **Create dashboard.**
   2. Name your dashboard, and click **Create**.
   3. Click **Add Gadget.**
3. If you already have a dashboard created, click **Edit.**
4. Click **Add Gadget**.
5. Type **Custom Reports** in the search bar in the gadget catalog or navigate to the **Custom Reports** section in the left side navigation panel.
6. Locate the **Custom Reports** gadget, click **Add,** and click **Open Editor**.
7. Paste the xkcd comic template in the **Editor**. Find the template to display the xkcd comic in the [list of Custom Reports templates](/cms_trial/space/RDD/1568374878/Custom+Reports+templates/).
