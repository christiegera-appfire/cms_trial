# Display data from different JSM projects

All the Jira Service Management gadgets are multi-project, which means that you can report across your whole portfolio of projects.

With gadgets, you can know the workload of all your agents, see customer satisfaction across all support teams, and more:

- [Time to Resolution](https://help.roninpixels.com/RDD/Time-to-Resolution.99779462.html)
- [Time to First Response](https://help.roninpixels.com/RDD/Time-to-First-Response.99779657.html)
- [Created vs Resolved requests](https://help.roninpixels.com/RDD/Created-vs-Resolved-Requests.99681587.html)
- [Agents' Workload](https://help.roninpixels.com/RDD/Agents'-Workload.99681450.html)
- [Customer Satisfaction](https://help.roninpixels.com/RDD/Customer-Satisfaction.99779752.html)
- [SLA Accomplishment](https://help.roninpixels.com/RDD/SLA-Accomplishment.136347649.html).
