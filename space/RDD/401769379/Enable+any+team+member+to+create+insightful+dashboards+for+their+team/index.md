# Enable any team member to create insightful dashboards for their team

With an intuitive interface and straightforward configuration, anyone can create powerful charts and metrics for their team. No need for data analysts/devs.

Learn more about what [dashboards](https://appfire.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/RDD/pages/146309952) and [gadgets](https://appfire.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/RDD/pages/146309955) are in Dashboard Hub.
