# Agile Dev for monday.com template

## Overview

A dashboard for an agile dev team improves communication, tracking progress, trends identification, decision-making, leading to a more efficient and successful project:

1. **Communication**: Quickly and easily see the progress of sprints, as well as the overall project, which increases transparency and communication within the team.
2. **Tracking progress**: The burndown chart and burnup gadgets help the team track their progress towards completing the sprint goals and deliverables, and stay on track, identifying potential issues.
3. **Identifying trends**: The velocity chart and cumulative flow diagram gadgets help identifying trends and patterns.
4. **Facilitating decision-making**: A clear and up-to-date view of the team's progress and challenges, ease making informed decisions about the direction of the project.
