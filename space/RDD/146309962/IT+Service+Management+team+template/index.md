# IT Service Management team template

## Overview

Information Technology Service Management (ITSM) refers to all the activities that organizations perform to offer IT support and services to customers and employees. Software solutions have to ensure that all the involved teams work together as efficiently as possible and ideally, provide a complete end-to-end experience.

This dashboard provides a set of gadgets to facilitate the spotting of business changes, so the ITSM team can deliver an amazing employee and customer experience.

![Dashboard Hub IT Service Management team template template preview](/cms_trial/assets/b61fa858-db0e-4b69-8d91-b871e4e3cb56.jpg)

From rapid detection of requests' debt (how quickly your request is created vs. resolved) or SLA radiators to customer satisfaction or key services status, agents will collaborate and respond better and quicker, thanks to easier adaptation to daily changes and challenges.

See [DevOps software team template](/cms_trial/space/RDD/146309250/DevOps+software+team+template/) for more on how to integrate Ops teams in your flow of work.
