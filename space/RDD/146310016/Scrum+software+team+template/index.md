# Scrum software team template

## Overview

This dashboard comes with a comprehensive set of gadgets to help your Scrum team progress during sprints and keep all relevant information clearly visible.

![Dashboard Hub Jira dash Black 1.1](/cms_trial/assets/b5322a02-8c6a-44ba-b2b9-8a14a2b4f71c.png)

Not only can you visualize all the relevant pending work like the sprint blockers, but also compare how you are doing this sprint *vs* the previous one, who’s the best team member to assign your PR to, or keep track of the sprint goals

## Gadgets
