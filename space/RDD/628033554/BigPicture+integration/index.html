BigPicture integration

2 min read

What is the integration with BigPicture?

The Dashboard Hub Pro and BigPicture integration brings the powerful BigPicture project portfolio for data management into the Dashboard Hub centralized and shareable dashboards.

Create custom visual charts with your project data and generate centralized dashboards to grant stakeholders and managers a unified view of multiple projects, progress, resource utilization, and overall status.

How does it work?

You can use the BigPicture Custom Charts and BigPicture OKRs gadgets in your dashboards and shape the portfolio management data visualization according to your unique needs.

BigPicture gadgets are supported with BigPicture Standard.
The OKRs gadget is supported only with BigPicture Advanced. The OKR module is not available in the standard version of BigPicture

What problems does it solve?

It expands data visualization over BigPicture built-in reports, allowing more customization, flexibility, and shareability in your reporting process.

With this integration, what can I do now that I couldn’t before?

You can:

  • Create custom BigPicture charts and dashboards.

  • Report on your BigPicture OKRs directly in a dashboard.

  • Share dashboards externally with password-protected links, in seconds.

  • Combine and integrate different data sources, instances, and products into the same dashboard, creating a single reference point for their team. By having all the data in the same place, users can stop jumping from one tool to another. Teams migrating can display data from different instances to ease their migration journey.

  • Thanks to the Dashboard Hub family of apps, you can access the BigPicture metrics from Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket, or even monday.com.

  • Leverage all of Dashboard Hub’s powerful features:

    • Advanced restrictions/permissions on charts and dashboards

    • Dynamic filtering

    • Create custom charts and segments with JQL and formulas

    • Use of templates

    • Nearly 100 prebuilt Jira metrics, and more

How does it help work to flow?

When using Dashboard Hub to visualize and report on BigPicture data, you gain better visualization and control over key insights.

What is the most important feature everyone should know about this integration?

Besides improving visualization and simplifying the reporting of BigPicture data, Dashboard Hub becomes the central data hub where teams align and coordinate, saving teams from jumping through different tools to fetch data.

This is achieved by leveraging Dashboard Hub product integrations and creating unified and centralized dashboards where teams can check all their relevant data across products at a glance.
