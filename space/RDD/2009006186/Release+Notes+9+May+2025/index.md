## New gadget: Status Transition Count

Monitor your workflow more effectively by tracking how frequently issues transition into, out of, or between specific statuses over selected dates. Quickly identify bottlenecks, spot repeated or unnecessary transitions, improve forecasting accuracy, and optimize SLA performance. Use historical transition data to streamline your workflow and enhance productivity.

Read more details of this new gadget here: [Status Transition Count](/cms_trial/space/RDD/1930461204/Status+Transition+Count/)

![Dashboard Hub Status transistion count 2](/cms_trial/assets/bbd013ed-adab-4ee2-9d7c-17c466bf61a9.png)

## New gadget: Issues Status Entry by Date

Gain deeper insights into your workflow by tracking how many issues enter specific statuses or grouped statuses over time. Quickly identify trends, bottlenecks, or shifts in your workflow.

Read more details of this new gadget here: [Work Items Entering Status by Date](/cms_trial/space/RDD/1914273796/Work+Items+Entering+Status+by+Date/)

![Dashboard Hub issues entering status by date](/cms_trial/assets/f6ff5def-bc01-422c-b78a-300ca6d9a7fd.png)

## Restrict Datasource Sharing

We’ve added new settings to help admins enhance datasource privacy. When enabled, users won’t be able to grant permissions to others, making all datasources private and accessible only by their creators. [Learn more about datasource permissions](/cms_trial/space/RDD/1899429890/Manage+permissions+on+datasources/).

## Bug fixes

The following bugs are fixed in this release:

- The Service Desk gadget shows ids instead of values of Asset objects
- A different user language is wrongly used in some cases
- Error rendering the Scrum Velocity gadget
- Bad request in the Time to SLA integration
- Some gadgets cannot be exported to csv/xlsx
- Opsgenie changes in APIs
- AQL Search gadget values are not sorted
- Pie chart mismatch between legend issue count and preview
- Add datasource disabled in DH for monday.com
- Transition Time from Status to Status returns and error when saving in DC
- Content filtering active in the Customer Portal don’t display Time in Status data
