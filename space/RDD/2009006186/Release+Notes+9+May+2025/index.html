Release Notes 9 May 2025

3 min read

Release date: May 9, 2025

Our team is thrilled to announce the latest release of the Dashboard Hub family of apps for Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket and monday.com.

[unmapped inline: placeholder]

New features

Custom Reports: Global Catalog beta

Catalog of Custom Reports templates! Accelerate your first steps in Custom Reports with our new global catalog of templates, curated and maintained by Appfire. Quickly access ready-to-use examples, from general use cases like chart creation, to integrations with popular products like GitHub, specialized templates for Atlassian apps (Jira, Confluence, Compass, etc.), and templates tailored for other Appfire apps. Read more details about the different templates here: Custom Reports templates.

We have also improve how we communicate the errors when creating custom reports. Thanks to all customers and partners for sending your feedback, please, keep contacting us 🤟

New gadget: Status Transition Count

Monitor your workflow more effectively by tracking how frequently issues transition into, out of, or between specific statuses over selected dates. Quickly identify bottlenecks, spot repeated or unnecessary transitions, improve forecasting accuracy, and optimize SLA performance. Use historical transition data to streamline your workflow and enhance productivity.

Read more details of this new gadget here: Status Transition Count

New gadget: Issues Status Entry by Date

Gain deeper insights into your workflow by tracking how many issues enter specific statuses or grouped statuses over time. Quickly identify trends, bottlenecks, or shifts in your workflow.

Read more details of this new gadget here: Work Items Entering Status by Date

Restrict Datasource Sharing

We’ve added new settings to help admins enhance datasource privacy. When enabled, users won’t be able to grant permissions to others, making all datasources private and accessible only by their creators. Learn more about datasource permissions.

Enhancements

Performance Improvements

  • Better memory handling in DC

  • Reduce rerenders of some previews

Misc

  • Public dashboards now offer: Download, refresh dashboard, full screen and dark/light modes.

  • Better memory handling in DC

  • More descriptive messages to users in the Customer Portal when access is forbidden

  • Assets field in textual format and link to the object

Bug fixes

The following bugs are fixed in this release:

  • The Service Desk gadget shows ids instead of values of Asset objects

  • A different user language is wrongly used in some cases

  • Error rendering the Scrum Velocity gadget

  • Bad request in the Time to SLA integration

  • Some gadgets cannot be exported to csv/xlsx

  • Opsgenie changes in APIs

  • AQL Search gadget values are not sorted

  • Pie chart mismatch between legend issue count and preview

  • Add datasource disabled in DH for monday.com

  • Transition Time from Status to Status returns and error when saving in DC

  • Content filtering active in the Customer Portal don’t display Time in Status data
