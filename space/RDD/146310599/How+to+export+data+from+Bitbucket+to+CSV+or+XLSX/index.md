# How to export data from Bitbucket to CSV or XLSX

Dashboard Hub can export data from most of the gadgets to CSV or XLSX; all can be exported to PNG. See[Export gadget data to CSV, XLSX and PNG](/cms_trial/space/RDD/146310658/Export+gadget+data+to+CSV%2C+XLSX+and+PNG/).

This is especially useful when it comes to

Bitbucket compatible gadgets

The reason is due to the inability to export data from the Bitbucket interface itself. You can export all your Bitbucket data: Pipelines, commits, pull requests, failed and successful deployments, and team member workload.

![Dashboard Hub Open Pull Requests export menu](/cms_trial/assets/25501f25-f801-4b01-95eb-1bc02ab9306b.jpg)

You can use that data in CSV or XLSX to create nice reports, process it with your corporate business intelligence (BI) tools, integrate with other systems, keep it for your own records, or present it in external audits.

![Dashboard Hub Bitbucket CSV and XLSX export options](/cms_trial/assets/5ddacb4a-cd43-4036-90ef-db0e22319efb.png)
