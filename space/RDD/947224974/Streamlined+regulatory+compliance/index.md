# Streamlined regulatory compliance

## Overview

Combine [Comala Document Management](https://appfire.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/CDML) and Dashboard Hub for Confluence to maintain stringent regulatory compliance within the financial services sector. This solution automates critical document control processes, enforces compliance through tailored workflows, and provides executives with real-time visibility into compliance status across multiple projects. Simplify adherence to complex regulations such as SOX, GDPR, and others by integrating robust document management with dynamic, customizable dashboards that track compliance metrics, audit trails, and approval processes.

## Benefits for corporate customers in financial services

1. **Automated compliance workflows**: Automate the creation, review, and approval of compliance-related documents. Set up customized workflows that incorporate regulatory requirements directly into document handling processes, ensuring all documentation is compliant with financial regulations. Use Comala Document Management to enforce necessary review cycles, e-signatures, and final approvals that are traceable and auditable.
2. **Real-Time compliance monitoring**: Leverage Dashboard Hub to create comprehensive dashboards that provide real-time insights into the compliance status of all documents and projects. Use custom charts to monitor key compliance metrics, such as document approval times, pending reviews, and compliance breach alerts, ensuring that any potential issues are immediately visible and actionable.
3. **Enhanced audit readiness**: Prepare for audits with ease by maintaining a complete, searchable archive of all compliance-related activities and documentation. With Comala Document Management, every change, approval, and review is logged and traceable. Dashboard Hub enhances this by allowing for quick generation of reports and dashboards that can be used to demonstrate compliance to auditors or regulatory bodies.

This use case effectively demonstrates how Comala Document Management and Dashboard Hub for Confluence can be leveraged in a high-stakes environment like financial services to ensure rigorous compliance, enhance operational visibility, and maintain audit readiness.
