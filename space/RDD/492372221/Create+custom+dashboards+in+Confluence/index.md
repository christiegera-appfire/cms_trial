# Create custom dashboards in Confluence

Dashboard Hub provides pre-defined templates, 12+ product integrations, and over 60 powerful metrics that can be combined to visualize advanced data and create insightful, easy-to-share reports.

![Dashboard Hub custom Confluence dashboard with multiple gadgets and chart visualizations](/cms_trial/assets/770a6cba-5cd3-4c82-bbc3-4be755c23c44.png)

## Benefits of powerful Confluence dashboards

- **Effortless Dashboard Creation**:Pre-defined templates and a wide range of nearly 100 advanced metrics and 12+ product integrations make it easy to design visually appealing and data-rich dashboards.
- **Enhanced Collaboration and Communication**:Use secure links to share these powerful dashboards with stakeholders outside your Confluence environment. Foster collaboration with clients, partners, or other external parties, ensuring they have access to the information they need.
- **Seamless Document Integration:** The ability to embed these dynamic dashboards into Confluence documents with a macro enhances the quality and interactivity of your documents.

## How to start

1. Start creating a dashboard: Select a name, the permissions, and a pre-built template.
2. Configure the template gadgets. Compatible gadgets will configure automatically.
3. Tailor your perfect data view by adding gadgetsfrom the [collection of Dashboard Hub metrics](/cms_trial/space/RDD/146309915/Dashboard+gadgets+-+KPIs+and+metrics/).

   1. You can also [create customized charts and metrics](/cms_trial/space/RDD/401802479/Create+Jira+Custom+Charts/) with the JQL Custom Charts gadget.
4. If you want even more customization, you can move and resize gadgets, change colors, add a slideshow, and more. Also, make sure to review the dashboard permissions.
5. Your dashboard is ready; share it with your team using a [public link](/cms_trial/space/RDD/146310576/Share+a+dashboard+with+a+public+link/) so they can share your joy.

Is there a metric or product integration that your team is craving for but you can't find in Dashboard Hub? [Make a request/suggestion for our team](https://apps.appf.re/support), and we might add it to incoming releases.
