# Help and support

## Support

If you need assistance, want to make a feature request, or book a demo, you can contact our support team through the Appfire [support portal](https://appf.re/support).

You can also ask questions in the [Atlassian Community](https://community.atlassian.com/), where we and other Dashboard Hub users actively reply.
