# Release notes 29th June 2025

**Release date**: June 29, 2025

Our team is thrilled to announce the latest releases of Comala Document Management Cloud.

---

## New Feature

### New space admin action to bulk apply workflows

Space Administrators can now **Bulk Apply Workflows** to scan all pages and blog posts in a space, apply the appropriate workflow, and initialize their workflow status, making them visible in the Document Report.

Click [here](https://appfire.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/CDMCD/pages/614009715/Apply+a+workflow+to+all+documents+in+a+space#Bulk-apply-workflows) to learn more.

## Bug Fix

### Copied Pages Retain Workflow Status

Fixed an issue where copied or moved pages did not retain their original workflow status. Pages now retain their workflow state after being copied or relocated.
