# Apply workflows

**Comala Document Management** workflows can be applied at both the **space** and **page** levels:

- **Page workflows:** A *page editor* can apply a workflow to an individual page or blog post.
- **Space workflows:** A *space administrator* can apply a workflow across all pages and blog posts in a space. A space administrator can also activate multiple space workflows and use **content labels** to control how each workflow is applied to specific documents.
