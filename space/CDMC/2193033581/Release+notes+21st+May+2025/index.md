# Release notes 21st May 2025

**Release date**: May 21, 2025

Our team is thrilled to announce the latest releases of Comala Document Management Cloud.

---

## Bug fixes

- **Fixed selection issue in the workflow parameter dialog:** We resolved an issue in the **Update parameters** dialog under **Space settings** where users were unable to select users or groups if the list extended beyond the visible area of the dialog. This fix ensures that users can scroll and select large lists.
- Improved internal analytics tracking to support future enhancements.
