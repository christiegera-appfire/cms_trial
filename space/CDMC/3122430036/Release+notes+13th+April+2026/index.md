## Migration framework and compatibility enhancements

This release includes internal infrastructure updates to support our ongoing migration from the **Atlassian Connect** framework to **Atlassian Forge**.

These changes update the core components of the app's architecture to align with Forge's requirements, ensuring the app remains compatible, secure, and stable throughout the transition.

No action is required. This update runs entirely in the background and does not affect your current workflows or experience.
