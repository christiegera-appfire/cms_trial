## Bug fixes

### Optimized content initialization for large assignee lists

We’ve improved how content initializes when a page has a large number of assignees. This update reduces the payload size during initialization, preventing loading issues and ensuring that the *State Dialog* works as expected, even with high-volume assignee lists.
