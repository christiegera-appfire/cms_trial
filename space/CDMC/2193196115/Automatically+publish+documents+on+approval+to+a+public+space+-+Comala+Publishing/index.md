# Automatically publish documents on approval to a public space - Comala Publishing

Comala Publishing provides between-space publishing to manage your draft and published content. The app works seamlessly with Comala Document Management Cloud to automatically publish pages upon approval to your public space.

Automated workflows are added to your draft space pages to help you better manage the document lifecycle. Approved pages in the draft space are published automatically upon approval to your public space. This ensures that only approved content is made public.
