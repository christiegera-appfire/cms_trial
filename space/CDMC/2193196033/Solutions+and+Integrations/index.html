Solutions and Integrations

1 min read

The Comala Document Management app is generally compatible with most apps available on Confluence Cloud. If you encounter an app that is not compatible, we recommend contacting the app vendor and asking them to reach out to our support team for assistance.

Integrations

Comala Document Management for Cloud can be used with Confluence macros and several other Confluence apps to provide a comprehensive solution to a business need. The following are some useful apps that can be integrated with Comala Document Management.

Some apps require additional settings or customizations to achieve optimal results.
