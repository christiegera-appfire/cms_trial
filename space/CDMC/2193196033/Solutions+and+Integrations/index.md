# Solutions and Integrations

The Comala Document Management app is generally compatible with most apps available on Confluence Cloud. If you encounter an app that is not compatible, we recommend contacting the app vendor and asking them to reach out to our [support](http://appf.re/support) team for assistance.

## Integrations

Comala Document Management for Cloud can be used with Confluence macros and several other Confluence apps to provide a comprehensive solution to a business need. The following are some useful apps that can be integrated with Comala Document Management.

Some apps require additional settings or customizations to achieve optimal results.

| Vendor | Product | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Atlassian Confluence Cloud** | Confluence macros   - Chart macro - Filter by Label macro - Confluence Search - Page Properties macro - Page Properties Report macro - PDF and Word export | Use page properties macros with the [app report page macros](/cms_trial/space/CDMC/2192838469/Page+macros+for+reporting/). |
| **Appfire Comala** | [Comala Publishing](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/apps/143/comala-publishing?hosting=cloud&tab=overview) | Use Comala Publishing with Comala Document Management to publish a page to another space. |
| **Appfire** | [Reporting for Confluence](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/apps/186/reporting-for-confluence?hosting=cloud&tab=overview) | A reporting engine to help visualize and understand Confluence data |
| **Appfire** | [Scaffolding Forms and Templates](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/apps/190/scaffolding-forms-templates-for-confluence?hosting=cloud&tab=overview) | - Dynamic data forms to increase data dependability - Live templates to dynamically update pages - Metadata within the page content |
| **Appfire** | [Dashboard Hub](/cms_trial/space/CDMC/2534703573/Monitor+Comala+Document+Management+Cloud+Workflows+with+Dashboard+Hub+Custom+Reports/) | Integrate Dashboard Hub and Comala Document Management (CDM) cloud to view key metrics of your document workflows in Confluence. |
