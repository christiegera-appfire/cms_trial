## New features

### Introducing Global workflows

You can now manage workflows across multiple Confluence spaces from the new **Global Workflows dashboard**. Create, enable, disable, and remove workflows in one central location, ensuring consistent workflow definitions, rules, and states for content review and approval processes across the organization.
