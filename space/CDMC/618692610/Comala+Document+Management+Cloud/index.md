# Comala Document Management Cloud

![Comala Document Management Cloud product logo](/cms_trial/assets/fc12d95b-ad19-4805-a04a-ba2d80c29c51.png)

- **Comala Document Management Cloud** lets you manage your Confluence documents and automate your document lifecycle.
- Add simple or complex review and approval processes to your Confluence pages and spaces.
- Use flexible, robust reporting to track and report on changes, reviews, and approvals and support compliance.
