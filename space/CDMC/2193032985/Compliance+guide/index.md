# Compliance guide

Some clients have used Confluence and Comala Document Management to support compliance with various standards.

We provide the following guides; however, Appfire does not claim compliance or certification for our tools. While our tools offer technical features that can support compliance efforts, it is the user's responsibility to implement the necessary procedural and administrative controls.
