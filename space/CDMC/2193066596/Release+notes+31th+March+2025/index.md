# Release notes 31th March 2025

**Release date**: March 31, 2025

Our team is thrilled to announce the latest releases of Comala Document Management Cloud.

---

## Enhancements

### Live Editing Mode Support

The new *Live editing mode* feature in Confluence allows state transitions of a page while it is in this mode, which could cause conflicts with the expected transition process. To prevent this, byline actions are now disabled in *Live editing mode*, and a message explaining the restriction is displayed.

---

## Bug fixes

The following issue is fixed in this release:

### Workflow Retention Issue

Pages now retain their applied workflow and state, even after activating a workflow on a space.
