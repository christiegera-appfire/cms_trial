## Bug Fixes

### **Problem applying a workflow from the app Onboarding screen**

When trying to apply a workflow from the app [Onboarding](/cms_trial/space/CDMC/2192869918/Onboarding/), after choosing a space to add the workflow, the user was not redirected to the app space settings, where the chosen workflow should have automatically been added and made active, with a Pendo guide displayed. The issue is now fixed.
