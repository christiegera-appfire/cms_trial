# Assign to [last] role member post-functions do not support skipIfAssignee, useSelectedUser and forceSelectedUser options

## Problem

The post-functions Assign to last role member and Assign to role member don't support skipIfAssignee, useSelectedUser and forceSelectedUser options. Therefore, workflows using these options won't work as before.

## Solution

None. This is a limitation of JIRA Cloud.
