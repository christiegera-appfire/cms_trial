## Take control of your Jira fields like never before

<https://app.arcade.software/share/FkrMzflOX9C4VvzZXnQl>

**Live Fields for Jira Cloud** enables you to customize your Jira interface extensively! Using JavaScript or TypeScript, Live Fields enables you to execute scripts on several Jira screens; you can hide or show, enable or disable, automatically calculate or validate, and more!

- **Customize fields dynamically**   
  Get full control: show, hide, or make fields mandatory. Say goodbye to one-size-fits-all. Customize field behaviors to match your specific needs.
- **Reinforce business operations**   
  Ensure data integrity and consistency. Catch errors early and control field access to keep reports and processes reliable.
- **Improve the user experience**  
  Automate routine field actions and validations to speed up processes, reduce manual steps, and simplify Jira for everyone.
