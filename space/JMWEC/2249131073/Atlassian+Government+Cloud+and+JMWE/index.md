# Atlassian Government Cloud and JMWE

**JMWE for Jira Cloud** is compatible with Atlassian Government Cloud. Atlassian Government Cloud is a separate cloud instance of their major products targeted at the FedRAMP Moderate market, which consists mostly of federal agencies. FedRAMP Moderate requires additional security controls, auditing processes, and compliance requirements beyond what is provided by the standard Commercial Cloud products.

For detailed information on AGC, see this page: [Atlassian Government Cloud](https://www.atlassian.com/government/cloud).
