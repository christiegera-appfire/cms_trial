# Installing or Uninstalling on Jira Cloud

## Install the app

To install the app, follow these steps:

1. Log in to your Jira Cloud instance as an administrator.
2. Click the **Settings** icon ⚙️ in the upper right corner and select **Apps**.
3. Search for ‘JMWE’ and select **Jira Misc Workflow Extensions (JMWE)**.
4. Click **Try it free**.
5. Click **Accept & Install** to confirm the app installation.
6. The system displays a progress message for the operation.

Click **Get Started** to go to the Welcome page for the app.

**Important**

After successfully installing the app, we recommend that you [verify your Jira instance configuration](#) to make sure all JMWE features will be able to work properly.

## Re-install the app

To uninstall and then re-install the app, follow these steps (don't skip *any*):

1. Log in to your Jira Cloud instance as an administrator.
2. Click the **Settings** icon ⚙️ in the upper right corner and select **Apps**.
3. Click **Manage apps** and locate **Jira Misc Workflow Extensions**.
4. Click the app to display its details view.
5. Click **Unsubscribe**or**Stop Trial**(depending on your subscription status).
6. Click **Uninstall.**
7. The system displays a progress message for the operation. Once finished, the app is removed from the **Manage apps** page.
8. Click **Find new apps** and search for ‘JMWE’.
9. Click the app to display its details view.
10. Click **Subscribe.**
11. Click **Accept & Install** to confirm the app installation.
12. The system displays a progress message for the operation. Once finished, the app is reinstalled.

Click on **Get Started** to go to the Welcome page for the app.

- Reinstalling the app will not modify your configured workflows. However, while the app is inactive, Jira might start failing transitions that have JMWE post-functions configured, so you want to minimize the time between uninstallation and re-installation of the app.
- Unsubscribing and then re-subscribing to the app will not impact billing, as long as you unsubscribe and then re-subscribe on the same day.

## During the trial

After installing/enabling the add-on, your 30-day trial period begins.

If you terminate the trial within the 30-day period, you can re-subscribe to it within the original trial period. You cannot restart the trial beyond the original trial period; at that point, you will only be able to subscribe.

If you do not terminate the trial within the 30-day period, a subscription for the add-on begins, and your billing cycle starts. Canceling the add-on takes effect immediately, and you will not be refunded. If you'd like to completely remove the add-on, disable it. See [Uninstalling or Disabling the Add-On](https://appfire.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/JMWEC/pages/466321985) for more information.

## Uninstall the app

To uninstall the app, follow these steps:

1. Log in to your Jira Cloud instance as an administrator.
2. Click the **Settings** icon ⚙️ in the upper right corner of the screen and select **Apps**.
3. Select **Manage apps** and locate **Jira Misc Workflow Extensions**.
4. Click the app to display its details.
5. Click **Unsubscribe** or **Stop Trial** (depending on your subscription status).
6. Click **Uninstall.**
7. The system displays a progress message for the operation. Once finished, the app is removed from Jira and will not display under **Manage apps**.

You have uninstalled the *Jira Misc Workflow Extensions*app.

## Disable the app

To disable the app, follow these steps:

1. Log in to your Jira Cloud instance as an administrator.
2. Click the **Settings** icon ⚙️ in the upper right corner of the screen and select **Apps**.
3. Select **Manage apps** and locate **Jira Misc Workflow Extensions**.
4. Click the app to display its details view.
5. Click **Disable**.
6. The system displays a progress message for the operation. Once finished, the **Disable**button is replaced with **Enable**.

You have disabled the *Jira Misc Workflow Extensions*app.
