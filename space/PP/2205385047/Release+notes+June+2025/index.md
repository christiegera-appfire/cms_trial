# Release notes June 2025

**Release date**: June 25, 2025

Our team is thrilled to announce the latest release of Planning Poker for Jira Cloud.

---

## New features

### Block editing of personal estimates after reveal

We’ve added a new game setting that allows moderators to prevent participants from editing their personal estimates after the cards are revealed.

As a moderator, you can now disable vote editing to follow the classic Planning Poker workflow with structured re-estimation rounds, instead of allowing post-reveal changes. You’ll find this option under the **Basic settings** panel when creating or editing a game.
