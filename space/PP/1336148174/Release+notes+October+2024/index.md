# Release notes October 2024

**Release date**: October 1, 2024

Our team is thrilled to announce the latest release of Planning Poker for Jira Cloud.

---

## Enhancements

Appfire is excited to introduce the following updates to Planning Poker for Jira Cloud.

### Improved ticket view

A new toggle is now available, allowing you to hide tickets that have already been estimated. This gives you a cleaner view, focusing only on tickets that still require estimation.

![image-20241001-101733.png](/cms_trial/assets/73c13fac-23e8-4951-a7a6-d9e3e1367687.png)

### Game cloning with admins and estimators

When cloning a finished game, admins and estimators are now automatically populated. This enhancement ensures you don’t have to manually assign permissions, streamlining the process for managing the cloned game.

![image-20241001-102121.png](/cms_trial/assets/5e877b59-dcfe-4e1f-ac79-5cc09da9942d.png)

### Average score display

Moderators can now view the average score directly in the estimation view. This feature eliminates the need for manual calculations, making it easier and faster to pick the final estimate.

![image-20241001-102348.png](/cms_trial/assets/e15a47f2-cec5-4e4e-9035-e4734b65e8e4.png)

### Avatars with tooltips

To make it easier to identify who voted, a tooltip now appears when hovering over avatars, giving you quick insights during the voting process.

![contentId-1336148174](/cms_trial/assets/cd1f3fbe-cc7e-4212-9087-f32b97e3b569.png)
