## **New features**

### JIRA CLOUD New guided onboarding experience

This release replaces the old setup flow with a guided, step-by-step configuration stepper that gets you from zero to a working integration between Jira and Salesforce. New onboarding guides you through creating a connection between Salesforce and Jira, configuring settings, and creating a binding for your Jira space with field mappings. For details, see [Set up your integration.](/cms_trial/space/CSFJIRA/1873412559/Set+up+your+integration/)

- **Improved entity and field mapping view**   
  A cleaner, visual side-by-side layout for mapping Jira and Salesforce entities, fields, and values. For details, see [Configure entity and field mappings from scratch](/cms_trial/space/CSFJIRA/3664152584/Configure+entity+and+field+mappings+from+scratch/).
- **Entity mapping templates**

  Setting up entity and field mappings between Jira and Salesforce is now faster with ready-made templates. Select from four templates based on your team's workflow:

  - Bug escalation: track unresolved bugs and bring Salesforce cases into Jira for your engineering team.
  - Feature requests: send customer feature requests from Salesforce into Jira and track them through development.
  - Deal enablement: connect Salesforce opportunities to Jira work items.
  - Deal and feature sync: align sales, product, and engineering by syncing Salesforce opportunities and customer requests with Jira.  
    For details, see [Configure entity and field mappings with templates](/cms_trial/space/CSFJIRA/3664152459/Configure+entity+and+field+mappings+from+templates/).
- **Configuration testing after setup**

  You can now test your configuration before completing the setup. A dedicated test step at the end of the setup process lets you associate a Jira work item with a Salesforce record and verify that your bindings, mappings, and sync behavior work as expected.  
  For details, see [Associate Jira work items with Salesforce records from Jira](/cms_trial/space/CSFJIRA/3663725288/Associate+Jira+work+items+with+Salesforce+records+from+Jira/).
