# How to optimize the association JQL querying?

If you are managing projects with large numbers of Jira issues, enabling the query optimization for Salesforce and Jira association JQL will help fine-tune the results and improve the speed of performance in retrieving the information.

Go to **Apps**> **Salesforce** > **Connections** > **Configure** and scroll down to the **Query Optimization** section and enable the toggle.
