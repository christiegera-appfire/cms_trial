## JIRA CLOUD Enhancements

## Salesforce bindings

### Improved binding list navigation and visibility

Enhanced the bindings list with a search field that lets you quickly find bindings by Jira space or Salesforce connection name. The page header and controls now remain visible while scrolling, making it easier to navigate long lists.

When filtering for invalid bindings, status badges help you identify missing Jira spaces and invalid mappings without opening individual bindings.

## SALESFORCE Bug fixes

- Fixed an issue where re-importing associations from Jira Data Center to Jira Cloud could fail due to duplicate Salesforce record IDs. The migration process now handles associations correctly, preventing duplicate entries and allowing existing associations to be merged successfully during re-import.
