# Get started with Connector for Salesforce & Jira

This page walks you through everything you need to get the Connector for Salesforce up and running, from checking prerequisites to configuring both sides of the integration. Follow the steps to ensure a smooth and complete setup. You need to have administrator rights on Jira and Salesforce to complete the setup.

## Check prerequisites

Verify compatibility and make sure your Jira and Salesforce environments meet the required versions and permissions before installing Connectof for Salesforce & Jira.
