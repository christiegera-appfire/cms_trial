# Overview

Use **Connector for Salesforce & Jira** to integrate Salesforce and Jira! Connector for Salesforce & Jira eliminates the gap between your sales and engineering teams by automatically keeping Salesforce records and Jira work items in sync.

- Use the automatic bidirectional synchronization feature to ensure that all issues are up to date, consolidated, and easily viewable.
- Create Jira work items from Salesforce — or Salesforce records from Jira — without leaving the platform.
- Track the progress of work items and ensure they’re linked to the correct Salesforce object.
- Generate Jira work items from any Salesforce record.
- Customize data mapping to fit your organization’s needs.

You bought Jira to deliver the products and services your customers love as efficiently as possible. You bought Salesforce to simplify customer relationship management and scale revenue. But it all breaks down when you try to use Salesforce and Jira together.

Your customer acquisition and retention strategy relies on up-to-date and transparent information about your prospects and clients. Close the gap between the products you love to increase productivity and transparency across teams. The Connector for Salesforce and Jira helps you streamline workflows, improve data accuracy, bring visibility to all your account interactions and status changes in real time.

Here's how:

- Remove team silos, allow teams to work with up-to-date and error-free data. Easily escalate the work to another team with a single click. Create Jira issues from Salesforce and vice versa without leaving your platform of choice.
