# Configure field displays and access the Details screen

Learn how to add Salesforce fields to existing objects and customize what Salesforce fields appear in the **Details** screen, found on the Jira Issue screen.

It also guides you on how to access the **Details** screen.

## Add a Salesforce field to an existing Object

1. Select **Apps** from the left sidebar in Jira.
2. Next to *Connector for Salesforce,* click **Menu** (▢) > **App settings**.

   ![App settings](/cms_trial/assets/4e12d48f-1928-4d7d-b055-113c3077bd0b.png)
3. Under *Connector for Salesforce*, select **Connections**.
4. On the **Salesforce Connections** screen, navigate to the **Connection** you want to configure and select **Configure.**
5. On the **Connection Configuration** screen, navigate to the Salesforce Object desired and click **Fields** to continue configuration.

   ![contentId-1873511110](/cms_trial/assets/40f8b4c7-8f83-451f-a53b-52bb7210ee01.png)
6. In the **Salesforce Field** search box, enter the **Salesforce Field** you want to add, and click **Add**.

   ![contentId-1873511110](/cms_trial/assets/37533a34-b01e-4944-ac4d-6d114cae6ab3.png)
7. Click **Apply Changes** when all **Salesforce fields** are added.

   ![Apply changes](/cms_trial/assets/faae6acd-0753-424a-bff1-63264c2a0a15.png)

   When the changes have been applied successfully, the Salesforce Fields you just added are visible on the **Details** screen.

## Access the Details screen

1. Go to a Jira work item that has already been [associated with a Salesforce record](/cms_trial/space/CSFJIRA/3092284698/Associate+a+Jira+work+item+from+Salesforce+with+Jira+Issues+(NextGen)/).
2. On the right side of the screen, in the *Connector for Salesforce* section, click the associated Salesforce record:

   ![Case in Jira work item](/cms_trial/assets/18e1e82c-bd3d-4e7c-a50f-954ad9ee4655.png)
3. Click **Details**.

   ![Details.png](/cms_trial/assets/1ca17e83-17f3-4235-b644-268f3813a001.png)

    The **Details** screen appears.

   ![Details view](/cms_trial/assets/a9907df6-d2e8-4e63-8124-69298add068f.png)
