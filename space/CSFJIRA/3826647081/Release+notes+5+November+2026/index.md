## JIRA CLOUD Enhancements

## Salesforce connections

### Improved Salesforce connection health monitoring

Connector for Salesforce & Jira now checks Salesforce connection health on the Connections page. Administrators can identify connections with authentication problems, view available failure reasons, and see when each connection was last used, so they can reuse idle connections before they expire. Each connection is checked individually.

## SALESFORCE Bug fixes

- Fixed an issue where the **Auto pull** and **Auto push** settings in the *Create Jira Issue* window could appear disabled after saving, even though the changes were saved successfully. The panel now displays the correct synchronization settings when reopened.
