## **Enhancements**

### SALESFORCE Getting started page update

We've refreshed the getting started experience on the Salesforce side. It now opens with a quick question: whether the Connector is already installed in Jira, and tailors the setup steps to your answer.

We've also reorganized the stepper into stages. Each stage includes a short description and a direct link to learn more or take an action.
