# Search Salesforce Object from Jira

Connector for Salesforce & Jira allows you to search Salesforce records from within Jira.

The search results can be used with the connector's [create issue](/cms_trial/space/CSFJIRA/3091792703/Create+a+Jira+work+item+from+Salesforce/) and [associate issue](/cms_trial/space/CSFJIRA/3091301556/Associate+a+Jira+Issue+from+Salesforce/) features.

1. From your Jira instance, click **Apps** > **Connector for Salesforce**. When the *Connector for Salesforce* menu opens on the left side of the page, click **Search Salesforce**.

   ![connector-search-sf.png](/cms_trial/assets/e0bc0852-5d97-4139-965f-66bbd0637a13.png)
2. The **Search Salesforce** window appears. In this example search, the **Salesforce Object** type is *Case*, the **Fields to Search** is *Name Fields*, and the **Search Query** is *sync*.

   ![contentId-1962541918](/cms_trial/assets/20461046-41e3-45e8-b4c6-134a01a47129.png)
3. Click **Search** to continue. A successful search displays the results lower on the page.

   ![contentId-1962541918](/cms_trial/assets/12fde5c2-4215-4a63-bc9a-5e7400e40d3b.png)
4. Click **Create issue** or **Associate issue** for the result under the **Operations** column on the right. For more details about these features, refer to the related pages below.

   ![contentId-1962541918](/cms_trial/assets/7d07c719-c40b-443e-b9ec-39c46e235bbf.png)
