## Jira Cloud Migration to Forge

At Appfire, we’re committed to maintaining the highest standards of security, reliability, and performance across our solutions. As part of this commitment, Connector for Salesforce & Jira Cloud has been developed and deployed on Atlassian Forge, Atlassian’s most advanced cloud development platform.

Moving to Forge ensures your data is protected within Atlassian’s trusted infrastructure, leveraging its built-in security, compliance, and scalability. By embracing Forge, Appfire continues to deliver on its promise to offer secure, enterprise-grade apps that evolve with Atlassian’s platform.

**Rollout schedule**

**Connect End of Support**:

- Atlassian has [announced](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/developer/announcing-connect-end-of-support-timeline-and-next-steps) the end of support for the Connect framework by the end of 2026.

- Please update to the latest version by the end of 2026 to keep your app running. If you miss the deadline, your data can be permanently lost.

### Upgrade to Forge manually

You can also upgrade to Forge manually. To upgrade the app to the Forge version, you need Jira administrative privileges.

1. Navigate to **Apps** > **Manage apps**.
2. Under *Connected apps,* the Connector for Salesforce & Jira app displays an **Update** label. Click **View app details**.
3. To upgrade the app, click **Accept & update**.
4. The *Confirm app update* dialog opens. Click **Update**.

Contact your network administrator and share this page, or reach out to our [support](https://support.appfire.com/page/support) for any further assistance.

## Jira Cloud DC to Cloud migration updates

This release requires you to use the latest Jira DC version to start any new migrations. If you attempt to start a migration on a previous version, it will not work. Any migrations you already have in progress will continue to run and complete as expected.

This release improves the reliability and transparency of the migration process from Connector for Salesforce & Jira (Data Center) to Connector for Salesforce & Jira (Cloud). The progress stop step has been removed; migration now runs continuously to completion.

For more details on migration steps, see [Migrate Connector for Salesforce & Jira Data Center to Cloud](/cms_trial/space/CSFJIRA/2161049701/Migrate+Connector+for+Salesforce+%26+Jira+Data+Center+to+Cloud/).
