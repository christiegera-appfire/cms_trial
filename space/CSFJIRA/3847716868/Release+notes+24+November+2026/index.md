## JIRA CLOUD Enhancements

## Support data residency migration

Connector for Salesforce & Jira now supports migrating existing installations from one data residency location to another. Previously, you could only set the location for new installations.

If you already have the Connector installed, organization admins can now move its data to a different location (Global, US, or EU) from Atlassian Administration. During the move, your data is exported, imported, validated, and then switched to the new location. If anything fails, the migration is rolled back, and your data stays in the original location.

Learn more: [Data residency for Connector for Salesforce & Jira](/cms_trial/space/CSFJIRA/2258305117/Data+residency+and+realm+persistence/)
