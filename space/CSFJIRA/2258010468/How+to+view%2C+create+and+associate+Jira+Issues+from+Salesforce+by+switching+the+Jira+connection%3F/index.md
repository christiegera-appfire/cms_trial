# How to view, create and associate Jira Issues from Salesforce by switching the Jira connection?

This feature is only supported in LWC (Lightning Web Component).

## Viewing Jira Issues in Salesforce

1. In a Salesforce object, you will see a dropdown option to choose the Jira site connection. Choose the Jira connection of your choice.   
     

   [Unmapped macro: inline-media-image — no content to fall back on]

The dropdown will not show if the Salesforce instance has only one Jira connection. To check the connections, read [How do I connect more than one Jira connection in my Salesforce environment?](/cms_trial/space/CSFJIRA/3091827403/How+do+I+connect+more+than+one+Jira+connection+in+my+Salesforce+environment%3F/).

1. Switching the connection will switch the content within the panel. A tick symbol indicates which Jira connection issues you are currently viewing. 

   ![contentId-2258010468](/cms_trial/assets/7e6b11a4-cac0-4880-a84f-d984c64456cd.png)

## Creating and associating Jira Issues in Salesforce

1. In the Salesforce object, click the **Associate/Create**button.
2. Click the **Select a Connection** dropdown to choose your Jira connection.   
     

   [Unmapped macro: inline-media-image — no content to fall back on]
3. To associate a Jira issue from Salesforce, read our [Associating a Jira Issue from Salesforce with Jira Issues (NextGen)](/cms_trial/space/CSFJIRA/3092284698/Associate+a+Jira+work+item+from+Salesforce+with+Jira+Issues+(NextGen)/) documentation.
4. To create a Jira issue from Salesforce, read our [Creating a Jira Issue from Salesforce with Jira Issues (NextGen)](https://appfire.atlassian.net/wiki/pages/createpage.action?spaceKey=csfjira&title=Create%20a%20Jira%20Issue%20from%20Salesforce%20with%20Jira%20Issues%20%28NextGen%29&linkCreation=true&fromPageId=2258010468) documentation.
5. The created and associated Jira Issue will appear in the relevant Jira connection panel that you can view in the Salesforce object page.
