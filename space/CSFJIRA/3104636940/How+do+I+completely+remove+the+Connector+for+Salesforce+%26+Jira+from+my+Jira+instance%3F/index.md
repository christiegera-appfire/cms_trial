# How do I completely remove the Connector for Salesforce & Jira from my Jira instance?

## Question:

How do I completely remove the Connector for Salesforce & Jira from my Jira instance?

## Answer:

Here's how to completely remove Connector for Salesforce & Jira from your Jira instance:

1. Log into your Jira.
2. Click the Jira icon in the top left (

   ,

   [Unmapped macro: inline-media-image — no content to fall back on]

   ,

   [Unmapped macro: inline-media-image — no content to fall back on]

   , or

   [Unmapped macro: inline-media-image — no content to fall back on]

   ) > **Settings** > **Add-ons**> **Manage add-ons**.
3. Locate Connector for Salesforce & Jiraunder **User-installed add-ons**.
4. Click **Stop trial**. A dialog box will ask you to confirm.  
   Click **Unsubscribe**.
5. Now click **Uninstall.**
