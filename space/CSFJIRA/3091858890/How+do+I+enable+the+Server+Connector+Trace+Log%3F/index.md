# How do I enable the Server Connector Trace Log?

1. Click the **Cog Icon > System.**
2. Under **Troubleshooting And Support** in the sidebar, click on**Logging & Profiling.**
3. Scroll down to **Default Logger,** then click **Configure.**
4. In the **Package Name**text entry box, type "*com.servicerocket*".
5. Under **Logging Level,** choose **TRACE**.
