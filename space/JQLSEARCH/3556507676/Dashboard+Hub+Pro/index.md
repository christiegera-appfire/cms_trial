# Dashboard Hub Pro

The use cases on this page provide examples of using JQL Search Extensions with Dashboard Hub, a leading Appfire reporting and dashboard solution.

Dashboard Hub is an all-in-one Jira dashboard app for building reports that keep teams informed. Nearly every gadget can be driven by a JQL query, so any filter, keyword, or function you use from JQL Search Extensions (JSE) works inside a Dashboard Hub gadget with no extra setup. Combining Dashboard Hub with JQL Search Extensions means you can report on relationships, comments, and content that standard Jira can’t surface.

## Why use JQL Search Extensions and Dashboard Hub together

**Report on more of your data**: JSE can retrieve work items by their links, comments, attachments, and hierarchy. Feeding those queries or filters into a gadget lets you chart data that native Jira gadgets simply can’t reach.

**One filter, many views**: Save an extended search once and reuse it across pie charts, tables, and stats. Every gadget refresh re-runs the full query, so your dashboards stay current on their own.

**Clearer picture for the whole team**: Turning complex searches into simple visuals gives team leads and stakeholders an at-a-glance view of blockers, progress, and workload without writing a single query themselves.

**Faster answers, less effort**: Describe what you want to find in plain language with JSE’s AI search, save it as a filter, and drop it into a gadget. There’s no development work and no manual data pulling.

### What scenarios benefit from this integration

Our gadgets serve an extensive range of industries and teams. Here are a few examples where our customers are implementing the real-life use cases presented in this section:

- **Software development**: Dashboards can visualize blockers and linked-work item dependencies across Epics, Stories, and Subtasks, keeping engineering, QA, and product teams aligned on what’s holding up delivery.
- **Agile teams**: Scrum Masters and product owners get live charts of sprint readiness and progress across every epic, without rebuilding reports each sprint.
- **Customer support and success teams**: Filters that track who last commented let support leads chart response coverage and spot issues awaiting a reply.
- **Project and program management**: Interconnected workstreams become easy to monitor when linked-work item filters feed a single dashboard, reducing the risk of overlooked dependencies.
- **Operations and compliance**: Filters that search attachment content surface work items tied to specific documents or clauses, giving teams a shared, always-current view for audits and reviews.
