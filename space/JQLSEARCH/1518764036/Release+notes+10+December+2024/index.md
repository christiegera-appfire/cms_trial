## Enhancements

## Functions and keywords cheat sheet improvements

Our in-app cheat sheet is your guide to our full list of keywords and functions. You don’t have to remember the syntax for every query; just refer to the cheat sheet to find what you need or discover more ways to add precision to your search in Jira. We have updated it with the following:

- A copy function for every example that lets you copy and paste the query and update the parameters to suit your needs.
- Clearer layout of categories and descriptions
- A new category for our most popular keywords and functions
- Highlighting of matching terms when using the search functionality
