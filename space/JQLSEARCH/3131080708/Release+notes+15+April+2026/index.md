## Streamlined trial management and minor improvements

To align with Atlassian's trial policies, we updated how trial licenses are managed. You can now use an Appfire trial license key on the new License Management page under **Manage apps** to extend your trial after a standard Atlassian trial ends. See the [Appfire trial licensing](https://apps.appf.re/license-management) documentation to learn about requesting a key.
