# Scripted Custom Fields

Scripted Custom Fields allows you to display dynamic, calculated data where you need it.

Whether you’re combining values from multiple fields or presenting calculated insights, Scripted Custom Fields bring a powerful new dimension to your data visualization.

Enjoy increased accessibility and discoverability as you put the scripted fields anywhere on your screens, dashboards, or agile cards, increasing collaboration and efficiency within your team.

Important Information about Scripted Custom Fields:

- Scripted Custom Fields are calculated in real-time, ensuring your data is always up-to-date.
- Results are refreshed anytime a change is made to the issue, or anytime a change is detected from a related issue.
