# Installing or Re-installing on Jira Cloud

## Install the app

To install the app, follow these steps:

1. Log in to your Jira Cloud instance as an administrator.
2. Click the **Settings** icon ⚙️ in the upper right corner and select **Apps**.
3. Search for ‘JMCF’ and select **Jira Misc Custom Fields (JMCF)**.
4. Click **Try it free**.
5. Click **Accept & Install** to confirm the app installation.
6. The system displays a progress message for the operation.

Click **Get Started** to go to the Welcome page for the app.

### Important

After successfully installing the app, we recommend that you [verify your Jira instance configuration](#) to make sure all JMCF features will be able to work properly.

## Re-install the app

To uninstall and re-install the app, follow these steps (don’t skip *any*):

1. Log in to your Jira Cloud instance as an administrator.
2. Click the **Settings** icon ⚙️ in the upper right corner and select **Apps**.
3. Click on **Manage apps** and locate **Jira Misc Custom Fields**.
4. Click on the app to display its details view.
5. Click **Unsubscribe**or**Stop Trial**(depending on your subscription status).
6. Click **Uninstall.**
7. The system displays a progress message for the operation. Once finished, the app is removed from the **Manage apps** page.
8. Click on **Find new apps** and search for ‘JMCF’.
9. Click on the app to display its details view.
10. Click on **Subscribe.**
11. Click on **Accept & Install** to confirm the app installation.
12. The system displays a progress message for the operation. Once finished, the app is reinstalled.

Click on **Get Started** to go to the Welcome page for the app.

- Reinstalling the app will not modify your custom fields. However, while the app is inactive, your custom fields will not recalculate, so you want to minimize the time between un-installation and re-installation of the app.
- Unsubscribing and then re-subscribing to the app will have no impact on billing, as long as you unsubscribe and then re-subscribe on the same day.
