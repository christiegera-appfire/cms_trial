# Uninstalling or Disabling on Jira Cloud

To uninstall the app, follow these steps:

1. Log in to your Jira Cloud instance as an administrator.
2. Click **Settings** ⚙️ in the upper right corner of the screen and select **Apps**.
3. Select **Manage apps** and locate **Jira Misc Custom Fields**.
4. Click the app to display its details.
5. Click **Unsubscribe** or **Stop Trial** (depending on your subscription status).
6. Click **Uninstall.**
7. The system displays a progress message for the operation. Once finished, the app is removed from Jira and will not display under **Manage apps**.

You have uninstalled the *Jira Misc Custom Fields*app.

## Disabling the app

To disable the app, follow these steps:

1. Log in to your Jira Cloud instance as an administrator.
2. Click **Settings** ⚙️ in the upper right corner of the screen and select **Apps**.
3. Select **Manage apps** and locate **Jira Misc Custom Fields**.
4. Click on the app to display its details view.
5. Click on **Disable**.
6. The system displays a progress message for the operation. Once finished, the **Disable**button is replaced with **Enable**.

You have disabled the *Jira Misc Custom Fields*app.

You are viewing the documentation for **Jira Cloud**.
