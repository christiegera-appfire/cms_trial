### A powerful collection of Calculated Fields to build better Jira screens, filters, and dashboards.

- **Create more useful Jira screens**  
  Empower users with insights “hidden” in your Jira issues. For example, display the dates and authors of all transitions, status, and field changes.
- **Calculate Jira values on-the-fly**  
  JMCF automatically calculates data within Jira rather than calculating data manually and risking errors.
- **Improve decision-making processes**  
  Enhance your Jira reports and dashboards with accurate, actionable data. For example, show a dashboard with specific weekly resolutions, per user.
