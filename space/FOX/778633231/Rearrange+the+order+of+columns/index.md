# Rearrange the order of columns

You can reorder metrics by dragging and dropping them in **Customize settings**. After you save the changes, you'll customize the settings. You’ll be able to see metrics in the order you defined in the Foxly table and the Foxly issue panel.

## Rearrange the order of columns

1. Click the *Priorities* tab > **Customize**.

   ![Screenshot of Priority matrix showing the Customize button.](/cms_trial/assets/62867b1f-ede2-4d6b-99bb-6567c1a3a7e4.png)
2. Drag and drop the metric you want to reorder and click **Save changes**.

   ![Gif of reordering your metrics in the Edit template window.](/cms_trial/assets/bc77e746-a587-4df3-9475-64c888326a3b.gif)
