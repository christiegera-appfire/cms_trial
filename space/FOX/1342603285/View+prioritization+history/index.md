# View prioritization history

Foxly allows users to review the history of prioritization directly from the issue details. This helps teams track and manage their prioritization decisions more effectively.

## View prioritization history

1. Click the **Issue options**▢button.
2. Select **history.**
3. View prioritization history.

![Screenshot showing Jira ticket prioritization history.](/cms_trial/assets/9a2d17dc-827a-4d84-a263-4af4a6b87f98.png)

## View from ticket

1. Click the ticket you would like to view.
2. Click the **History**[history icon icon]icon.
3. View prioritization history.
