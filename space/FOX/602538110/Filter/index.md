# Filter

Foxly offers multiple filter views:

1. **Basic Filters** allow you to quickly select filters from preselected options like Projects, Assignee, Fixed versions, and more.
2. **Advanced Filters** allow you to use JQL search to filter by anything you want! Plus, you also get hints while typing your search to make it much easier.
3. **Saved Filters** allow you to select any of your saved filters in Jira, which is ideal for quickly switching between the views.

## Basic Filters

1. To use the basic filters in Foxly, select **Basic Filters** from the drop-down on the left.
2. Click **More Filters** on the right and select a field to filter on.

## Advanced Filters (JQL)

1. In Jira, under *Apps*, click **Priorities.**
2. Click **Basic Filters**.
3. From the drop-down, click **Advanced Filters.**
4. Enter your desired **JQL query** in the search field. Hints will appear as you're entering your query.

![Foxly filter bar showing filter type dropdown options](/cms_trial/assets/f00b731f-9189-4cc7-a7ea-37e22594695d.png)

## Saved Filters

1. From the Filters drop-down, select **Saved Filters**.
2. Click **Saved Filters** to see a list of the filters that have been saved in this Jira project.
