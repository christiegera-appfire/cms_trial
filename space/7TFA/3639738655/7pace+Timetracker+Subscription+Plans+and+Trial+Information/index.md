# 7pace Timetracker Subscription Plans and Trial Information

This article explains how licensing works in 7pace Timetracker for Azure DevOps, including the Free plan, trial availability, subscription tiers, and what happens when a trial or subscription expires.

## Licensing model

7pace Timetracker uses a **named-user** licensing model. Each person who uses 7pace Timetracker needs an assigned role and, when required, an active license slot.

- User roles are managed in **Settings** > **Permissions**.
- Setting a user’s role to **None** removes access and frees that license slot.

## Subscription tiers and benefits

| **Plan** | **Team size** | **Price** | **Key limits** | **Best fit / benefits** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | Up to 3 users | Free | 500 worklogs per organization | Good for small teams evaluating light usage with no paid subscription. |
| Start | Up to 19 users | $8 per user per month on annual pricing | 1,000 worklogs per user | Designed for smaller teams that need core time tracking without the full reporting history and heavier API needs. |
| Team | Up to 299 users | $13 per user per month on annual pricing | One organization | Best for teams that need fuller work history, stronger reporting, API access, and broader day-to-day operational use. |
| Ultimate | From 20 users | $17 per user per month on annual pricing | Unlimited organizations | Best for large enterprises that need maximum flexibility across multiple Azure DevOps organizations and local installations. |

## Trial availability

A **free 28-day trial** is available for the entire team.

- Project Collection Administrators can view subscription or trial status in **Settings** > **Subscription**.
- Plans, users, billing, payment, and invoices are managed directly within the 7pace Timetracker app.
- When your trial is nearing completion, administrators will see the *7pace Timetracker Free Trial* countdown.

## Billing terms

- **Annual and monthly plans** are available.
- **Prorating** allows customers to change the number of users, billing periods, and plans at any time.

## Switching to the Free plan

Reducing the number of active users to 3 or fewer in **Settings** > **Permissions** does **not** automatically convert a paid subscription or expired setup to the **Free** plan.

To move to the Free plan, an administrator must:

1. Reduce active users to 3 or fewer in **Settings** > **Permissions**.
2. Go to **Settings** > **Subscription** and explicitly select **Free**.

## When a trial or subscription expires

- Access to 7pace Timetracker is blocked when the subscription or trial expires.
- Users cannot continue using the application until a valid subscription is restored or the organization qualifies for and switches to the **Free** plan.
- For cloud environments, historical data is retained for up to **1 year** after expiration.

## How to restore access

1. Open **Settings** > **Subscription**.
2. Purchase or renew the subscription if continued paid access is needed.
3. If the organization qualifies for the Free plan, reduce active users and explicitly select the Free plan.
