Release notes 5.51.2

Release date: March 7, 2024

Our team is thrilled to announce the latest release of 7pace Timetracker for Azure DevOps.

Hotfixes

  • Reporting: Project dashboard filter was removed when CurrentProject prefilter was selected to solve possible filter conflicts; Added logic to handle the intersection of widget and dashboard filters correctly.

  • Approval: Fixed incorrect scroll behavior on the Approval page.

Questions and feedback

  • Explore exciting features, pricing updates, reviews, and more on the Marketplace.

  • Stuck with something? Raise a ticket with our support team.

  • Do you love using our app? Let us know what you think here.

Credits

A heartfelt thank you to our valued customers! Your incredible support inspires us to improve our apps continually. We appreciate your trust in 7pace Timetracker for Azure DevOps!
