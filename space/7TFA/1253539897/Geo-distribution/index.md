# Geo-distribution

Choose an Azure DevOps data center to house your 7pace Timetracker data: Western Europe, Central US, South India or Australia East.

### **Introduction**

Previously, all 7pace Timetracker customers automatically had their data housed in a Western Europe location. Beginning in 2020, 7Pace Timetracker began using a geo-distribution system, which also stores and processes data within Microsoft Azure data centers in the following geographical locations:

- Western Europe
- Central US
- South India
- Australia East

Keeping your data in an Azure DevOps data center closer to your geographic location will improve the performance of 7pace Timetracker.

During the initial installation and setup of 7pace Timetracker, you are prompted to select where your organization’s data is located.

![Choose_Location.png](/cms_trial/assets/cccb75f3-4f91-48bb-9a68-8269a9faa541.png)

The default data location is based on the closest geographical location available when you install Timetracker on your DevOps server/services organization.

![Overview_Window.png](/cms_trial/assets/751a4bd5-0482-47a6-bbee-c5bde5814564.png)

You can choose your organization's region based on locality and network latency or because you have sovereignty requirements for data centers. We recommend choosing a location that is as close as possible to your Azure DevOps organization’s location ([learn more and find your organization’s location](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/devops/organizations/accounts/change-organization-location?view=azure-devops)).

To change your organization’s datacenter to one closer to you, click [here](#How%20to%20change%20your%20data%20location).

### How to find your Timetracker data location

You can find your organization’s data location here: **7pace Timetracker** menu bar > **Settings** > **Overview** > **Data Location**.

![Timetracer_Data_Location.png](/cms_trial/assets/ebb7323c-bedc-45a0-86e9-509c21afaa95.png)

### How to change your organization’s data location

To switch your data location, please reach out to 7pace Timetracker support on our [Customer Support Portal](https://appfire.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/35).
