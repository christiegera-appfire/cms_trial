Planning Poker for ADO becomes paid

In 2022, we launched the free Planning Poker app to bring a user-friendly, convenient, and flexible native ecosystem experience to Azure DevOps Services users.

Since then, the team behind the app has been busy improving the app and implementing your requests — thank you very much for all the provided feedback!

After getting over 5.5k installs, thousands of users, and monthly active instances, we need your help with supporting the infrastructure to develop Planning Poker further. That’s why we're converting Planning Poker to a paid app.

Action plan​

info

On August 3, 2026 Planning Poker version 2.1 was released.

Trial​

After August 3, 2026 To use the app, you will need to either purchase access or start a free 30-day trial. The trial will be available to both existing and new customers.

The trial has to be started manually by an organization admin (member of the Project Collection Administrators group).

  • Organization admins will see the following banner in the app:

  • Other users will see the following message:

  • Starting the trial creates an organization-wide subscription on annual billing in trial status. No purchase required to begin the trial.

  • Every ADO instance user will be able to use the trial.

  • The organization admin who started the trial will receive email updates 7 days before the trial ends and when it has ended.

Once the trial is activated, the information about the days left is displayed in the app:

The Buy subscription button is available for admins only.

Purchase​

Once a Project Collection Administrator starts the trial, it is possible to buy a subscription from Organization settings → Planning Poker → Subscription:

  • Two plans (Team and Organization) and two billing options (monthly and annual) are available.
  • The free trial is always created as an Organization plan with annual billing.
  • After selecting a plan and billing cycle and completing checkout (providing billing details), the trial continues. The first charge is applied 30 days after the trial start date.

Annual billing costs 10× the monthly rate (equivalent to two months free per year).

After the purchase, manage your subscription (seats, tier, renewal, cancellation) using Manage subscription on the same tab (Organization settings → Planning Poker → Subscription).

Only one subscription exists per Azure DevOps organization.

Team plan​

  • Pay for named seats (max 100).
  • Only assigned users can use the app.
  • Assign seats in User Management after purchase.
  • Need more than 100 users → switch to the Organization plan.

Organization plan​

  • Covers the whole organization - no seat assignment.
  • Access: all users.

Cancellation​

You can cancel your subscription at any time from Manage subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You keep Planning Poker access until then.

If you have any questions, please let us know by creating a ticket on our support portal.
